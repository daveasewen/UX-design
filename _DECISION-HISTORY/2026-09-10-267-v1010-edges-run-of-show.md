to `HELPER_HOMES`: that table is a typed seed and would go stale at the next lazily-imported

★ This is [[no-gate-parses-the-artefact]] in the release machinery: every release gate parsed the

## 3. The dig was a sphere, and the screenshot was right

Dave: ***"you can spin it but its just on a flat plane, it wast before."*** He was describing
v1.8's **camera-plane ring** — which #266 had introduced deliberately, to stop neighbours hiding
behind the focus when they lined up with the camera. So the complaint and the fix were the same
object seen from two sides: the ring solved occlusion by destroying the three-dimensionality that
made the picture worth showing.

Lane **K** restored v1.6's world-space sphere and paid the occlusion cost a different way — a
per-frame **screen-space radial nudge capped at 12px**, applied only to halo dots whose projected
centre lands inside the focus disc, with world positions untouched. Then it **re-implemented the
#266 sweep**, because #266's script was never committed, and ran it against **both** builds so the
comparison is like-for-like: the sphere costs **4 wrong-colour centres in 1,512 samples (0.26%)**
against v1.8's zero. Reported, not hidden.

★ The method is the lesson: when the baseline's instrument is missing, rebuild the instrument and
re-measure the baseline. Quoting #266's numbers against a new build would have compared two
different rulers.

## 5. canon.css was never a divergence — it was a stale generator output

C6's first finding read as a drift between `canon.css` and three snippets. Lane **N** found the
opposite: **`canon.css` is GENERATED** (`canon/gen_canon_components.py`, from the snippets), and
nobody re-ran it after #261 revised those snippets. `canon.css` mtime 2026-09-08; the snippets
moved 2026-09-09. One `python3 knowledge/canon/gen_canon_components.py` closed the whole 20-snippet
list — `.nv-item` went 0 → 59 occurrences.

★ The general form, and it is the reason this dossier exists: **a divergence between a generated
file and its source is not a divergence, it is an un-run generator** — and the diagnosis that
treats it as drift will hand-patch the output and make the next regeneration a regression.

nowhere) was removed. And `_validate_compose.py` read `var(--x, fallback)` as an unresolved

generalising `s258-D3`'s name-suffix allowance to the grammar.

⛔ **None of this is in the v1.0.10 zip** — it landed after the bake. It is a **v1.0.11 fast
follower** and that is carried, not closed.

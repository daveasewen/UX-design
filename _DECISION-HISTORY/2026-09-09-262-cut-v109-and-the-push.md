## 4. A finding in the release machinery: `--seed` is silently order-dependent

Lane W ran `_gate_frozen_release.py --seed` immediately after `--release`, before committing the
zip, and got:

    apollo-spider        version v1.0.9      8 file(s)  c115142abcda

— **v1.0.8's digest and v1.0.8's file count**, while a ninth zip sat untracked on disk. No error.
The row said the frozen surface had not moved, at the exact moment it had. Re-run after the
release commit it reads `9 file(s) babd4fe1b727`.

⚠ **#260-R3's order was already right** — commit the release, *then* seed — and this seat departed
from it by reading the report's sequence rather than its reason. ★ **The defect is not the order;
it is that the order is nowhere stated.** `--seed`'s own printed warning says *"if a frozen surface
MOVED, bump that row's version in this same commit"* and says nothing about **when** to run it, so
an early seed is indistinguishable in its own output from a surface that genuinely did not move —
[[roll-pointer-is-not-an-absence]] inside the release line. Caught here by reading the printed
table against the disk, which is the only reason it is a finding and not a wrong ledger row.

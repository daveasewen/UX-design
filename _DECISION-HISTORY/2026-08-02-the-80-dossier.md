### 1. The refusal — where the risk actually lived, and it was not where the ruling pointed

Dave ruled at #79's wrap: ***"make it refuse."*** The obvious reading is a one-line change in
`_gauge_tokens.py::count()` — replace the silent `len(text)//4` fallback with a raise. That reading
is a trap, and #79's own conductor had already found the trapdoor: `count()` is reached from
`assert_budget_clears_floor() → measure_boot() → count()`, called at `_capture_gate.py:2774` inside
a list comprehension **with no try/except**. A bare raise there does not make the gate honest — it
KILLS a 39+-check sweep, which then reports nothing at all. Strictly worse than the estimate it
replaced. [[a-crash-is-not-a-fail]]

⇒ **the deliverable is the PAIR.** `MeasurementRefused(RuntimeError)` in `_gauge_tokens.py`, and a
handler at `_capture_gate.py:2782` that turns the refusal into ONE named failure. ⛔ **Recorded as a
FAILURE, never waved through:** an UNMEASURABLE floor is not a cleared floor, and defaulting UNKNOWN
to "fine" is the exact defect the gauge programme exists to prevent.

★ **The sharp decision, and it went AGAINST this repo's own precedent.** `_context_gauge.py` and
`_checkin.py` both refuse via `SystemExit`. Following that precedent here would have compiled, read
as consistent, and silently defeated the whole pairing: `SystemExit` is a `BaseException`, so
`_arm()`'s `except Exception` would never have caught it and the selftest would have died. The
precedent is not wrong — it is scoped to CLI ENTRY POINTS, and `count()` is a library function. **A
precedent is a claim about a call site, not about a repo.** [[premise-ages-faster-than-rule]]

**And the guard for that is structural, not behavioural.** `selftest_gauge_refusal_seam()`'s first
arm asserts `issubclass(MeasurementRefused, Exception)` — because re-parenting the class to
`BaseException` leaves every paired handler compiling while it silently stops catching. That is
[[gate-the-presence-not-the-drift]] applied to an exception hierarchy. Mutation-tested ×3, each
mutation biting with a DISTINCT named failure, all three reverting green.

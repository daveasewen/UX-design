## 2. The finding: there is no legal form for an honest mention

Both repairs took the same shape — **delete the backticks** — because that is the *only* form the
detector accepts today. Which means:

> A document may not name a file it plans to build, in the notation the corpus uses for filenames,
> without being flagged as a rotten pointer.

That is [[honest-refusal-needs-a-legal-form]] in a new artefact. The remedy is a **declared-planned
vocabulary** (a way to say "this path is intended, not present" that the detector reads as legal),
and it is **QUEUED, NOT BUILT** — `s172-D3`(e): never build the instrument in the same breath as
the finding. Writing it down without building it is the whole point of the clause.

it was inferred from the eight cards that were ruled. An absent instruction is not a silent yes.

## 1. The compaction brief — a verdict PUT, not RULED

The lane produced `_BRIEF-compaction-strategy-2026-08-15-v1.html`: a Swiss-design research-and-brief
page built to the standing type/colour discipline (two-red law `#DA1A00` on white; ink `#1A1A1A`;
markup parse-verified; accent and ink checked at build time).

**The verdict it puts, in one line: compaction is a parachute UNDER the wrap, never a substitute for
it.** That framing matters because the obvious reading of an auto-compaction feature is the opposite
one — that it removes the need to stop and capture. It does not. A compaction event preserves a
*summary*, and this project's entire record discipline exists because a summary is exactly the artefact
that inscribes confident false facts [[memento-framing]]. The wrap writes the tattoos; compaction only
keeps the conversation breathing long enough to reach a wrap. If compaction ever ran *instead* of a
wrap, the session would be certified by a machine-written précis that no gate can check.

⛔ **It is a PUT, not a ruling.** Nothing in `knowledge/_rulings.json` says it, and this dossier does
not promote it. Section 06 of the brief carries **five questions that are Dave's and are UNANSWERED**:

card 4. This is a deferral with a return condition rather than a return date, which is the same shape
as `s179-D1`'s return-with-numbers.

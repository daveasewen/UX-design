## Finding 1 — the rise is exact, and the render proved the engine honours it
The recipe pins the letter ~4px above its bar top by translating it `--kh`(bar height)→0 on the **same**
duration/delay/easing as the bar's `scaleY` grow. WHY it holds: at any instant both share the eased progress `e`,
so `bar_top = 230 − kh·e` and `key_y = 226 − kh·e` → the gap is a constant **4px, independent of the easing** (it
cancels). Verified numerically (max deviation 1e-14) AND by render at frozen `currentTime` 400/700ms — every
letter rode its rising bar top. The only residual was engine-level (does Chromium treat `transform-box:fill-box` +
`translateY(px)` as SVG user units): the render settled it — yes (viewBox maps 1:1 to px here).

## Finding 2 — "wire a gate" is real because the current gate has a BLIND SPOT
The toolbar `.dv-vt`/`.dv-tbl-toggle` were 32px with no 44 target — yet the build was green. WHY it passed
silently: `_validate_a11y.py` (a) only checks a **hardcoded control allowlist** (`button`, `a.<class>`, `.x`…) that
doesn't include `.dv-vt`; (b) reads only **literal `width/height:Npx`**, so `height:var(--control-h)` is invisible;
(c) treats 44 as **advisory**, blocking only `<24`. So the "foundational" ask isn't just applying the existing
rule — it needs the gate rebuilt markup-driven with 44 blocking-for-controls. That's the proposal, pending sign-off.

The brief said the toolbar gap was in "all 5" chart snippets. Grep said **4** (`bar/combo/donut/line`); sparkline

## Finding 4 — the swatch ruling REVERSED mine (component-invariance)
I applied the WCAG 2.5.8 dense-case exception to shrink the horizontal bar-legend swatch to 24px (to stop the 44
targets abutting). Dave reversed it: the swatch is **one component — its hit area is invariant across layout**, so
both bar + donut swatches are 44; don't invoke the dense-case exception to shrink a control that presents 44
elsewhere. Enacted by spacing the bar rows (`gap:28px`) so the 44 targets tile (swatch grows left, 5px clearance).
Recorded as a principle in the proposal. (Also this session: responsive centring of the bottom legends + donut.)

## Finding 5 — beating sandbox instance-flapping for render-verify
Fresh sandbox, so the render-verify runbook's browser setup was needed — but bash calls **load-balanced across two
sandbox instances** (`gifted-…` ↔ `quirky-…`) whose `~/.cache` and pip installs aren't shared, so a browser
downloaded on one call was gone on the next, and a cold download doesn't fit one 45s call. Fix: stage Playwright +
the headless-shell + system libs + the HSBC fonts on the **shared mount** (`PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH`,
`LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, `FONTCONFIG_FILE` all → mount) so any instance can render. This is a durable improvement OWED
to `_RUNBOOK-render-verify.md`. (Lesson: don't truncate the staging until fully done — I zeroed it, then had to re-stage.)

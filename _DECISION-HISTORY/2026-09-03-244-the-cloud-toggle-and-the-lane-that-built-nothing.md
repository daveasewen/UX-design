*read it before anything loads.*

put ≈89% of it outside the repo. The cloud toggle was the last repo-adjacent lever anyone had a story for. It is
now measured, and it is not a lever. ⛔ **The honest consequence is that the boot's remaining levers are all Dave's
panel or none at all** — which is the same wall `s242-D2` hit, arrived at from a second direction.

⚠ The temptation at a null result is to look for a second variable to explain it away. This session did not: one
variable was changed, one reading was taken, and the reading is written once, in the stratum, per `s241-D2` S5.

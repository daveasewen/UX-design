01

Colour contrast on the dark theme

Needs fixing

The rail "current balance" value renders red text on a tinted panel at 3.23:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 floor for normal text. Red is structurally unfit as a small-text colour on any dark tint; keep it to solid fills with reverse text. Fix: bind the value to --paper (white) and keep red to the accent/approval role only.

The balance figure is set in red on a dark panel, and the two colours are too close in brightness to read comfortably — anyone with weaker eyesight will struggle with it. Red also doesn't hold up as a colour for small text on a dark background.

What to do: set this figure in white so it reads cleanly, and keep red for buttons and highlights rather than body text.

02

A missing "in-between" step

Have a look

The flow jumps from list to confirmation with no interstitial empty state or loading feedback, so on a slow connection the screen looks frozen. Consider a skeleton or a status message bound to the request state.

Moving from the list to the confirmation screen, there's a moment where nothing on screen changes while the app fetches data. On a slow connection that pause can look like the app has frozen.

What to do: show a brief "loading…" indicator during that step so people can see it's still working and hasn't crashed.

4 · What this changes, stated plainly

  • Two values per hue, from now on. rag/*-glyph (marks, badges, dots — your #122 values, unchanged) and new rag/*-graphic (chart series and any standalone mark where colour is the only carrier).
  • Who consumes which: cb3’s --status-* and canon’s .cn-chart-bar spine now read --rag-*-graphic. Everything else still reads the glyph/bare roles and is visually unchanged.
  • R-D3’s known consequence returns, for four hues instead of one: “side by side in one view they read as two ambers, not one.” A status chip and a chart series on the same dashboard will not match exactly. That was accepted for amber; you are now accepting it for red, green and blue too.
  • Dark mode gets quieter. At the mark values the dark legs sit at 5.55–8.77 against #1A1A1A; the graphic legs sit at 3.66–5.76. Still above 3:1, but less loud. The per-hue control below lets you take the light leg only.
  • Scope held: mono base store only. Legacy and console/supercharge declare this rung ABSENT in their palettes (a declared hole, not a ruling) — they set --status-* themselves and are untouched. s151-D1 / s155-D1 / s149-D1 MONO-ONLY scopes were not widened.

File upload / dropzone - four themes x light/dark (#203, Lane F)

Read this first. The brief sent me to build this component from nothing, on the strength of the itinerary spreadsheet marking it Status = Gap. It is not a gap - it already exists, and has for some time. I verified that by hand before writing a line: the snippet is in the tree, the meta is in the tree, and for File upload and Stepper the meta’s own purpose field names its itinerary row and says it was built. So I did not rebuild anything. Rebuilding would have thrown away real work. What I did instead is the part that genuinely had never been done: put the component in front of you across all four themes, light and dark, and chase down the specific defect the brief named.

Itinerary row 18. Its own meta says "Itinerary row 18 (P1); built Phase-2 wave 2". The real job here was the one the brief named underneath the build instruction: reconcile this component’s improvised progress bar with the gated Progress bar (#174 spotted it). I did that, and measured it.

The finding, in one line: the per-file upload bar is not bound to the progress tokens. Its track is bound to form/background/hover and its fill to text/default, where the gated Progress bar uses progress/incomplete and progress/complete. The fills happen to land on the same colour in all eight legs today - so nothing looks wrong. The tracks do not agree: they differ in every one of the four dark modes.

An ARIA gap as well: the gated Progress bar requires aria-valuetext and leans on it - that is how a bar carries meaning when the fill and track are hard to tell apart. This bar has role="progressbar", aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax, but no aria-valuetext and no visible percentage. A screen-reader user hears a bare number; a sighted user sees only the bar move. Neither is fatal, but it is a different contract from the gated component.

Why the track divergence actually matters — and this is the part I would look at first. A progress track tells you how much is left. Measured against the page it sits on, the gated Progress bar’s track reads 1.9:1 in dark; the File-upload track reads 1.11:1 — it is, in practice, invisible. You can see this in the Mono dark pane above: the white fill is obvious, but where it ends is not. Neither track clears 3:1, and the gated bar is fine with that because it always prints a number beside itself. This bar does not print a number, and has no aria-valuetext either. So on an upload row in dark mode, the bar is the only thing carrying the progress, and it is the weakest of the three carriers. That compounding is the finding — not the token mismatch on its own.

PROPOSED #203, your eye owed. Rebind .fu-bar to progress/incomplete and progress/complete, bind border-radius/indicator, and add aria-valuetext plus a visible percentage. That would give one progress grammar across the whole system. But the counter-argument is real: an upload row is field furniture, and the lighter form/background/hover track may be the right reading inside a form - a progress bar in a field is arguably not the same object as a progress bar on a page. That makes this a design call, not a bug fix. It is yours. Nothing was changed.

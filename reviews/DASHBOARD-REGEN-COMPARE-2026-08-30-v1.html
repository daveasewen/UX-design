The synchronised theme control cannot reach inside the frames. Chrome treats every file:// page as its own origin, so this page is not allowed to set data-theme / data-apollo-theme on the two dashboards. Two ways round it: serve the repo over HTTP (cd <repo> && python3 -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000/reviews/DASHBOARD-REGEN-COMPARE-2026-08-30-v1.html), or use each dashboard's own account-menu theme toggle for light/dark.

By-the-book regen

What to look at. Both files pass knowledge/_validate_screen.py with an identical verdict, so the gate sees no difference at all — your eye is the only instrument here.
  • The tiles. Canon gives a dashboard-role bento tile no background, no border and no padding; the regen therefore has none. Your .dashboard-tile invented that surface.
  • The row height. --layout-bento-row-unit is a fixed 320px. The regen ships that. You overrode it to minmax(148px,auto).
  • The corners, in Console. Look at the chart toolbar: the Monthly / Year-to-date switch is rounded, the "Copy data (CSV)" and "View as table" boxes beside it are square, and so is the payment search field. None of those three bind a radius token in canon.
  • The masthead. Canon's masthead reference ships a text wordmark. The regen has the word "HSBC"; yours has the hexagon.

07 · Findings

  1. The page cannot own the grid's width, and the snippet says it does. canon.css:10538 — :where(.cn-data-grid) .dg{--dg-max:760px} — sets the showroom's dial on .dg itself. Data-grid.reference.html:125 says "the PAGE owns the width (s210-D3); the showroom harness sets --dg-max:760px inside its fence". s258-D3 took the dial out of the copied markup but the canon projection re-imported it, so every page that links canon.css gets a 760px grid. Measured: .dg 760px inside a 1,376px tile, Amount column outside the scroller. A page-level --dg-max:100% on the wrapper had no effect (inheritance loses to a rule on the element).
  2. Rule 2 and _validate_screen.py contradict each other. The gate fails on 2 native radio/checkbox not composed from .cn-selection-controls. Both checkboxes are Data-grid.reference.html's own markup, copied verbatim, which is what rule 2 demands and what the receipt gate then re-hashes and matches. A builder cannot satisfy both without editing a snippet's markup on the way in.
  3. Step 2's new sentence is the difference between a working toolbar and a dead one. filter-toolbar-bar.meta.json → behaviour gives the script address, the four events and the data-open="true" contract; reading it makes carrying wireDD verbatim the obvious move, and all three listboxes open. But the key is absent on 19 of the 23 metas read here (tabs, kpi-tile, stat-card, every chart, drawer, pagination, segmented-control, multi-select, footer, app-shell-side-nav). The instruction only pays where the key exists.
  4. The [hidden] attribute does not hide a bento tile. Switching views by el.hidden = true left the chart group visible, because .c-bento__tile sets display and the attribute has no display:none !important behind it in canon. Driven and repaired at step 6 with a harness class; a page that trusted the attribute would ship a nav that changes nothing.
  5. The fence rule catches the builder's own prose. The first assembly of this page carried the string APOLLO-DEMO inside a comment explaining that no fenced span had been copied — which the receipt gate refuses as FAIL:DEMO-CHROME-COPIED. The marker is a substring test, not a structural one.
  6. The screen gate writes into the repo. One run modified knowledge/_screen-gate/dashboard.md and knowledge/_SCREEN-GATE.md. This lane copied the ledger out and restored both files from HEAD; a consumer running step 5 on a checkout gets two dirty files and no warning.

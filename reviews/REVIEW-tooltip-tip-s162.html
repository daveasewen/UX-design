tooltip.tip — top | bottom (default top)

The one unruled row of the s142-D1 wave. Meta note: “Which edge the pointer sits on (tooltip above vs below the trigger).”

What the reviewed snippet actually does: placement is space-aware — on open the tip flips above/below and shifts to stay on-screen (per Figma node 1887-92040). So tip is a preference/default the runtime may override, not a fixed visual style. Its sibling tipAlignment was ruled no-bind:structural-param (C10).

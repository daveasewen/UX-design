§1 decision — pick the canon manifestation(s)

M1 Cell — strongest scan in dense tables; the fill carries the meaning. Needs the background role + text-colour rules (white, amber-black).

M2 Pill — same fill, softer; reads as a tag. (Square in mono.)

M3 Dot + label — the "belt & braces" form: never colour alone (icon-013). Lightest touch, best for accessibility, works inline in prose.

M4 Bar / edge — for cards and grouped rows where the whole object has a status.

My read (yours to overrule): these are context-scoped, not competing — M1 for table status columns, M3 for inline / dense / accessibility-first, M4 for cards. M2 only if a tag pattern already exists. If you'd rather name one canonical form, say which.

Your call: which form(s) are canon, and is the mapping to surface right?

§2 · Dark mode — a balanced (isoluminant) RAG set

Your steer: match the intensity across the hues in dark mode. The term is isoluminant — holding the same perceptual lightness (OKLCh L) across red / green / blue so none reads brighter or muddier than the others (amber excluded — it's the carve-out and stays #F0B13A / #C58900). Doing this reveals the real structure: you can't get both roles at one lightness. White-text-on-fill wants the hues dark; a mark on the dark page wants them light. So a matched set lands as one of two bands, each internally balanced — measured on the real ground #1A1A1A:

What each band costs. Band A keeps the light-mode logic (white text on fills) and changes the least — but red/blue nudge slightly darker off their R-D1 dark values (#CC4333→#C33A2B, #2674DC→#1F6ED5) to sit at the matched L, and bare coloured RAG text on dark stays disallowed (marks are icons or label-paired — the "belt & braces", icon-013). Band B is the more unified end: every fill takes black text, so amber stops being a carve-out in dark mode and marks work as bare text — but the fills read lighter/pastel and it moves red/blue further from their ruled values. Both bands are equally balanced; the choice is which role you privilege.
§2 decision — the balanced dark set

Band A (recommended): matched white-text fills; marks icon / label-paired. Closest to light mode and to the existing dark red/blue intent; smallest change. Introduces the dark green that was missing and gently re-balances red/blue.

Band B: matched black-text fills; marks self-sufficient as text. Maximally unified — one text colour for all dark RAG fills, amber no longer special — at the cost of a larger visual shift.

Keep red/blue exactly as ruled (don't isoluminate): accept a small intensity mismatch, cut only the dark green to fit. Least churn to canon; gives up the balance you asked for.

Recommendation: Band A. It answers your balance request, needs one genuinely new value plus a gentle red/blue re-cut, and pairs with the mark rule to make dark RAG one coherent system: fills carry white text; marks are icons or label-paired, never bare coloured text.

Your call: Band A, Band B, or hold red/blue and cut green only? (I can also re-run the match at a different L if you want to eyeball a specific intensity.)

In-context component controls — the μX concept

Selecting a component surfaces its own controls in place (no sidebar). Two grab bars: one moves the component within the layout stack (DOM-flow, not absolute — snaps between slots; ↑ ↓ reorder it); one moves the controls. The controls now float beside the component and track it, so they never sit on top of it.

Try it: drag the component grab bar or press ↑ / ↓ to reorder it among the blocks · drag the controls grab bar to reposition the panel (Re-dock snaps it back beside the component) · Size / Style / Max time / Theme update live.

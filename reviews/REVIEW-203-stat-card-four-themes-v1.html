Stat / metric card

The gated Stat-card component, rendered through the generated canon.css in all four themes — light and dark. Two sizes (figure-4 display at 32px, figure-3 compact at 24px) and three delta registers: up, down, and no change. Real snippet markup throughout.

Colour is never the only carrier here. Every delta declares data-carries="symbol label" — an arrow glyph and the word “up” or “down” sit beside the figure. That is deliberate for your eye: red and green are the two hues you read least reliably, so direction has to survive the colour being wrong. Squint at Legacy and Supercharge — the rise and the fall should still land.

PROPOSED #203, your eye owed — the spark slot. The Wave-3 brief named a sparkline slot as a candidate for this card. It is not built, and I have not built it. The gated Chart-sparkline already exists as its own component, so the real question is a composition one: does the stat card grow a slot, or does a board simply place a sparkline under a card? Yours to settle.

Declared absence, not a silent one. This component’s rag-on-neutral contrast pairs were moved out of the embedded manifest and onto the rendering gate (_validate_state_contrast.py) under s151-D2, so the non-colour carrier is checked against real DOM rather than asserted. That is why the manifest below looks shorter than you might expect.

Measured defect, your call — the delta arrows are bound to the FILL reds and greens, not the ink ones. --up binds rag/success and --down binds rag/error. Those are the fill family — banner and roundel backgrounds. The store’s own note says so in as many words: “rag.error is the FILL family … a different seat.” Your two-red law (s151-D1) says the dark ink red is what goes “on WHITE — text AND atoms alike”, and s155-D1 mirrors that in green. These arrows are atoms, and in the four light panes they are sitting on white.

Measured first-hand today with the repo’s own _contrast_utils:

  • down arrow as built — #F6604C on white = 3.14:1 (scrapes the 3:1 non-text floor)
  • down arrow on the ruled ink red #DA1A00 = 5.09:1
  • up arrow as built — #66CC8D on white = 1.98:1 — this one fails 3:1 outright
  • up arrow on the ruled green mirror #137F3C = 5.09:1

Both dark legs are already correct (5.26:1 and 8.31:1). It is the light legs only, and it is a two-token rebind — rag/error → rag/error-ink, rag/success → rag/success-ink. I have not touched it. Rebinding a ruled colour seat is yours, not a sub’s, and the arrow-only treatment may have been deliberate. Look at the pale green “+12.4% up” arrow in the four light panes above and you will see the 1.98 with your own eye. The words carry the meaning regardless, which is why nothing is broken — only quieter than you ruled.

EVERYTHING DERIVED ON THIS PAGE IS PROPOSED. Nothing here is minted and nothing is canon. The card padding formula, the three scales, their paddings and the derived-radius floor are all starting values for Dave to tune or reject. No token file was touched.

Colour is not under review here. The specimens use neutral greys deliberately — the judgement asked for is geometric (do the corners look concentric?), so no theme palette has been applied or invented.
The mechanism, in plain words
  • Cards: the card takes its minted radius, and its internal padding is worked out from that radius — take whichever is bigger, the radius or 8px, then round up to the nearest even number. So a 20px radius gives 20px padding; a square (0px) card still gets 8px of breathing room. (formula PROPOSED)
  • Segmented controls: the padding is hand-tuned per size — small 2px, medium 4px, large 6px as starting points — and the thumb’s radius is then simply the container’s radius minus that padding. That is what makes the inner and outer curves sit concentric, and it means a new size needs one number tuned, not two. (scales and paddings PROPOSED)
  • Every padding steps in 2px so that stacked totals always land on the 4px grid. (this part is RULED, s200-D1)
  • Floor: derived radii are floored at 2px so a thumb never goes negative — except where the theme is deliberately square, which is flagged rather than silently rounded. (floor PROPOSED)

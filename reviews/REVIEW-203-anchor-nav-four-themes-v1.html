Anchor / scrollspy

Itinerary row 37. In-page section navigation: links to headings inside the current document, where the link matching the section you are reading marks itself as you scroll. Two placements, one grammar — a sticky horizontal bar and a vertical “on this page” rail. Scroll inside the panes: the marking is real, driven by an IntersectionObserver.

Read this first. This is a new component, built at #203 through the gated-component route. Step 0 proved the row genuinely absent, unlike 18 of the 18 rows wave 3a was briefed on — the probes are quoted in notes/_receipts/2026-08-19-203-wave3b-laneI-navigation-depth.md. Every pane below is the real gated snippet markup, extracted from the artefact by the builder with a fail-loud content assertion, rendered through the generated canon.css. Nothing here is a ruling: every judgement call is PROPOSED #203, Dave's eye owed.

How it differs from its gated neighbours

  • vs Tabs Tabs swaps panels — only one exists at a time and the rest are removed from the document. This hides nothing: every section stays present and scrollable, and the nav is a shortcut, not a switch. Announcing one as the other is the classic accessibility defect this component exists to prevent.
  • vs Navigations That moves you between pages. This moves you within one page — hence aria-current="true", not "page".
  • vs Breadcrumbs Breadcrumbs says where the page sits. This says where the reader sits.

A claimed variant that was never built. navigations.meta.json declares an anchors variant with a brand-red underline indicator and a pattern in-page-anchor-nav — but the gated artefact contains no anchors markup at all. This snippet makes that claimed variant real and keeps the underline it describes rather than inventing a new one. PROPOSED: the Navigations meta should now point here, or stop claiming a variant it does not render. Editing that shared meta was outside this lane's fence.

Measured — the brand-red “you are here” bar is under the 3:1 floor in Supercharge dark. primary/border/default (#DB0011) against the ground it sits beside:

It is deliberately not declared as a gated contrast pair — that would be a green that cannot fail in the one theme where it is false. The component leans on the shape carrier instead: the bar is present or absent, and aria-current says so to assistive tech. PROPOSED: Supercharge dark either needs its own current-location value, or the pattern needs a non-red mark in that theme. Your call.

One measurement worth recording

  • The gate caught a pair that should never have been declared. The first draft declared divider/border/section on the page as a 3:1 pair; the snippet gate failed it at 1.31:1. It is not a defect — a section divider is a decorative separator between blocks of prose, not a boundary you must perceive to operate the component. The declaration was removed and the reasoning recorded, because afterwards an undeclared pair and an unmeasured one look identical.

What is not faithful — declared

      
    
        
  • Icons are placeholders. The HSBC icon paths are lifted verbatim only for the
        close glyph (Tags/Banner/Modal) and the link chevron (Cards). The card-details and RAG glyphs are
        drawn as a plain circle. Icons carry no radius, so nothing under review depends on them.
    • 
        
  • The modal renders inline. The real .dialog sits inside a fixed
        .overlay; here it is placed in flow so both modes are visible at once. The dialog's own
        box, border, shadow, padding and both radius bindings are the snippet's.
    • 
        
  • The container shell is a schematic, not a lifted snippet: a sub-bento wrapper with
        six tiles bound to --border-radius-container, standing in for the s217-D2 model (the theme
        radius sits on the sub-bento container, tiles at 1px spacing). The corner-keyline construction of
        s218-D8 is not modelled.
    • 
        
  • Hover/press physics are present but partial — the Button family's
        --press-travel/--press-darken partial is carried; the reduced-motion and
        container-query reflow blocks are not.
    • 
        
  • Per-component canon projections are included, colour and all, pasted from
        canon.css AUTO-THEMES for these eight components. Any component not on this page is not represented.

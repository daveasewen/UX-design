03Dark mode — the -V2 step-up

Same roles, on dark. Weights step up one (thin→light, light→regular…) so the type holds its colour instead of going spidery.

Your statement is ready

Three payments cleared overnight, and your available balance has updated.

Why + the refined rule: light/thin cuts thin out on dark, so the dark theme steps display + headings up a weight (the source’s “-V2”). Body sizes are exempt — brand disallows light/ultra on body, so body is regular in both modes and needs no step-up. Theme-driven, so the mode picks it, not the designer.

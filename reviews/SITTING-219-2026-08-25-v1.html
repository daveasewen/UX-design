Which corner does the old lg segmented control borrow?

The four minted sizes are xs / s / m / l. The three sizes every page actually uses are the older sm / md / lg, and their type is bound to those names, so they cannot be renamed. They borrow a minted corner instead. The two readings agree everywhere except lg.

  • A — ordinal. lg→m, 10px in console. Three distinct corners, a ladder that keeps growing. This is what is wired today.
  • B — nearest height. lg→s, 8px. lg's 36px is exactly scale s's 36px, so md and lg end up looking alike.

The conductor recommends B, and it is a recommendation, not a ruling: the old lg is 36px, which is scale s to the pixel, so nearest-height matches the curve to the height the control actually has.

  • Where it lives: SEGMENTED-ADOPTION-2026-08-25-v1.html — flip the switch at the top, watch the third control in the legacy row, console theme.
  • Raised in: lane 1, Q1.

A ruling here mints the legacy→minted corner map, and settles a curve-match judgement s199-D3 says is the whole point.

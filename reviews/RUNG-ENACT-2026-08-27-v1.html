Where it stands

Your words, and the primitive nearest to them

“I prefer rgb(48,48,48) · #303030 … is there a primitive that’s close in the neutral ramp?”

Dave, #220, off the caption-dark ladder.

  • There is: color/neutral/5 — #313131. One grey point off your pick.
  • It already exists in the ramp, so nothing was minted and nothing was promoted. It is being consumed, which is the whole point.
  • The bind is dark only, and console only. Light mode is untouched, in every theme, and the other three themes are untouched in both modes.
  • The other two grounds the capsule chord offers — lightest grey and white — are untouched. Mono is still not offered this ground at all.

Why dark only, and not just a token swap. The dark surface ramp is mode-inverted: the tokens that are dark greys in dark mode are pale greys in light. Point the capsule’s caption slot at one of them in both modes and the light card takes a pale ground with white text on it — 1.14:1 and 1.31:1, both far under the ruled 4.5:1 floor. So the bind lives at the mint site, scoped to dark, and light cannot be reached from it.

Not decided here

Four things this page does not answer

  • Is this the rung? If #313131 reads too light or too dark beside the page, the next points on the ramp are neutral/4 (#1A1A1A, which is where it was) and neutral/6 (#484848). Nothing between them exists as a primitive.
  • Does a semantic token get minted for it? Today the page reads the primitive directly, which is the honest shape of a value with no role yet. A named role — a caption-ground token with a light value and a dark value — is a promotion, and promotions are yours.
  • Does the same bind extend to legacy and supercharge? Their capsule captions in dark were not measured and are not touched. Only console has a capsule chord at all.
  • Does the shipped console default move onto this ground? It does not today: the console gallery ships capBg: grey (#1F1F1F in dark), which is your own #219 export. The ground shown here is the chord’s option. Moving the default is a separate yes.

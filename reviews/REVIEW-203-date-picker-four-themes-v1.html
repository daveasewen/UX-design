Date picker

The gated Date picker, rendered through the generated canon.css in all four Apollo themes, light and dark, on one page. Every pane is the real snippet markup — the calendar cells are produced by a faithful port of the snippet’s own build() function, not hand-drawn.

Read this first. This component was not built in #203. It was built on 2026-07-22 (Phase-2 wave 2) and has been gated ever since. The Wave-3 brief carried it as a P1 gap from the 2026-07-14 itinerary; that status column is stale. Nothing in the gated snippet was edited. This page exists because the component has never been put in front of your eye across all four themes at once — only through the showroom's one-at-a-time theme switcher.

PROPOSED #203, Dave's eye owed — nothing below is ruled:

  • The three date/time components each carry 9 type-composite violations (27 total) — raw font: / font-weight: declarations that predate the composite ratchet. They are inside the standing 1,097 debt, not new. Proposal: a follow-up lane rebinds all three onto .t-cm-*. Not fixed here — editing a gated snippet is outside this lane’s fence.
  • form/border/active resolves to pure #000000 in mono light. Ink is ruled blackest-not-pure-black (s175-D1/s176-D1) for ink; whether a border token is in that camp is a token question, surfaced not swapped.
  • Day cells carry no press physics (selection targets, not buttons) — a #147-era decision that was receipt-flagged for review and has never been ruled.
  • The four .dp-nav month/year buttons are button-family membership candidates (ADR-0013); $members registration is still unmade.
  • Measured this session: the calendar panel has a hard floor of about 336px — seven 40px cells plus gaps and padding, none of which compress. In the 360px column at the bottom of this page the panel therefore overflows its container. On a 360px phone the calendar would need either smaller cells or a full-screen presentation. Neither is ruled; the component has no answer for it today.

Static page: “today” is pinned to 22 July 2026 so the today-ring is visible in every pane. In the live snippet the ring follows the real date.

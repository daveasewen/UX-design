Legend redesign — isolate + toggle

Two affordances per row. The swatch is a checkbox (additive: turn any series on or off, independently). The label is an isolate (exclusive, radio-feel: solos that one; click the active one again to show all). Try the interplay: isolate A → tick B’s swatch → isolate C. Same behaviour on the axis chart and the donut — they’re the two families that need it.

Axis chart — grouped bars (3 series)

Donut — 5 segments

What each gesture does — Swatch (checkbox): toggles just that series, additively; the others are untouched. Label (isolate): shows only that series; clicking the currently-isolated label again restores all. Doing a swatch toggle after an isolate clears the “isolated” flag (you’re back in additive mode). Keyboard: Tab reaches every swatch and label; Space/Enter activates; ↑/↓ on a label moves between labels. Every series stays reachable — nothing is ever colour-only (shape swatch + text name + the data table carry it).
DECIDEOne open a11y call to make while you feel it: the isolate is built here as exclusive toggle-buttons (aria-pressed, “Show only X”) rather than a true role="radiogroup". Reason: after an additive swatch toggle you can be showing two series, where a radiogroup has no single “checked” member. Toggle-buttons stay honest in that state. If the radio feel (arrow-to-scrub-solos) matters more than that edge, we switch to a radiogroup and accept the multi-state quirk.

Three questions for your ruling

Q1 · Dark-mode ground — pure white or off-white? Proposed: pure #FFFFFF (mono/15) for maximum emphasis. Cost: it trips the dark-surface flatness gate, so it needs a $darkNote exemption (same mechanism border/action-strong already carries) and can bloom slightly against the dark page. Alternative: #FAFAFA (mono/14) — one notch down, no exemption, calmer. Toggle to dark and judge the bloom.
Q2 · Light-mode hover — darken to black? Default is already near-black #1A1A1A, so hover can only go one way. Proposed: darken to #000000 (mono/1). It's a subtle shift — perceptible but quiet. Alternative: keep the ground and signal hover another way (elevation/opacity), or don't distinguish hover on the primary at all. Hover the live button above to feel it.
Q3 · Pressed — lighten, or invert the label? Proposed: lighten the ground to #313131 (mono/5), label stays white — a "recede" press, since we can't go darker than default. Alternative: the Legacy-primary behaviour (ground to black + invert the label to dark) — but that's a heavier, more theatrical press. Which reads as "Mono"?

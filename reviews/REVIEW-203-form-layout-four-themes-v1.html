Form layout + validation

Itinerary row 13, the “biggest single gap”. It is not a gap — it was built and gated on 2026-07-22 and has been through the s160 rebase since. Every pane is the approved Form-layout.reference.html markup through canon.css.

Each pane is live and independently validated: press Add payee in any pane with empty fields and that pane alone raises its error summary, marks its fields, and moves focus. Try it in Legacy dark and in Supercharge light — the error channel is the part of this component most likely to break across themes.

This component is currently failing the snippet gate at HEAD, and it is not the only one. Its --pri-hover is #626262 light / #B7B7B7 dark, but the store now says #636363 / #B2B2B2. Nine snippets carry the same stale pair — Action-bar, Button, Confirmation, Drawer, Empty-state, Form-layout, Icon-button, Modals, Stepper — 18 failures in all. This is drift left behind when --pri-hover was re-minted at #198/#199; it was already red before this session touched anything, and nothing here fixed it. The visible effect is small (a primary button hover a shade off) but the gate is red, so the tree is red.

PROPOSED #203, your eye owed — the error summary is not an Alert. The form-level error box is .fl-summary with role="alert", built from scratch inside this snippet with its own icon sprite and its own error styling. The Alert component (row 68) solves the same problem with a different shell. They share nothing. Two independent inline-messaging grammars is either a duplication worth collapsing or a deliberate distinction worth writing down; either way it is your call.

PROPOSED — type debt. Contributes 9 of the 1,097 type-composite violations, the largest of the three Lane A components, including font: 500 12px/16px on the tip body — 12px is on the ramp, but the declaration is raw rather than a composite class.

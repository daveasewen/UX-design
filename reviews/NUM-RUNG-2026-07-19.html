THE THREE OPTIONS

optionwhat it meanscost
A · keep 24
new rung		 Add a Component composite at 24px (a numeric/figure rung, 500 weight). Bind .num to it. Widens the type ramp by one step — a ruling, not a sweep (every type.css selector is global). The figure ramp becomes 52 / 40 / 24.
B · snap to 20 Reuse .t-cm-section-label (20 / 500). Weight already matches. Number drops 4px. No new rung. Conflates a numeric display with a label composite; slightly smaller in the roundel.
C · snap to 32 Reuse .t-cm-heading (32 / 400). Number grows 8px. No new rung, but weight shifts 500 → 400 and a 4-char string gets tight in the 84px roundel.
My lean: A (keep 24, add a rung). The countdown number is a figure (display numeral), not a label — it belongs on the figure ramp (52 / 40 / …), and 24 reads as purpose-built in the roundel where 20 feels slightly under and 32 crowds the width and drops to weight 400. But this is a see-it call, and adding a rung is deliberate type-system growth — so it's yours. If you prefer no new rung, B is the safer snap (weight already matches).

In-context component controls — the μX concept

After generation, selecting a component surfaces its own controls in place — a floating overlay docked to the selection, exposing only the parameters that component has. No sidebar. Drag the grab bar (the checkered strip) to move the panel out of your way.

Try it: drag the grab bar to reposition · Size snaps to the 4px grid + type ramp · switch Style · step Max time · flip theme. The component updates live.

Step colour — a page to rule from

Everything below is the real component markup, styled by the real canon.css. Nothing here is enacted, and nothing is recommended. Two questions, then the picker at the bottom.

Something to see before you rule. In Apollo Mono and Apollo Console the completed steps currently paint nothing at all — the dots and the bar fill come out empty. That is not a styling choice; those two themes have no value for the step colour to resolve to, so the paint simply falls away. The left-hand cells below show that honestly. Whichever way you rule, those two themes need a value.
No new greys or inks were invented for this page. Every colour you see is resolved live from a token already in canon.css. The "ink" candidate simply points the step colour at the same ink the progress bar already uses in that theme.

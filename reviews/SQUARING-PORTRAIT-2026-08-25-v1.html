The flattened portrait

PROPOSED — nothing here is ruled. s219-D2 (2) makes edge: square the gallery-role default in all four themes, and says in its own words that the orphan / flattening refinement (the W-99zi third bend, incl. the stocksy-6629948 case) is expressly NOT ruled here. This page exists so that the case can be seen rather than described. It decides nothing and proposes no fix.

The squaring pass buys an exact rectangle by re-spanning the LAST few tiles. On the 251-photograph wall at its 4/3/2/1 ladder it re-spanned 4 tiles in the last 5 (tile 247 1×2→3×1; tile 248 1×1→2×1; tile 249 1×1→2×1; tile 251 1×1→3×1), for a crop cost of 10 on the pass's own ordinal scale, where 0 is “nothing moved”. The wall is then an exact rectangle at every band (4 cols = 96 rows / 3 cols = 128 rows / 2 cols = 191 rows / 1 cols = 251 rows). One of those re-spans lays a portrait on its side, and that is the tile below.

The tile — stocksy-6629948-w1600.jpg, 1067×1600 (portrait, 1:1.50)
BEFORE — the aspect mapping's own answer, 1×2

Canon's span derivation (s217-D2) reads the picture's own aspect: taller than 1:1.15 spans two rows. The tile is portrait, the photograph is portrait, and object-fit: cover crops almost nothing.

AFTER — what the squaring pass shipped, 3×1

The same photograph in a three-wide, one-tall cell. The cover crop now keeps a horizontal band out of the middle of a portrait and discards the top and bottom. Nothing is wrong with the maths — this is the cheapest assignment that closes the last hole at the ruled tail cap. It is a question about whether the wall should be allowed to buy its rectangle with this particular crop.

The tail in context — the last 12 tiles, before and after

What this page is, and is not

  • It rules nothing. s219-D2 (2) ruled the default and expressly left the orphan / flattening refinement open. No option is recommended here, and no third bend of W-99zi is closed.
  • Nothing is re-drawn. The stylesheet, the header, the theme script and the tile markup are copied out of the shipped showroom/_foundations/photography.html. Two edits, declared: the tile's opener is a <div> rather than a <button> (there is no lightbox on this page), and the asset paths are one directory shallower.
  • Every number is the generator's. Spans, the changed list, the crop cost and the rectangle check come from gen_foundations_217.read_photos() and canon's square_wall — not from a reading of the picture.
  • The crop is geometry, not theme. The theme switcher is live so the tile can be seen in all four; the flattening is identical in every one.

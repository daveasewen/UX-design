Blue — the readable point

Blue is the hard hue: short wavelength + astigmatism = the edge blurs whatever we do. The lever that actually helps is text luminance contrast (the luminance channel stays sharp when colour blurs) and lower chroma (less colour edge to smear). The catch: both make blue lighter, which raises its loudness toward green. Readability wins — pick the crispest, and I'll re-seat green above it to keep the ramp.

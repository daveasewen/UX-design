Calls made

8 things I decided rather than asked

Per s251-D15. Strike by number. Nothing here is ruled.

  1. answers words are questions, not categories. The nine new words are phrased as the reader's question (where-am-i, what-can-i-do) to match the five existing intent words, which are the chart's question. Strike the phrasing and I will re-cut them as nouns.
  2. summary answers details-before-commit, not a count. roles.json puts it in status-surface ("n × count as a key/value list"), but the meta's own purpose is "confirming details before or after an action (a payment review, a receipt)". I authored the purpose, per the brief. If the role membership is right, the word should be a counting word and the purpose prose is the thing that is wrong.
  3. kpi-tile answers two words, one of them existing. [one-number, change-over-time] — the tile states a number AND the shape of its change, and the second half is exactly the existing intent word its embedded sparkline carries. Strike if you want one word per non-chart meta.
  4. chart-bar took priority 84, not roles.json's 60. Lane B, authoring concurrently, broke the illustrative five-way tie at 60 into a ladder (chart-line 88 … chart-sparkline 30) and gave 60 to Chart-boxplot. 84 is the free rung directly below chart-line and keeps chart-bar in the top tier the roles page put it in. Neither lane could see the other's file; this was reconciled from my side only.
  5. No not-with promoted from prose. stat-card and chart-bar carry edges.mustNotNeighbour entries about "a chart making the identical claim"; chart-bar's names component:stat-card resolvably. I did not promote it — the brief authorised only the one the roles page names (kpi-tile ↛ status-surface), and the ~50 prose entries are a declared migration backlog. Say the word and the resolvable ones migrate.
  6. not-with on kpi-tile targets a ROLE, not a meta. The validator prints "reciprocal NOT DERIVABLE (target is a ROLE, not a meta)". That is honest, but it means this one exclusion gets no symmetric partner. If exclusions should always be meta-to-meta, this entry needs re-cutting against status-indicator and summary individually.
  7. when is two halves separated by an em dash. A C9-readable predicate, then prose naming the sibling it beats or yields to (clause R2 of this morning's adopted research). This page counts the field list in §12 from the predicate half ALONE; the validator prints the whole string. If the two halves should be two fields, say so and it is a schema addition.
  8. button and layout-utilities got shape values that mean "no data". no-data and arbitrary-blocks. The shape axis was defined for data-carrying components; these two carry none. Strike if the field should simply be absent on them.

Segmented control — the minted radii and the scale grammar, wired

reviews/SEGMENTED-ADOPTION-2026-08-25-v1.html · #219 lane 1 · 2026-08-25 · PROPOSED — nothing here is ruled

What was wrong. The radius tokens for this control were minted at #200–#202 and have sat in canon.css ever since — console at 6/8/10/12 outside and 0/2/4/6 inside. No rule ever read them. The component set no border-radius at all, so segmented controls rendered square in every theme, console included. The bottom row of each panel is that state.

What is new. The track now reads --border-radius-segmented-container-<scale> and the sliding thumb reads --border-radius-segmented-thumb-<scale>. Square themes resolve both to 0 from the base tier and stay square, exactly as s201-D5 says. Nothing is calculated live.

The one thing that needs your eye

The four minted sizes are xs / s / m / l. The three sizes every existing page actually uses are the older sm / md / lg, and their type is bound to those names (T-D15), so they cannot simply be renamed. They now borrow a minted corner instead. Two readings of which corner — and the two agree everywhere except .lg:

Reading A — ordinal. sm→xs, md→s, lg→m. Three distinct corners, a ladder that keeps growing. This is what is wired today.

Reading B — nearest height. sm→xs, md→s, lg→s. lg's 36px matches scale s's 36 exactly, so it borrows the same corner as md and the two look alike.

Flip the switch below and watch the third control in the legacy row. Point at the one that looks right — console is where you will see it.

Declared, so it is not mistaken for settled

• The minted scales carry no mini-ramp type step — T-D15 binds 12/14/16 to sm/md/lg only, and that binding is yours, not this lane's. xs/s/m/l inherit the default control face. A step per minted scale is a ruling-shaped question, not an oversight to patch.

• The label inset on the minted scales (8/12/16/20) is read from the fixed spacing ramp. It was never tuned on the corner tuner — PROPOSED.

• The 44 hit zone is honoured as ruled: max(44, visual height), so xs and s expand to 44, m sits at 44, l keeps its natural 48. Still no gate reads the 44 — unchanged since #200, and saying so here is not a fix.

• The nine other snippets that carry their own copy of .seg still render square. Fixing those is a separate motion and is named in the lane report, not done here.

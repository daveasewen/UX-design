Stepper (interactive) - four themes x light/dark (#203, Lane F)

Read this first. The brief sent me to build this component from nothing, on the strength of the itinerary spreadsheet marking it Status = Gap. It is not a gap - it already exists, and has for some time. I verified that by hand before writing a line: the snippet is in the tree, the meta is in the tree, and for File upload and Stepper the meta’s own purpose field names its itinerary row and says it was built. So I did not rebuild anything. Rebuilding would have thrown away real work. What I did instead is the part that genuinely had never been done: put the component in front of you across all four themes, light and dark, and chase down the specific defect the brief named.

Itinerary row 34. It was built to do exactly what the brief describes: take Progress-tracker’s visual grammar - 28px dots, 4px connector, done = fill plus check, current = inset ring, and the same dots-to-track collapse - and add the interaction Progress-tracker deliberately lacks. On the reconcile question the Stepper is already clean: its track binds progress/incomplete, its fill binds step/complete, it binds the indicator radius, and it carries aria-valuetext. It is the File-upload bar that is the outlier, not this.

This file is failing a gate right now, and it is not from anything I did: _validate_snippets.py is red at HEAD - 18 failures across nine snippets, all the identical defect, and Stepper is one of them. Each declares --pri-hover as #626262 / #B7B7B7 while the token store now says #636363 / #B2B2B2. That is the --pri-hover mint from #198 and #199 landing in the store without these nine snippets following it. It is one value per file, it belongs to no single lane, and it is queued for the conductor rather than quietly patched here. Visually it is a few units on a button hover - you will not see it in these panes.

Worth your eye. The completed-step fill is step/complete, which aliases rag/success - the green roundel chain from #176 - and Legacy overrides it to the primary red. So in the Legacy panes below, completed steps read red, not green. That is a ruled inheritance rather than a mistake, but red-for-done is exactly the kind of thing that should be looked at rather than assumed, and red is a problem hue for you. Compare the Legacy row against the other three and say whether it stands.

PROPOSED #203, your eye owed. The Stepper uses role="progressbar" with aria-valuemin="1" and aria-valuemax="4", counting steps, where the gated Progress bar counts percent, 0 to 100. I think that divergence is correct - it matches Progress-tracker, and "Step 2 of 4" is what a screen reader should say in a flow, not "50 percent". So I am recording it as a deliberate documented divergence rather than an inconsistency to iron out. Say if you disagree.

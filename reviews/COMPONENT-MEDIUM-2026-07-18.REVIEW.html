Small-label Medium — structural, or drift?

Apollo SDS · 2026-07-18 · blocking the TYPE-002 rebind (100 declarations of the 721).

The situation. Of the 721 raw font declarations, 339 map cleanly onto an existing composite and can be rebound without a ruling. 100 do not, and they are all the same shape: Component scope, single-line, weight 500, at 12 / 14 / 24px. The Component composites at those sizes are all 400 — .t-cm-legal 12/400, .t-cm-caption and .t-cm-tooltip 14/400.

So either the corpus is right and the composite ladder is missing a Medium rung, or the corpus drifted and these should snap to Regular. Binding them to the 400 composites would answer the question silently, in the direction nobody chose.
This sheet can come back null — and that is a real outcome. If every family below reads fine at Regular, the answer is "drift: snap all 100 to Regular, add no composites" and the ladder stays at eleven. Medium winning everywhere is equally valid, but it has to be seen here, not inherited from the fact that someone once typed 500.
What this sheet is NOT. It is not the reverse-text question. {#col26-020}(c) — 12/14/16px = Medium, 20px = Light — was settled from the v2 specimen and is conditional on reverse text over an extreme ground. Only family A sits in that condition. The other four are on ordinary grounds where col26-020 has nothing to say. Keeping that separation visible is half the point: a rule quoted outside its condition is how a preference becomes a gate by accident.

3 · To rule

  1. R1 — per family, is Medium structural? Five answers from §2. Each structural answer costs a composite; each drift answer costs nothing and snaps to Regular. [ per family: structural / drift ]
  2. R2 — if family C is structural, is it really a new composite? .t-cm-button is already 16px/500. Controls at 12/14px may be the same role at a smaller size, in which case the fix is extending the button composite down the ramp rather than adding .t-cm-control-sm. Cheaper, and it keeps the ladder honest about roles. [ extend button / new composite ]
  3. R3 — naming, if any composite is added. The Editorial set already uses .em as its emphasis modifier. A Component equivalent could be .t-cm-caption.em rather than a parallel -strong family — one mechanism instead of two. R1 in _TYPE-DECISIONS left Component naming open for exactly this. [ .em modifier / -strong family / other ]
  4. R4 — Q5, carried forward from specimen v2 and still open. Scope of the reverse-text rule: badges only, or every light-on-chroma surface (RAG banners, primary buttons, pressed states, tags)? Family A is the population it would bite. [ library-wide / badges only ]

Independent of the above, both breaking existing rules: an 11px size (below the 12px floor set in specimen v2) and a font-weight 600 (type25-004 — not a licensed weight, no OTF ships, so it renders as browser faux-bold). Confirm both snap to 12px / 500. [ confirm / handle separately ]

6 · What this does not measure

  • Quality is out of scope. GATED means five artefacts exist and the radius ratchet holds it — not that the component meets #203's rule set. Wave 3a's four-theme review surfaces found real defects in components this page calls GATED.
  • PARTIAL rows are not sized. The itinerary's Partial rows carry prose about missing variants; no signal here reads a variant list.
  • Layer 2 is one structural gap, not 28. No shell/template/lock-up artefact class exists in the store, so per-row absence carries no information.
  • This is a snapshot of a LIVE tree. It was generated while five sibling lanes were writing snippets into the same working tree; rows measured PARTIAL with only a snippet present are mid-route, and the generated surfaces (showroom, canon) had not run yet. Re-run after the generator pass — --check failing later is the surface doing its job, not a defect.
  • Nothing here is promotion. Component promotion is on Dave's DO-NOT-RULE list. This page reports what is on disk.

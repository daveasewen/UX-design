Popconfirm

Itinerary row 75, PROPOSED #204 — your eye owed. Verified absent first-hand: no snippet, no meta and no content match under popconfirm, confirm-in-place or inline confirm anywhere in the corpus, and no ruling in _rulings.json.

A Popover answers; a Popconfirm asks. It borrows Popover’s bubble chrome byte-for-byte and Confirmation’s button pair byte-for-byte, and refuses Popover’s close × — because here dismissal is the cancel, and a × beside a Cancel button offers one outcome under two labels. It is not a Modal either: no scrim, no trap, and the row you are about to change stays visible behind it. That is what “confirm in place” means.

Your call — there is no destructive button in this system. Probed: the token store has no button/danger, button/destructive or button/critical seat at all. So the destructive variant does not invent one. It carries its weight with the warning glyph in the error ink (the two-red law’s own values), the verb repeated in the confirm label, and the question itself. Squint at any pane: the meaning should survive the hue completely. Whether a danger seat should exist is yours, and it touches two firm rulings.

Your call — and one thing a green gate is not telling you. The right role for a confirm-in-place is alertdialog. Built that way, the a11y gate went red: its role vocabulary carries “alert” and “dialog” but not their compound. That gate is a shared file this lane may not edit, so the snippet fell back to dialog and the one-line fix is proposed to the build lead. The gate is green because it is measuring dialog, not because it approves of it.

Also unruled: whether a destructive confirm should trap focus (this one does not, inheriting Popover’s non-modal reading); which actions may use the pattern at all — nothing here licenses it for moving money; whether cancel sits left of confirm; and whether “Popconfirm” is the word this system would choose.

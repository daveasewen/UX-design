What each cut could break — replayed, not hand-waved

1 · The M10 selftest bites are a two-sided vice: too fat fails, too thin also fails

The two red bites today (_capture_gate.py --selftest, build step 13) are the negative controls: "an ordinary chain warned — the budget fires on everything" and "a fat §A/§C warned the CHAIN — the re-point did not take". They fail because state_block() ignores its repo argument — it reads the live knowledge/_state.json by absolute path — so every temp-dir fixture, whose whole GM is 214–347 tape, gets the production 125-row worklist stapled to it and lands at 13,232 tape, past the 10,000 block-candidate. But the very next bite requires a fat banner to warn. Driven: with a ~336-tape wrapper the fat-banner fixture lands at 6,545 — under the 7,700 warn — and that bite flips red. Columns C (8,376) and D (13,167) both stay above it.

So the wrapper has a floor as well as a ceiling: roughly 1,670 to 7,486 tape. Below the floor the fat-banner bite dies; above the ceiling the two negative controls that are red today come back. C at 2,326 has ~650 tape of floor margin. D at 7,117 has only ~370 tape of ceiling margin — about eight more of your items, at 44.6 tape per title row, and D re-breaks the very bites it was picked to fix. D is a fix with an expiry date. C is not.

2 · The M10 budget is ADVISORY — no candidate makes the live chain "pass" it

CHAIN_BUDGET_TK = (7700, 10000) and the live chain file is 22,864. Even column C lands at 12,129 — still over the block-candidate, so the "OVER THE BLOCK-CANDIDATE" warn keeps firing on the real repo. It is a warn, by Dave's #18 ruling, and a bite asserts it may never reach FAILS. Do not let a wrapper cut be sold as "M10 goes green" — it green-lights the selftest's fixtures, not the live budget.

2b · The ratio red is not one red — it cascades into the skipped-wrap net

_capture_gate.py --selftest runs _gen_chain.selftest() and fails if it is not green: "#70/#71 non-catch: _gen_chain.py --selftest is NOT green — the regime's only net for a skipped wrap (the stale-title bite, one session late) is broken." So today's four red lines are three M10-family failures in _capture_gate --selftest plus the one ratio bite in _gen_chain --selftest, and every one of them traces to the same wrapper. Only column C clears all four. Column D leaves the ratio bite and the #70/#71 cascade standing.

3 · The negative wrapper — a measurement defect the fat wrapper is currently hiding

Proven here by single-variable isolation. On the fat-banner fixture the slice measures 9,107 real and the whole file — which contains the slice — measures 6,554 tiktoken cl100k_base, so the generator prints wrapper -2,553. Cause: _gauge_tokens.count() returns "real" on any cache hit in knowledge/.token-cache.json, and this environment cannot reach the API at all, so fresh text silently falls to tiktoken. Two instruments, one sentence. Re-run with CAPTURE_GATE_NO_REAL=1 (the CI shape) and it resolves: slice 6,070, file 6,574, wrapper +504. Today's 13,061-tape wrapper masks this by swamping it. A leaner wrapper unmasks it — locally only. The M10 UNIT BITE ("the FILE figure does not exceed the SLICE") is one fixture away from firing on any machine holding that cache.

4 · Cold-boot orientation is the real thing being traded

The state_block() docstring says the block is ADD now, CUT at the swap — duplication accepted deliberately while the hand-authored presence index still reaches the chain through chain_parts. Cutting the bodies is the swap it always anticipated; that is column C's licence, and it is Dave's to grant. What is lost is real: a cold session that reads 125 ids and no titles cannot triage without a second call. Column D keeps the triage and misses the floor; column C clears the floor and loses the triage.

5 · Two more consequences worth naming before anyone cuts

  • Every gate that parses the chain reads the OPEN WORK heading, not the rows. The bites assert the heading is computed and that the block declares a refusal rather than an empty worklist. None of them parse a row. Removing bodies does not blind a bite — but _gen_chain.py's own "OPEN WORK: NOT RENDERED" refusal path must survive the edit.
  • The chain is shipped inside every Gumdrop pack, where _state.json is the designer's, not yours. A pointer block ships better than 125 rows of Apollo's private worklist — an argument for C that has nothing to do with the ratio.

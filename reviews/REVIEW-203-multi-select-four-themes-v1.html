Multi-select

Itinerary row 22, newly built at #203. Choose several values and keep them visible. Three gated grammars are composed without alteration: the Input-fields box, the Tags chip, and the Dropdown menu. Only the multi half is new — the list stays open while you choose, and each choice becomes a removable chip.

Every pane is live. Open a list, pick two or three accounts, remove one with its cross, then put the cursor in the empty field and press Backspace to take the last one back.

PROPOSED #203, your eye owed — two variants, no default. Variant A keeps every choice visible but the field grows as chips wrap; variant B states the count, stays one row tall, and puts the chips underneath. In a dense form the growing field shoves the layout around; in a filter bar the visible chips are the point. Both are on this page, in all four themes. Which is the default?

The chip is the gated Tags chip, copied. Not a second chip, and not the Selection-controls chip — that one is a toggle you press, this one is a receipt of a choice made in the list, and removing it is all it does. If the chip should change, it changes in Tags first and both inherit.

The dismiss crosses are 24px, not 44px. That is the gated Tags value inherited unchanged, and it is the WCAG minimum rather than our own 44px floor. Raising it would change Tags, which this lane may not touch. Flagged for the hit-area sweep.

PROPOSED — selecting does not close the list. That is the point of a multi-select, but it means there is no obvious moment of completion. Escape and clicking away both close. Watch whether it feels unfinished.

Variant A — chips in the field

Every choice is visible and individually removable. Choosing does not close the list, so several accounts can be picked in one visit. Backspace on an empty field removes the last chip. The field grows as chips wrap.

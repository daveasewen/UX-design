Everything on this page is settled except one thing. The four control heights (24 / 36 / 44 / 48), the four track insets (2 / 2 / 4 / 4), the existing thumb radii (4 / 6 / 6 / 8) and the new switch radii (0 / 2 / 4 / 6) are all minted numbers. Nothing here is asking you to re-tune a number.

The one open question is scope: which column’s grammar ships? In the BESIDE column the new squarer radii apply to the two-segment switch only, and the ordinary three-segment control keeps the rounder 4 / 6 / 6 / 8 it already has — two shapes living side by side. In the REPLACE column the squarer radii apply to everything, switch and ordinary control alike — one shape everywhere. Same numbers in both columns; only the reach differs. That reach is yours to rule; it is not decided.

What you are looking at, in plain words
  • Four sizes down the page — xs, s, m, l. Each size gets its own block.
  • Two columns inside each block. Left is BESIDE, right is REPLACE. Compare left to right.
  • Two controls inside each column. The three-segment one (Day / Week / Month) is the ordinary segmented control. The two-segment one (Off / On) is the switch variant.
  • Only the corners differ between the columns. Heights, insets, type and spacing are identical everywhere. If the two columns look the same to you at a given size, that is a real finding, not a mistake — at m and l the difference is small.
  • The outer corner never moves. Every control on this page — both columns, both variants, all four sizes — uses the minted container radius border-radius/segmented-container/{xs,s,m,l} = 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 (s201-D1). Only the thumb corner changes. Per s202-D2 the switch thumb radius is that container radius minus 6, and the container is left alone — so at the switch the inner curve is deliberately not concentric with the outer one. That break is the carve-out, not a slip.
  • The controls are live. Click a segment and the thumb moves.

Two products, one methodology

Same discipline.
Different jobs.

Memento · for any project

A memory system for AI. It lets an AI assistant keep what it learns — on any project, in any domain — so every session builds on the last.

Apollo · for designers

A design generation engine. On-standard, governed-at-source design — the AI builds from your design system, and the standards are enforced before anything ships.

They share a way of working — but each stands on its own. We'll take them one at a time.

Memento · the problem

An AI wakes up every morning
with no memory of yesterday.

Every session is the first day on the job. The context is gone, the decisions are gone, the half-finished thought is gone.

Memento is named for the film. Leonard can't form new memories — but he survives by a system: tattoos for what must never be wrong, Polaroids for the day's working notes, and a hard discipline about whose handwriting to trust.

Apollo · the problem

AI can build an interface
in seconds.

The catch: left to itself, it drifts — off brand, off accessibility, off everything we've agreed matters. Fast and wrong is still wrong.

Apollo governs at the source: the AI builds from our design system, and the standards are checked before anything counts as done.

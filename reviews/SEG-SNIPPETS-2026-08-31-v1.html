The four fixed segmented snippets — and the two that opted out

#230 · W-307’s close condition · specimens spliced from the reference files, styled by the built canon.css

Page chrome
Held hover

What you are being asked to look at. #227 swept the segmented radius defect through the knowledge base by hand and repaired eight files. It missed four. #229 found the four, named them as the highest-value item on its ruling list, and deliberately did not touch them — the fix moves container and thumb pixels you had not seen. Those four are now repaired at source, and the repair arrives through the segmented partial group rather than by hand, so the three radius declarations are injected from the atom and a membership scan fails the build if a snippet grows a .seg rule without joining. Nothing on this page is a description of that. It is the built CSS, rendering.

What "fixed" means, in one line. In Mono, Legacy and Supercharge the minted radius is 0, so these controls look exactly as they always did — that is the ruling (s201-D5), not an oversight. Only Console mints a corner: 8 px on the track, 6 px on the thumb and on the hover/focus box. So the whole question your eye is being asked is in the two Console cells of each spread, and the other six cells are the control that proves nothing leaked.

The hover box is the subtle half. The box that paints on :hover and :focus-visible is geometrically the same box as the sliding thumb, but it only exists in a state — which is why no static render and no gate ever contained it. The Held hover switch above holds one unpressed segment open per control so you can see its corner in a still image. The switch paints a background and nothing else; the corner it reveals is canon’s.

And two components said no. Table and Tab-bar both declare a rule on .seg and both carry a written exemption from the membership scan. They are at the bottom of this page, rendered as they stand today, each with the three-way choice laid out. Those two are the only decisions here. The four repairs above are enacted; your eye is the check on them, not the gate.

One thing to know before you judge the dark cells. The corner is correct in dark — it measures 6 px, same as light. But the fill that carries it is very nearly the same colour as the track it sits on, so in dark mode you may not be able to see the hover state at all, let alone its shape. That is not something #229 or #230 changed; the hover fill has always been tertiary/background/hover. It is measured live below because it affects what your eye can actually report on this page.

If the dark rows read as invisible to you, the light Console cell and the zoomed real-hover images in the report are the honest place to judge the corner — and the visibility of the hover state itself becomes a separate question worth its own look.

Calibration — a question the numbers raised, put in pixels

The repaired controls are 42 px tall and read the s radii. The atom’s s is 36 px.

All three specimens spliced verbatim: the outer two from knowledge/snippets/Segmented-control.reference.html, the middle one from View-options.reference.html.

The minted ramp has four rungs — xs 28, s 36, m 44, l 48 — and Console mints a container/thumb pair for each: 6/4, 8/6, 10/8, 12/8. All four repaired snippets bind the s pair, so they take 8/6. But their button is height:40px inside a 1 px frame, which puts the control at 42 px — six pixels above the s rung and two below m.

Nothing here is broken and nothing is proposed. The binding is consistent, the partial is doing its job, and the number on the box is exactly the number in the store. The question is only whether a 42 px control should wear the 36 px rung’s corner or the 44 px rung’s. Below, in Console, is what each looks like. The middle control is what shipped.

A second difference is visible in the same picture, and it is not a defect either. The atom carries a 2 px track padding, so its thumb floats inside the frame with a visible gap all round. All four repaired files are flush — no padding, the thumb inset only by the 1 px border. The minted pair is a tuned dial (container − 2, s202-D2), not a strict derivation, so a flush thumb lands about a pixel under exact concentricity and an inset one about a pixel over. That is the same tolerance the eight files repaired at #227 already ship, and the snippets say so in their own headers. Adopting the atom’s 2 px inset here would move pixels, and it was flagged not taken. Look at the gap around the black thumb in the outer two against the middle one.

Table — the demo-harness switcher

Specimen spliced verbatim from knowledge/snippets/Table.reference.html; style from canon.css via .cn-table.

The snippet’s own header type variant switcher. The file labels it “demo controls”.

The exemption reason on file, from knowledge/component-types.json → segmented.$scan.$exempt:
DEMO-HARNESS CHROME, and a different construction. Table’s .seg is the snippet’s own headerType variant switcher — the file labels it “demo controls” — built the pre-atom way: inter-segment border-left dividers, no sliding .ind, active state by background and disabled. The atom’s construction explicitly abandoned the divider, and injecting a thumb radius into a control with no thumb, plus a button radius onto divider-separated segments, would round interior corners against their dividers. FOUND BY THIS SWEEP AT s229 and reported, NOT fixed: it renders square in every theme, which is a real if minor gap, and it is Dave’s call whether demo chrome follows the theme at all.

Tab-bar — the mobile pill

Specimen spliced verbatim from knowledge/snippets/Tab-bar.reference.html; style from canon.css via .cn-tab-bar.

The mobile navigation lozenge. A 56 px track with border-radius:999px on both the track and the sliding indicator, <a class="seg__item"> anchors rather than buttons, and no aria-pressed.

The exemption reason on file, from knowledge/component-types.json → segmented.$scan.$exempt:
NOT the segmented atom. Tab-bar’s .seg is the MOBILE PILL nav lozenge — 56px track, border-radius:999px on both track and indicator, .seg__item anchors rather than buttons, no aria-pressed. The atom’s own header records the split: “the rounded PILL shape is NOT a seg variant (Dave 2026-07-24) — reserved for a MOBILE tab-bar alternative”. Joining would flatten a deliberately round control onto the square themes’ 0. The NAME COLLISION is the real finding and is left standing, not silently absorbed: two different atoms both call their track .seg. Flagged for Dave at #229, not decided here.

Avatar group

Itinerary row 57, PROPOSED #203 — your eye owed. The gated Avatar disc, copied verbatim; what is new is only the arrangement.

It adds nothing to the disc. Avatar’s surface, ring, round exemption and 32/44/64 size ramp are copied as they stand. What this adds is the overlap, the separator ring, the display cap, the overflow counter, and one accessible name for the whole set. One person = Avatar; a countable set = Avatar group; a list of people you act on individually = List-items.

⚠ The ring defect is shown, not described — scroll to the last block in each pane. The separator ring binds background/default, so the stack only separates correctly on the page. Dropped onto a card it is the wrong colour. In light mode the card is also white and the fault hides; in the dark panes the page is #1A1A1A and the card is #1F1F1F, so every disc wears a dark halo. The fixes are a surface-keyed token or a ring the consumer sets — both yours, and I invented neither.

PROPOSED, your call — two accessible shapes for one component. By default the GROUP carries the name and the discs are hidden from screen readers, because a stack read out disc-by-disc is noise. The “roster” variant does the opposite for when the individuals genuinely are the content. Two shapes is a decision, not a feature.

Interactive discs are built at medium and large only. A 32px clickable disc would breach the ruled 44px minimum, and no gate reads that token — so it is enforced by simply not building it. Small stacks that need to be clickable become one 44px target instead.

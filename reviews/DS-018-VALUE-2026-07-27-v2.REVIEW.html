1First, a correction — the collision in the record does not exist

The handoff carries, in three places, that B-D4 ruled #808080 while canon ships #9D9D9D — "two ledgered numbers that disagree". They do not disagree. It is one ruling read against half of itself.

B-D4's prose says text/on-disabled = #808080 "both modes". But the same ledger's Values — SETTLED (Dave, 2026-07-20, dialled on the v7 live editor) block gives the light/dark pair. Canon and the token store both implement that pair exactly:

The record compared B-D4's prose against canon's light value only, and reported a disagreement between what are actually the two halves of one light/dark ruling. Nothing needs reconciling. This is the "trust the spine, don't mine the prose" shape — and it will be inscribed as a correction in all three places at wrap, as loudly as the original claim.

2The question that replaces it — the backdrop was never matched

text/on-disabled was dialled to sit on the disabled fill #E1E1E1. That is the backdrop its "faint but visible" was judged against. The legend label sits on the chart ground — the row has no background at all (Chart-bar.reference.html:203).

So borrowing #9D9D9D for the chart legend does not borrow a ruling. It borrows a judgement made against a different backdrop, and lands somewhere else. Same swatch, two grounds:

5Value 2 — --muted, the one standing next to it

OBSERVED: .dv-leg-sw[aria-checked="false"]{border-color:var(--muted)} — the hidden-series swatch. --muted is used in 4 of 6 chart snippets (bar, combo, donut, line) and declared in none. Not in type.css either. It is declared in canon.css:748, which is not in the chart cascade.

Why it belongs in this decision and not a later one

C2 is the wide gate: a declaration referencing a custom property that resolves nowhere in its own scope is a build failure. The day it lands it fails on --muted too. So the enactment either answers this in the same pass, or ships a gate that immediately red-flags four snippets. One decision, one scope.

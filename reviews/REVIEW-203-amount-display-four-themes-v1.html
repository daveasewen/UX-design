Amount / currency display

The money-format primitive (itinerary row 89) — currency before the value closed up, tabular lining figures so columns align, three sizes on the figure ramp, monochrome but for the one ruled positive seat.

Every pane below is the real gated snippet markup, copied verbatim and rendered through the generated canon.css — not a redraw. Four Apollo themes, each light and dark. Behaviour (typing, auto-advance, blur-normalise) is not wired here; it lives in the gated snippet and the showroom. This surface is for your eye across themes.

Premise correction, before anything else. The #203 brief and the itinerary (dated 2026-07-14) both list this component as a P1 Gap. It is not. It was built, gated and generated on 2026-07-22 (Phase-2 wave 1) and has a snippet, a meta, a .cn- canon partial and a showroom page. The itinerary row is stale by five weeks. Nothing was rebuilt here; what was missing was this — a four-theme surface for your eye. Correcting the itinerary is yours, not a sub's.

Open, for your eye — the ruling says MONO ONLY, and nothing makes it so. The positive-amount green binds --success-ink: var(--rag-success-ink). Probed against canon.css: --rag-success-ink and --rag-error-ink have two definitions each — base light and base dark — while their siblings --rag-success and --rag-error have eight, one per theme per mode. That asymmetry is correct: s151-D1 rules the ink pair MONO ONLY. But the cascade has no mechanism to enforce mono-only, so under Legacy the mono green #137F3C simply renders anyway — beside a theme whose own success colour is the teal #00847F. Look at the Legacy and Console panes and judge whether that reads as a bug.

It is not one component: --rag-*-ink has four consumers in canon.css (this atom's --success-ink, an --error-atom, and the two sparkline --spark-up/--spark-down), none of them fenced. PROPOSED #203, your eye owed: a fence, so a mono-only ink resolves to something theme-legal outside mono. Re-legging rag/*-ink per theme is the other route, but that would reopen s151-D1 — yours, not a sub's, and not proposed here.

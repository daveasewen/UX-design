KPI / trend tile

Itinerary row 55, PROPOSED #203 — your eye owed on all of it. Real snippet markup rendered through the canon theme cascade in all four themes, light and dark.

This component is FLOATED, not ruled — and that is your own ruling, not my caution. s182-D2 names it and declines to rule it. Your words, quoted in the ruling: “might be a partially built trend card with option, I haven’t decided yet”, and the ruling adds “never launder into a ruling”. So row 55 was never an ordinary gap for a sub to close. What is below is the drawing that lets you answer it by eye. Nothing here closes anything.

How it differs from the gated Stat card — in one line. Stat card states the change; the KPI tile states the shape of the change. Everything else is Stat card, copied verbatim: the label, the value, the arrow, the wording, the period. The only addition is the twelve-point spark, and that is the Chart-sparkline atom at the exact inline scale its own source already calls “the KPI / Stat-card scale”. A KPI tile with no series is a Stat card and should be one.

⛔ Look along the rows: the arrow and the line beneath it are DIFFERENT GREENS in seven of these eight panes. This is the live two-red question. I have deliberately not resolved it — I inherited both bindings exactly as they stand. The arrow takes the FILL seat it inherits from the gated Stat card, and that seat forks per theme. The spark line takes the INK seat that s182-D3 rules for this atom — “the rules for colours should just follow the red and green ink colours for all themes” — and that seat is theme-invariant by that ruling. One forks, one does not, so they disagree almost everywhere. Measured in the browser today:

Payment card visual

Itinerary row 93, PROPOSED #204 — your eye owed on all of it. Real snippet markup rendered through the canon theme cascade in all four themes, light and dark.

Every brand surface on this card is deliberately EMPTY, and that is the headline. Card-network marks, issuer logotypes, the EMV chip contact pattern and any embossing are licensed artwork governed outside this design system — and #86-D2 fences our own marks too: your words, “the crescent is only a mark for apollo, we use it when we need designs to be anonymous / use the hsbc mark normally”. So this file builds the anatomy and leaves the brand surfaces as dashed, labelled, declared placeholders. The probe: ls knowledge/assets/icons/**/ | grep -iE "chip|visa|mastercard|amex|network" returns nothing — no such glyph exists in the HSBC library, and drawing one would be an invented brand mark, which is worse than an invented icon.

The one glyph on the card is real. assets/icons/media/contactless.svg, byte-matched via a <symbol>. It is a functional indicator (this card taps), not a brand mark.

How it differs from the gated Account card, in one line. Account card’s subject is money — label, masked number, balance, status. This card’s subject is the physical instrument, and it carries no balance at all. Account card answers “how much is in it?”; this answers “which piece of plastic is this, and is it usable right now?”. A payment-card visual showing a balance is an Account card wearing a card shape. Everything shared — the shell, the ··· masking idiom, the Tags atom, the RAG chip, the tertiary button — is copied from the approved artefacts and was not re-drawn.

No dark or “premium” card face — because the token does not exist. knowledge/_COMPONENT-GAPS.md already logs it: “Inverse / hero surface — no light-mode dark-band role — the portfolio invented charcoals (#0E1014…)”. Painting a dark card face today would re-commit exactly that recorded defect, so the face binds tertiary/background/default like Account card. If you want the dark face, the answer is to mint the inverse-surface role first. Yours.

Geometry is a physical standard, not my taste. aspect-ratio: 1.586 is ID-1 (ISO/IEC 7810, 85.60×53.98 mm). Corners follow the angular rule — square in Mono and Legacy, 20px in Console via the cascade. Whether a card visual should deviate from the angular rule because real cards are rounded is yours; I flagged it rather than taking it.

Inherited, not resolved: the chip dot binds rag/success / rag/warning as a fill (1.98:1 and 2.18:1 on white, measured today). That is the same seat as the live Stat-card / KPI-tile two-red question and it is on this lane’s do-not-rule list. It is never load-bearing here — every chip carries its word (“Active”, “Frozen by you”, “Expired 08/25”), and the unusable states also drop opacity, a second non-colour channel.

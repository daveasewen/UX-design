Empty state

The gated Empty-state component, rendered through the generated canon.css in all four themes — light and dark. Three registers: first-run (invite the first action), no results (offer the way back), quiet (legitimately nothing to do, no action at all). Every pane is the real snippet markup, not a redraw.

PROPOSED #203, your eye owed — the glyph. The icon library has no spot-illustration set, so an empty region is text-led with a decorative 48px glyph held at 0.4 ink. That was the component’s own recorded finding, carried forward untested across themes. Look along the eight panes: in Console and Supercharge dark that 0.4 glyph is the faintest thing on the page. The question is whether 0.4 is right in every theme, or whether the glyph should go and the title carry it alone.

PROPOSED #203 — one action, maximum. First-run offers a button, no-results offers a text link, quiet offers nothing. That escalation is deliberate and worth a nod or a no.

Defect found, deliberately NOT fixed here (outside this lane’s fence). Empty-state.reference.html declares --pri-hover as #626262 light / #B7B7B7 dark, but the token store now says #636363 / #B2B2B2 after s198-D1. The snippet gate is already red at HEAD on exactly this, across nine snippets — eight of them nothing to do with this lane. It is a one-line-per-file correction for the conductor, not a decision for you.

Measured, your call — the no-results link is an 18px-tall target. The first-run button measures exactly 44×44 in all eight panes, as ruled. The no-results “Clear the search” text link measures 18px tall in all eight. No gate enforces the 44px minimum, so nothing has ever gone red on it. Whether a recovery link inside an empty state should be a 44px target, or whether inline links are legitimately exempt, is a rule you have not written down — so I have not written it either. Flagging, not fixing.

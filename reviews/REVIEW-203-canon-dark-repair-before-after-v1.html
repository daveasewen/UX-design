Canon dark-drop repair — before vs after

#203 Lane G. gen_canon_components.py's harness drop test read any selector starting [data-theme…] as a harness variable block. It is not: [data-theme="dark"] .se-msg .ic{…} is a reviewed dark-mode component decision. 33 such rules across 19 components never reached canon.css — with every gate green.

Left column = the shipped behaviour, driven from a byte snapshot of the pre-fix canon.css. Right column = repaired. Both columns share the identical base cascade; the diff between the two canon files is exactly 33 added lines, 0 removed.

PROPOSED #203, Dave's eye owed. Nothing on this page is a ruling.

  • The repair is a discrimination, not a new value: no descendant ⇒ still dropped as a harness var block; a descendant ⇒ scoped and kept.
  • The recovered colours are the snippets' own reviewed decisions — mostly the mono error-ink camp (white shape, --mark:#000000) and the dark error border.
  • Still open, and NOT touched here: no gate parses the generated partial against its source snippet. That absence is why 33 rules could vanish silently.

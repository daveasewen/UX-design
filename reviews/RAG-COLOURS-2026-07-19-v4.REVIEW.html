The weight rule, side by side

You settled red and spotted the "weird" bit — and it's a real rule. On a bright ground (page, red) the stroke blooms fatter, so Regular (400) read best. On a light fill (amber) the fill blooms inward and thins the text, so Medium (500) read best. Compensation follows polarity — the bright side always eats into the dark side. Here it is side by side, plus mixed-vs-uniform so you can weigh your eye against a normally-sighted one.

§W decision — the dark-mode weight rule
Emerging ruling (your calls, generalised): compensation by polarity. In dark mode — light text on a bright/dark ground (page, red) steps down to 400; dark text on a light fill (amber, green, blue) steps up to 500. It tracks which side blooms into which. Lands as a TYPE ruling paired with R-D6.

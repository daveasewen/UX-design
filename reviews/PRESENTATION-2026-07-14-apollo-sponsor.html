Apollo · design-system engine

Apollo

A governed engine for designing with AI — on brand, accessible, at speed.

Sponsor briefing · July 2026 · David Ewen

The tension

Today

Designers spend their days assembling interfaces from components that already exist. Build work — not craft.

And now

AI can generate an interface in seconds. But left ungoverned, it drifts from brand, from accessibility, from every standard we hold.

The opportunity sits exactly between the two.

The idea

Apollo is a governed
design-system engine.

It generates on-brand, accessible interfaces at speed — and it is built so the machine does the build, and the designer does the craft.

What it is

Four moving parts. No black box.

01

Canon

The design tokens and the reviewed, gated components — the single source of truth for what "on brand" means.

02

Gates

Executable checks for brand fidelity and accessibility. Not guidance — enforcement that can withhold "done".

03

Runbooks

The method: how a task is generated, retrieved, and reviewed. Repeatable, inspectable, improvable.

04

Host agent

The AI that operates it — running today in VS Code with GitHub Copilot, the tools designers already have.

Portable by design — no bespoke runtime. Any capable AI agent can operate the engine.

What's real today

Not a concept.
A working system.

Every component here is generated from the canon and rendered in light and dark, together, for review — the same view designers and reviewers use.

The shift

Craft over build.

The machine assembles what is known. The designer is freed for what is new — patterns, journeys, judgment. AI doesn't replace the designer's craft. It clears the ground for it.

One engine, many modes

The same brain, tuned for the task.

A

Factory

Strict, compliant assembly of known components. Boring by design — and for production, that is exactly right.

B

Creative

Inject inspiration to explore new directions. What earns its place flows back to become new canon.

C

Component dev

Build and review new components — the raw material the whole system runs on.

D

Explore

Open, low-stakes noodling — where ideas start before they are shaped.

The compliance gates hold across every mode. That shared floor is the moat.

Why it matters

Four returns, one system.

Value

Designers on the high-value work

Less time assembling the known; more on the craft only they can do.

Governance

Accessibility built in

Brand and accessibility enforced by the engine — not bolted on, not left to chance.

Craft

The output quality rises

A consistent floor frees attention for taste, nuance, and the new.

Speed

Brief to compliant UI, faster

The repetitive build collapses from hours to moments.

Where we are, honestly

This is experimental. The component inventory is still early, and the approach is deliberately phased. We are showing progress and learning in the open — not claiming a finished product. That candour is the point: it is how we get this right.

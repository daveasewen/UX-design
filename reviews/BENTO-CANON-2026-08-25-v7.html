Three roles, one grammar

You said a page-level bento and a sectioned one are not the same thing. They are now three roles, and the role decides where the corner radius sits, what the spacing is, and whether the wall is squared off. Everything else — the columns, the bands, the span vocabulary, the portrait rule — is unchanged and shared.

The page opens in console on purpose. Console is the only theme whose corner radius is not zero, so it is the only theme where you can see the difference between the three roles. The grammar is identical in all four: switch the theme above and the same rules simply resolve to a radius of 0. Nothing else moves.

Dashboard — radius on each inner container, tiles 1px apart

A sectioned app: a bento of bentos. The radius sits on each inner bento’s container and the tiles inside it are square and one pixel apart, so each section reads as one rounded slab of information. The outer wall keeps the theme gutter — it is holding the sections apart, not the tiles.

Gallery — ragged is allowed, captions are roomier

Page-level photography. Radius and spacing sit on the tiles, as brochureware — but the squaring pass relaxes. Orphans are acceptable here; the only requirement is a layout that suits portrait and landscape pictures, and that is the aspect rule, which is untouched. Portraits are still two rows tall.

The caption space is more generous. It is now 86px, up from 62 — +24px, about 39%. That is not a taste pick: it buys one more line of description (two → 3) and steps the padding one rung up the 4px ladder (8 → 12). The clamp is derived from the space, so the two can never disagree.

What is canon here, and what is this page

Canon: the container, the grid, the span vocabulary, the bands, the three role rules and the gallery caption space — all generated into knowledge/canon/canon.css by canon/gen_canon_bento.py from tokens/layout.json (layout/bento, including the new $roles block and caption-space). The role is an data-bento-role attribute on the instance — no JavaScript, no new class. This page declares no grid, no gap, no bento radius and no role rule of its own.

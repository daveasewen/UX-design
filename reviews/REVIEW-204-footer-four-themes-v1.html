Footer

Itinerary row 81, PROPOSED #204 — your eye owed. Verified absent first-hand: no snippet, no meta, no content match under doormat or mega-footer, and no ruling in _rulings.json.

“Footer” already means something else here, and that is the first thing to decide. In this repo a footer is a container’s action row — Drawer’s, Modals’, Data-grid’s pagination strip — and the gated Action-bar describes itself, in its own words, as “a footer bar that pins a screen/flow’s actions”. This component is the other footer: the end matter of a document. The two can sit on one screen and are not variants. If it keeps this name it inherits the collision. Renaming is yours; nothing here renames anything.

The link atom is the gated one, unchanged. Underline never colour, hover and pressed as one combined state, the inset focus underline with no blue ring, and the current page marked by weight plus a persistent thick underline. Links’ own source already calls its related-links list “the footer / utility pattern”, and its meta already declares a Footer context — the graph anticipated this component before it existed.

Your call — every link here is a 44px target at every width. The gated pattern only grows its targets when stacked; this decides it once, everywhere. The cost is visible: the doormat is taller than the compact typographic ideal a footer usually reaches for. Look at the four columns and tell me whether the height is worth the certainty.

Also unruled: the band colour (it sits on the subtle surface so the end matter reads as a band — in Mono light that is a very quiet distinction); whether the doormat and the slim bar are one component or two; and what belongs in the legal row, which is regulatory and varies by market.

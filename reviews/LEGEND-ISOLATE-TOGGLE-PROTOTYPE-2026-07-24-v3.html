DECIDEOne open a11y call to make while you feel it: the isolate is built as exclusive toggle-buttons (aria-pressed, “Show only X”) rather than a true role="radiogroup". After an additive swatch toggle you can be showing two series, where a radiogroup has no single “checked” member — toggle-buttons stay honest there. A radiogroup gives the arrow-key scrub-through-solos feel but carries that mixed-state quirk. Rule by feel — this is the thing to decide. reviews/LEGEND-ISOLATE-TOGGLE-PROTOTYPE-2026-07-24-v3.html

Pattern across all six: the answer is stored where only a human editing CSS or a ruling can change it. A factory producing options needs the same facts stored where a program can read them — which is the case for adding tags, and also the case against inventing tags where a smaller lever (a dial, a variant list) would do.

Which tags let a factory produce three distinct solutions

Three, in this order of leverage: answers (what the brief needs) → interchangeable-with with weights (which parts could serve it, ranked) → fits (which of those can sit in the space available). data-shape is the pre-filter that keeps a status out of the KPI row; prominence sizes the row. variant-of, contains, related-to add nothing a factory would choose on.

The validator (one script, the repo’s existing shape)

  • every interchangeable-with target resolves to a meta (the _validate_kg.py arm-2b pattern) and shares at least one answers word;
  • answers and data-shape words come from one vocabulary file (the chart-intents.json / _validate_intent_resolve.py pattern: an unknown word refuses, loud and named);
  • fits is written in the ruled span vocabulary and min ≤ max;
  • a component may not be interchangeable with something it mustNotNeighbour in the same view without a when clause.
04

Two worked examples

The status metric, and the 1100 band.

One shows a tag turning a ruling into a routing rule. The other shows a template default becoming two weighted alternatives.

A · “Awaiting approval: 14, threshold 5”

Today this datum arrives as a kpi-tile in the headline row (the W1 Q2 tiles), which breaks kpi-tile’s own antiPattern (“a KPI tile with no series is a Stat card”) and which s247-D3 rules must not happen: the two status tiles “are NOT to be hidden among the KPI tiles… their own surface, or in tandem with a status bar component at the top.” DP-08 says the same from the other side: a headline metric is value + delta + sparkline, never a gauge.

With tags the factory never has to know the ruling. The brief item is typed data-shape: count+threshold. kpi-tile and stat-card declare data-shape: series / delta, so neither is a candidate. status-indicator and the needs-attention recipe declare count+threshold and answers: status-of-N; both are candidates, ranked by interchangeable-with weight and filtered by fits against the space left after the prominence-1 row. s247-D3 becomes a consequence of the data, and the ruling text can be cited from the tag’s governedBy.

B · The 1100 band — reflow vs compress

s248-D4: “At 1100 the side-by-side 4+2 (chart compressed) could equally be right — it depends on the prominence the chart should have; the template ships the re-flow, and which one applies is the designer’s choice in edit mode.” Today that sentence is enacted as rule 6b — CSS that always reflows. As metadata it is two entries on the evidence group:

What it costs: the C9 gate must learn to read the alternatives (it cannot read rule 6b today either, so this is a debt already owed).

05

Alternatives and the risk

“It might not be the solution.” Three cheaper levers that reach some of the same place.

Tags are the most general answer and the most expensive to keep true. Each alternative is judged on the same test: can it give three distinct solutions to one brief without a person editing CSS?

1 · Prompt-side option generation

Ask the model for three arrangements and let it substitute freely. Cost zero. #246 measured the ceiling: with the library as it is, four runs produced the template’s specimen four times — “the library dictates the thin result.” Without candidates in the library the options are the model’s taste, unauditable and unrepeatable.

2 · A recipe library

Store three or four whole compositions (4+2 · 2+4 · 3+3 · strip-led) as snippets, the s246-D4 route. Cheap per recipe, no validator, reads like the library already does. Ceiling: options grow by hand, and a recipe cannot answer a brief whose data does not match its specimen — the status-tile problem recurs inside every recipe.

3 · Band rules with a “variant” dial

Keep the CSS, add spans and bandMode: reflow | compress to _bento_edit_rails.json (the s246-D3 dial that is still absent). Small, fits the rails vocabulary that already exists, and covers examples B fully. Ceiling: it varies layout only; it cannot move a status out of the KPI row because it does not know what the datum is.

The honest risk

Tags describe a judgment as if it were a fact. A weight of 0.8 is Dave’s taste at authoring time, frozen; the 25 $differsFrom blocks show the library has preferred prose precisely because the judgment has a “when”. A tag set without a when clause, a validator, and a consumer that reads it would be an instrument without a consumer — the repo’s own named anti-pattern. The smallest true thing may be the dial (3) plus data-shape alone.

06

Decide

Ruling-shaped questions.

Nothing here is ruled. Each question names its options; a skip is not a yes.

  1. Is the option space a library fact or a prompt fact?

    (a) tags in the metas, read by the factory · (b) the rails dial only (layout options, no surface options) · (c) prompt-side, no library change · (d) tags for data-shape + answers only, dial for layout.

  2. One span vocabulary before any fits tag: 6 or 12?

    The template is 6 (--layout-bento-columns:6); Dave’s notes and the #216 showcase are 12; s249-D2 says 12 is “desirable only.” (a) 12 canonical, 6 = pairs · (b) 6 canonical · (c) both, with a declared mapping.

  3. Are weights authored numbers, ordered lists, or nothing?

    (a) 0–1 weights as Dave floated · (b) an ordered list, no numbers · (c) a when clause only, the factory ranks by fit. (b) and (c) are cheaper to keep true.

  4. Does answers extend the chart-intents vocabulary or start a second one?

    (a) extend chart-intents.json to non-charts (status-of-N, one-number, what-needs-me) · (b) a sibling file with its own resolver · (c) not now.

  5. Which components get the first authoring pass?

    (a) the 11 in template-dashboard-bento $composes · (b) those plus the 14 charts · (c) everything with category: molecule|organism.

  6. Does rule 6b stay as CSS, become an alternative pair, or move to the dial?

    (a) leave — the template ships reflow · (b) two alternatives on the evidence group, gate reads them · (c) bandMode in the rails. Also settles whether C9 is taught to read the new fact.

  7. Is “three solutions” a factory parameter (k) or a prompt phrase?

    (a) k in the generate-from-canon skill, default 1 · (b) the user asks in words, the model decides · (c) k=3 in factory mode always, 1 for the presentation.

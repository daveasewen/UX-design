Where does SPLICED end and PARAPHRASE begin?

“it seems to interpreted rather than get the component, it was really close but I could tell it was built locally rather than retrieved… bit odd” Dave, testing Apollo-Spider v1.0.2 — notes/_receipts/2026-08-31-230-postwrap-dave-v102-test.md

The detector is built and mutation-tested. It reads the canon skeleton — tag names and class nesting, never your copy, labels, data or aria text — and scores how much of a built page’s structure exists in knowledge/snippets/. One thing is still yours to decide: the line. Below are the same eight samples read three ways.

How to read this

  1. FIDELITY is the grade. “Is every structural move in this build one the canon snippet makes?” It is precision, not recall — because trimming a component is legitimate (keep the chart, drop the sort switch) and must never go red, while inventing structure is the whole tell.
  2. COVERAGE is information, never the verdict. How much of the canon component survived. A low coverage with a perfect fidelity means “spliced, then trimmed” — which is fine.
  3. Read down each dial column and find the one whose verdicts you agree with. The three real samples at the top are the ones that must stay green; the planted ladder below them is what must go red.

What is yours to rule

  1. The SPLICED threshold. Dial A (0.97), B (0.90) or C (0.78) — or a number of your own. The samples leave an empty band between 0.791 and 0.974; anything in it separates every real splice from every planted drift.
  2. The PARAPHRASE floor — where “close but rebuilt” becomes “not this component at all”. Only the hand-written sample P4 reaches it in this set, so this one is under-evidenced and it is honest to say so.
  3. Does the dial need a COVERAGE floor beside the fidelity floor? Sample P5 is the reason: strip canon class names entirely and elements become invisible to the reading rather than wrong, so fidelity RISES while coverage falls. A coverage floor would close it; it would also red a legitimate deep trim. Both costs are real.
  4. Does this ship in the designer pack, and when? Not touched here — the roster is HELD at 58 (s228-D5) and no release machinery was opened.

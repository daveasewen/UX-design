The segmented switch corner — beside the thumb, or in place of it?

Console theme only, light and dark, all four scales. Geometry and colour read at runtime from knowledge/canon/canon.css. Baked 2026-08-18. Version v1.

What is settled and what is not. Dave has ruled the carve-out itself: Console gets its own segmented-switch corner, one dial of 6 off the container, giving 0 · 2 · 4 · 6 across xs, s, m and l. Those four numbers are minted and on disk.

The open question on this page is scope, and it is a question for the eye, not the arithmetic: should the new switch corner sit beside the existing thumb corner — two tokens, the plain segmented control keeps its softer 4/6/6/8 — or should it replace it, so every segmented thumb in Console tightens to 0/2/4/6? Left column is beside. Right column is replace. Nothing on this page decides it.
In plain words
  • Same outer box in both columns. Heights, insets and outer corners never change here. The only thing moving is the corner on the inner moving part.
  • Beside means the plain three-up control stays as it is today — softer inner corner, 4 at xs up to 8 at l — and only the two-state switch tightens.
  • Replace means one inner corner for everything Console draws inside a segmented track: 0 at xs, 6 at l. Tighter, more concentric, and one fewer token to reason about.
  • Where to look: the top control in each block is the plain segmented control; the bottom one is the switch. In the left column those two differ. In the right column they match.
Controls are live — click a segment, the thumb moves.

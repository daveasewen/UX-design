Skeleton loader - four themes x light/dark (#203, Lane F)

Read this first. The brief sent me to build this component from nothing, on the strength of the itinerary spreadsheet marking it Status = Gap. It is not a gap - it already exists, and has for some time. I verified that by hand before writing a line: the snippet is in the tree, the meta is in the tree, and for File upload and Stepper the meta’s own purpose field names its itinerary row and says it was built. So I did not rebuild anything. Rebuilding would have thrown away real work. What I did instead is the part that genuinely had never been done: put the component in front of you across all four themes, light and dark, and chase down the specific defect the brief named.

Itinerary row 72. Every pane below is the real component markup rendered through the generated canon.css, not a copy.

Defect found, for your eye - not fixed here: this snippet is the only one of my three that does not pull canon/type.css (gate TYPE-001). Its headings and captions fall back to a raw font-size (14px and 12px) instead of a type composite. On this page it looks right, because this page links type.css itself - open the snippet on its own and it will not. The fix is one line, but it means editing a file outside my fence, so it is queued for you rather than done.

PROPOSED #203, your eye owed. The bones are deliberately low-contrast and carry aria-hidden, so the manifest declares no contrast pairs at all. That is a defensible reading of 1.4.11 - decorative placeholders convey no information - but it is the only one of my three with an empty list, which means no gate can ever fail on the bone colour. Worth a look at the Supercharge and Legacy dark panes especially: are the bones still readable as structure there, or have they sunk into the background?

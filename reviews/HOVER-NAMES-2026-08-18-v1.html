The naming question — what "-1 / -2" is actually encoding

Every other mono step is a numbered ramp rung (mono/7, mono/10). But hover-1 / hover-2 encode light mode vs dark mode — a different meaning wearing the same suffix shape. The precedent raise-1..3 encodes elevation offsets, so the tier already has suffixes that aren't ramp positions; the question is whether a mode-pair should look like a sequence.

One collision worth your eye: the deprecated Sutherland tokens icon/on-light (depricate)/hover-1 and hover-2 share these exact leaf names. They're retiring, but until they're gone a grep for hover-1 hits both families.

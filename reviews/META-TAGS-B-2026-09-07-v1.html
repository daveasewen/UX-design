Batch B is the 13 chart metas other than chart-bar — every knowledge/components/*chart*.meta.json that carries intent (14 of them), minus chart-bar, which lane A authors as part of the bento template's $composes list. Each one now carries provides · answers · shape · span · priority · when, plus not-with on the 6 whose own prose demanded one.

How to answer. Strike or change by section number. A value is changed by naming it ("07 priority → 50", "chart-pie span → 4–6"). The numbered calls in the last section are the things I decided rather than asked — strike those too. Nothing on this page is ruled.

Grounding rule. Every value is read off the meta's own purpose and responsive prose, never off roles.json — roles.json's 60/60/60/60/60 priorities and its when strings are ILLUSTRATIVE by s252-D1, and the metas become canon. Where I used one of its strings, it is quoted so you can see it.

Gates. _validate_roles_resolve.py · _validate_kg.py · _build_integrity.py · gen_component_partials.py — all GREEN over these 13. The resolver proves no address dangles; it proves nothing about whether a value is RIGHT. That is what this page is for.

Calls made

10 things I decided rather than asked

Per s251-D15. Strike by number; anything unstruck is what gets kept.

  1. The ladder is 92–30 in steps of 4, not 60–30 in steps of 5. roles.json ties five charts at 60 and three at 40; the resolver requires in-role uniqueness, so the ties had to break somewhere. Starting at 92 rather than 60 leaves headroom above chart-bar for a chart nobody has built yet.
  2. chart-bar belongs at the TOP of the role — this lane proposes 92. It is lane A's field to write, so this lane wrote nothing into chart-bar. 92 because the bento template composes it and it is the default answer to the most common intent (comparison). Lane A, running concurrently, authored 84 instead, which puts chart-bar BELOW chart-line. The ladder above shows lane A's landed number; if you want chart-bar first, the value to change is 84 → 92.
  3. chart-pie 48 > Chart-candlestick 44 > Chart-butterfly-h 40. roles.json ties all three at 40. I broke the tie by how often a brief would reach for the form: a composition snapshot is asked for more often than OHLC, which is asked for more often than a mirrored pair.
  4. shape is written as <x-dimension> × <mark>. A two-part string reads as a shape rather than a label, and it makes true substitutes collide on purpose: chart-donut and chart-pie both say parts-of-whole, the two butterflies both say categories × two-series-mirrored. The resolver's instead-of grade compares shape strings, so equality there is load-bearing, not cosmetic.
  5. span is read off each meta's responsive prose, not off a house rule. A chart whose meta says "FIT hooks are NOT wired" gets max 8; one whose fractions are declared gets max 12; the two circles, which DV-D02 excludes from compression, get 6–6. 100px per column at the 1200px max is the conversion.
  6. The when predicate is two halves, separated by an em-dash. Left = the gate (fields only). Right = which sibling this beats or yields to, with that sibling's priority in brackets. R2 asked for the second half; keeping it on the right of a dash means a future parser can still read the gate alone.
  7. not-with is authored in ONE direction only. chart-pie ↛ chart-donut is written; the donut's reciprocal is DERIVED and printed by the resolver (s251-D6, ISO 25964). Authoring both would be the second home ADR-0017 forbids.
  8. The dv-014 "a second chart re-using these series colours" edges were NOT promoted to not-with. They name no slug, and s251-D6 makes slug required. They stay in edges.mustNotNeighbour as the declared migration backlog.
  9. Chart-bullet provides headline-metric and nothing else. It is the one chart in this batch that is not a chart-panel — ROLES-REVIEW call 3, one role per component. Its answers is still comparison, because answers must equal intent and the intent word is not the role.
  10. chart-stacked-area keeps a TWO-word answers. Its intent is already the array [change-over-time, composition], and the resolver asserts equality — so the array is copied, not collapsed. It is the only meta in the batch that spans two intents.

Still open after this page, and not decided here: whether shape becomes a closed store with a resolver · the when field list · whether a brief may ask for chart-panel and get chart-sparkline at all (ROLES-REVIEW §03 left it in the role at 30).

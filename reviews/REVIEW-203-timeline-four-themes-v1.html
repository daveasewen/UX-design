Timeline / activity feed

Itinerary row 56, PROPOSED #203 — your eye owed. Verified absent first-hand: no snippet or meta existed under timeline, activity, feed, history or audit.

Three gated components already sit close to this one, so the meta says plainly how it differs. List-items is the interactive, unordered transaction row — every row a button — and answers “which one do I open?”. Progress-tracker is a finite forward process with done / current / upcoming. This is neither: it is the passive, ordered record, and the time axis is the structure. The tell: take the ordering away and a Timeline stops meaning anything.

It borrows Progress-tracker’s rail and refuses its semantics. The connector-and-node mechanism is copied from the gated component; aria-current and role="progressbar" are deliberately not, because a timeline has no current step, no total and no completion. Putting them on would lie to a screen reader.

Colour never travels alone. Every status dot has its word beside it — Completed, Pending, Failed, Submitted. The neutral label on every RAG tint measures 8.21–14.46 in both modes. The warning dot on its own is under 3:1, which is precisely why it never appears on its own. Squint at Legacy and Supercharge: the meaning should survive the hue entirely.

PROPOSED, your call — the rail nodes are round. The 12px node and the 8px status dot are circles. That is my reading of the angular rule, not a ruled exemption: Avatar and Badge are the two documented ones. Square nodes are equally buildable. Also unruled: newest-first ordering, and whether the activity form should carry a status node as well as the avatar.

How to use this

  1. Each card opens with a plain-English summary. That is the whole decision. The technical detail is folded underneath — open it only if the summary does not settle it for you.
  2. Pick an option. A RECOMMENDED tag means the record argues for it; it is a reading, not a nudge, and the reasoning is in the pros and cons.
  3. Add a note wherever your reasoning matters more than your pick — the why is what gets inscribed in the ledger.
  4. You do not have to finish. Ruling Tier 0 alone unblocks real work.
  5. Export at the end produces a paste-back block. Nothing is stored in the browser — the export is the save.

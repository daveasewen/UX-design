§W · Text weight — settling the dark-mode light-bleed rule

Light-on-dark makes strokes bloom heavier (canon: "icon strokes appear heavier"), so the dark-mode fix is to step the weight down. Your idea: that doesn't apply on colour. The model agrees and the rig shows it — bloom (how much the stroke fattens) is high on the dark page, nearly as high on the deep red, and small on a light fill. §W decision — the dark-mode weight rule

A · Step-down on the page only: in dark mode, light text on the dark page drops one weight step (e.g. label 500→400); text on a coloured fill keeps its light-mode weight. Matches the model + your instinct.

B · No step-down anywhere: keep light-mode weights everywhere; accept the page bloom. Simplest rule, ignores the effect you can see.

C · Step-down everywhere in dark: the blunt current-canon reading; over-lightens text on colour where it isn't needed.

Recommendation: A. The compensation should track the bloom, and the bloom is on the dark page, not on colour. This becomes a TYPE ruling (T-D) and pairs with R-D6. Which weights step where is your eye's call — tell me the pair (e.g. "labels 500 on colour, 400 on page").

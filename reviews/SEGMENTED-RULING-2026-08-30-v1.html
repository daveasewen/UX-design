Segmented control — your #227 ruling, enacted

reviews/SEGMENTED-RULING-2026-08-30-v1.html · specimens are the canon atom under the built canon.css · renders UNPROVEN (no browser in the build VM)

“were going to make changes across the board for seg-conts, xs is going to be 28 height. medium 44 is going to have 2px padding not 4. small and extra large stay the same. the inner radii is just not right for console on the the two small sizes, and I got fed up of it continually wrong, especially as we had a way of calculating it, thats why I dropped it.”

What moved

xs outer height 24 → 28px and m track padding 4 → 2px, in all four themes. Console’s xs and s thumb radii are DERIVED again, not hand-typed: the concentric law — thumb = track − padding — is back at the two small sizes, giving 4 and 6 where the flat “dial 6” gave 0 and 2. s and l keep their heights and paddings, as you said.

The flat dial is exactly why it kept coming out wrong at small sizes: subtracting a fixed 6 from a 6px track leaves 0 — a square thumb inside a rounded track. Concentric subtracts the padding instead, so the inner corner stays parallel to the outer one at every size. There is now a check that goes red if any minted thumb ever disagrees with the calculation again.

One thing I did not decide for you

Your ruling moved two things that meet at m: the padding dropped to 2, and the concentric law came back at xs and s only. Console m therefore now reads track 10, padding 2, thumb 4 — where concentric would give 8. Scale l reads track 12, padding 4, thumb 6 vs concentric 8. You said only the two small sizes were wrong, so I changed nothing at m and l. Extending concentric to all four is one constant away — look at the m and l cards in the two Console rows and say the word.

A height match worth not believing

It was put to me that xs at 28 now exactly matches the legacy sm step. It does not. sm’s button is 28px, but its outer box is 34px (28 + 2×2 padding + 2×1 border). xs’s 28 is the outer box. So xs’s whole control equals sm’s inner button — close, but a different measurement. The proposed sm→xs map stays PROPOSED; I have not touched it.

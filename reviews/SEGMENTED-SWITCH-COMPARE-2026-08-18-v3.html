Console theme only, light and dark, all four scales. Geometry and colour read at runtime from knowledge/canon/canon.css. Baked 2026-08-18. Version v3 — supersedes v2.

Why v1 and v2 did not work — and what actually changed

Dave, v2: “its still not working, why?” — the black pill’s corner barely changed across the scales, and the s switch, labelled 2px, looked much rounder than 2px.

The CSS was never the problem. I re-rendered v2 at eight times resolution and sampled the actual pixels of the black pill’s corner. Every corner paints the number it is labelled with. I also repainted the pill bright magenta: no black shape was left behind, so the pill on screen really is the element carrying the per-scale corner, and nothing else was painting over it.

Two things were defeating the eye instead.

  1. A bigger arc sits right next to the small one. The track around the pill has a corner of 6, 8, 10 and 12 — two to four times the pill’s — and it sits only three to five pixels away. At scale s the eye is asked to read a 2px arc nested inside an 8px arc. It reads the 8. That is exactly why 2 looked “much rounder”.
  2. Two pixels at real size is not a decision you can see. The whole ramp spans 0 to 6, in 2px steps, drawn at 100% with antialiasing, on four controls of four different heights sitting in four separate rows. There was no magnification and no shared baseline anywhere on the page.

So v3 changes the presentation, not the geometry. There is a loupe — the same live controls, same tokens, magnified by a vector transform so a 2px arc draws as a 12px arc — and an isolate switch that hides the track entirely, so the only corner on screen is the one being ruled. Use both together and the 0 / 2 / 4 / 6 staircase is unmistakable.

What is settled and what is not. Dave has ruled the carve-out itself: Console gets its own segmented-switch corner, one dial of 6 off the container, giving 0 · 2 · 4 · 6 across xs, s, m and l. Those four numbers are minted and on disk.

The open question on this page is scope, and it is a question for the eye, not the arithmetic: should the new switch corner sit beside the existing thumb corner — two tokens, the plain segmented control keeps its softer 4/6/6/8 — or should it replace it, so every segmented thumb in Console tightens to 0/2/4/6? Left column is beside. Right column is replace. Nothing on this page decides it.
In plain words
  • Same outer box in both columns. Heights, insets and outer corners never change here. The only thing moving is the corner on the inner moving part.
  • Beside means the plain three-up control stays as it is today — softer inner corner, 4 at xs up to 8 at l — and only the two-state switch tightens.
  • Replace means one inner corner for everything Console draws inside a segmented track: 0 at xs, 6 at l. Tighter, more concentric, and one fewer token to reason about.
  • Where to look: the top control in each block is the plain segmented control; the bottom one is the switch. In the left column those two differ. In the right column they match.

A page-edge side sheet for focused sub-tasks that keep the page context visible. The panes below show the snippet's own static anatomy specimen — the same sheet the live drawer uses, laid in-flow — rendered through the generated canon.css.

How Drawer differs from its gated siblings

  • vs Modals Drawer shares the modal mechanics — scrim, aria-modal="true", focus trap, inert background, Esc closes, focus returns to the trigger — but docks to the page edge instead of centring, so the page stays glanceable behind it. Modals for a decision that must interrupt; Drawer for adjacent detail.
  • vs Popover The opposite pole of one axis: Drawer is modal and trapped, Popover is non-modal and untrapped. Nothing overlaps.
  • vs Toast A Drawer is dismissed by the user; a Toast dismisses itself.

The rest of the audit — all PROPOSED, all yours

  • One side only. side is an enum whose only value is "right". Left-docked drawers — navigation, filters, RTL — are unbuilt. Deliberate scope box, or gap? Never ruled.
  • Fixed 400px, full-width under 480px, and the footer stacks its buttons there. Narrow this window past 900px to watch the panes reflow.
  • 44px hit area — checked by hand, passes. Footer buttons are 44px tall; the close × is a 36px visual with a 44×44 ::before. Note the file's own comment: the wave-1 receipt claimed 44 and the file measured 36, and the conductor corrected the receipt to the file. That class of error has happened here before.
  • Footer order is primary-first, left-aligned, mirroring Modals. Consistent.
  • Type-composite debt: 3 violations, all demo chrome, none in the component.
  • The buttons are a namespaced mirror (.dbtn, not .btn), to keep clear of the acknowledged type.css blast radius. It is a hand copy of canon Button, so it can drift from canon Button silently — no gate ties the two together.

Measured finding, all four themes — in Supercharge dark the only thing holding this overlay off the page is a hairline at 1.96:1. These overlays are lifted by the elevation recipe: a shadow in light, a 1px border in dark. In light the panel is exactly the page colour in every theme (1.00:1) and the shadow does all the work — that is the canon recipe and it reads fine. In dark the border takes over, and it is not equal across themes: mono's hairline is #808080 on #1A1A1A = 4.41:1, comfortable; Supercharge's is #524842 on #1A1A1A = 1.96:1, under the 3:1 non-text floor.

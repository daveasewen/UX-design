Segmented-switch radius carve-out — tuner v4 · 2026-08-18 · #202

The open item from s199-D3: console broke border-radius/control off the default alias and set it to 8, with a note that the segmented switch is ruled to get its own carve-out token, because inner/outer nested radii cannot share a value (curve matching). That token was never minted and no tuner exposed it. This page exposes it: one dial, live, against the real minted geometry. v1–v3 are kept untouched.

What is decided, and what is not

  • Minted and shown as-is: console heights 24/36/44/48, insets 2/2/4/4, container radii 6/8/10/12, thumb radii 4/6/6/8, border-radius/control 8. The three square themes' base set (minted s202-D1 at #202) is the same geometry with radii 0.
  • Not decided here: the carve-out's VALUE, and what the carve-out MEANS. Pick a reading below — whichever you pick is exported as a proposed reading with your number attached.
  • Square themes are not on the dial. s201-D5 rules their thumb as max(container − padding, 0) = 0. A carve-out for console cannot round them; they are shown for contrast only.

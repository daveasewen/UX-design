The dark caption ground, lifted in all four themes

Console was fixed first. This page extends the same fix to Mono, Legacy and Supercharge — and shows the one case it does not fix, so you can rule on that separately.

Every colour on this page is resolved out of canon.css at build time. Nothing is typed. Each right-hand card carries no override at all: it renders through the enacted rules.

1 · What you said

Your words, and what they were enacted as

“yes the other themes need an appropriate lift too, align with the neutral primitives please”

Dave, 2026-08-27

The four themes do not share one grey ramp — Supercharge’s is warm by ruling. But they do share one address: the neutral DNA tier. --color-neutral-5 is declared once and each theme resolves it through its own ramp. So the fix is one index in four rows, not four hand-picked greys — and if a theme’s ramp ever moves, this moves with it.

  • Mono, Legacy and Console resolve --color-neutral-5 to #313131.
  • Supercharge resolves the same token to #312C26 — its own warm step, not a neutral grey dropped into a warm theme.
  • Light mode is untouched in every theme. The mint cannot reach it, and the generator refuses by name if anyone tries.
  • No token was minted and no alias repointed. color/neutral/5 already existed; consuming it is the point.

Picked by lift, not by look-alike. The same grey sits differently on different pages, so the test is the lift off each theme’s own dark page, measured in ΔL*. The band is Console’s own number — ΔL* 11.06 — give or take 2, so 9.00 to 13.00.

5 · A separate decision — yours to veto alone

The caption ground the page actually ships in still barely lifts

Everything above is the dark grey caption ground — an option in the capsule chord. It is not what the gallery ships in. Console and Mono ship a lightest-grey caption, which in dark resolves #1F1F1F against a #1A1A1A page: ΔL* +2.49. Nearly as invisible as the thing that was just fixed.

Nothing here is enacted, and the reason is a gap in the ramp, not caution. The band member that fixed the dark-grey ground is --color-neutral-5. Point the lightest-grey caption at it too and the two options become the same colour with two different ink rules — the option stops being an option. The next primitives down are the elevation steps, and they fall short of the band. So there is no primitive that lifts this ground into the band without colliding, and inventing a value is a promotion, which is yours.

Drawn below: what ships today, and the two existing primitives nearest to a fix. Both are real tokens; neither reaches the band.

7 · Not decided here

What is still yours

  • Is the lift right? Three themes land on Console’s exact number. Supercharge lands 9.08, a little under, because its warm ramp has no step nearer the band. The one that is nearer — its third elevation step, #322E29 — is a calculated value, not one pulled from Figma, and it belongs to the raised-surface family the other two caption grounds already use.
  • Should Supercharge stay warm here at all? Its dark page is a neutral #1A1A1A while its surfaces are warm. Keeping the caption warm follows the theme; matching the page would mean a neutral grey. This page draws the warm answer — the neutral one would be #313131, ΔL* +11.06.
  • The lightest-grey default (section 5). Veto it on its own: leave it, move the shipped default onto the dark-grey ground that now works, or add a step to the ramp — which is a promotion.
  • Does a semantic role get minted? Today the binding reads the primitive directly. That is honest for a proposed value with no role yet and wrong as a permanent shape.
  • Does the chord open to the other three themes? Untouched here, and still open.

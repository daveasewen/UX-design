Two vocabularies, on purpose. Sparklines say positive / negative trend and use the default ink for a flat trend; status bars say positive / negative / monitor / neutral as four explicit categories with four explicit colours. The word "neutral" therefore means different things in the two places, and that was ruled acceptable in #184 — trends and categories are different jobs.

Not renamed: the rag-error / rag-warning / rag-success / rag-information tokens. Those are the semantic spine that alerts, banners and inline messages read from; only the chart-facing alias layer took the new words.

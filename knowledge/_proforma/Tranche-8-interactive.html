Bottom tab bar

organism

Three-to-five primary destinations pinned to the bottom, thumb-reachable and always visible — the default primary nav for banking apps. Mobile/app only; on wider screens it promotes to a top bar or side rail. The “More” tab opens an overflow sheet.

In-page nav · on this page

molecule

Jump-links to sections of a long page (help articles, terms, product detail). Sticky, with scroll-spy highlighting the section in view. Links move focus to the target section and degrade to plain anchors with JavaScript off. The rail collapses to a “Contents” disclosure when narrow — drag the width slider to see it.

Eligibility

You must be a UK resident aged 18 or over to open this account. Some products have a minimum income or balance requirement — check the summary box before you apply.

Existing customers can apply in the app; new customers will need proof of identity and address.

Rates & fees

The headline rate is variable and may change after opening. There is no monthly account fee, but charges may apply for arranged and unarranged overdrafts.

Representative examples and the full fee schedule are set out in the terms document.

How to apply

Applying takes about ten minutes. You will be asked for your personal details, then to verify your identity, and finally to review and submit.

Once approved, your account details are available immediately in the app.

Managing your account

Manage standing orders, direct debits and payees from the Move money tab. You can freeze a card, set spending limits and view statements at any time.

Paperless statements are on by default and can be switched to post in settings.

Support

Message us in the app 24/7, or call the number on the back of your card. Accessibility support and alternative formats are available on request.

If something has gone wrong, our complaints process is set out in the help centre.

Atoms: AnchorLink + scroll-spy behaviour. <nav aria-label="On this page">; the active link tracks the section in view via aria-current; activating a link moves focus to the target section (tabindex="-1" + scroll-margin-top). Back-to-top reuses IconButton. Smooth-scroll respects reduced-motion.

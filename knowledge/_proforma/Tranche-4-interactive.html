Accordion

organism

Disclosure rows — activate a header to expand its panel; the chevron rotates. Single-open by default (opening one closes the rest). Tick Allow multiple to keep several open. Keyboard: Enter/Space, ↑/↓, Home/End.

Open the payee, choose Standing order, then set the amount, first date and frequency. Standing orders send automatically until you cancel them, and you can amend the amount at any time.

Faster Payments usually clear within seconds. Card payments can sit as pending for up to a few working days until the merchant collects them; your available balance already reflects them.

Open the transaction and choose Report a problem. Tell us whether you recognise the merchant but dispute the amount, or don’t recognise it at all. Most cases are resolved within 10 working days.

Multi-open variant — headers act independently.

Tooltip

molecule

Hover or Tab to a trigger to reveal help after a short delay. The tip is near-black with mono text and a small arrow; it dismisses on blur or Esc. Meaning is always in the icon and text, never colour alone.

Available balance excludes pending card authorisations and cheques not yet cleared. Reference rate The mid-market rate at 16:00 BST, used for indicative conversions only. Statements older than seven years are archived and may take a little longer to retrieve.

The tip is exposed to assistive tech via aria-describedby, so it’s announced on focus as well as shown on hover.

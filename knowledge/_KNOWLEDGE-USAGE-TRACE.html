What governs layout?

The KB governs the measure — not the composition

Direct answer to the question. The knowledge base does govern layout primitives: a 12-column grid, ~1280px max width, 20px gutters/margins and the breakpoint ladder (web-foundations.md §Responsive grid, tokens/layout.json, --layout-web-columns, --breakpoint-*, --gap-*). But it governs no page template: the charter states it outright — "the canon has no template layer — this is always inferred" (§ fixed-flex line 34). Confirmed: there is zero .cn-page / .cn-grid / .cn-layout class. Component-internal composition is retrieved from .cn-*; the arrangement of components into a screen is always the model's own inference, steered only softly by brand-principles.md (single focal point, clarity over decoration) and, at expressive only, the Linear/Stripe/Mercury/Ramp reference nudge.

So layout is the one dimension the rules don't retrieve. The provenance chart above shows governed screens at pure retrieval — but that measures colour, tokens and components, not layout. The "flatness" can't come from retrieving a bad layout, because no layout is retrieved. It comes from composing an inferred layout out of a fixed vocabulary of create.hsbc human components — which is exactly Dave's hypothesis that the rules may be too tight at the source.

Button — four tiers of motion

Same canonical colours, four motion treatments to calibrate against. Press the buttons in each column. Refined (the new fourth) sits between Themes and Unrestrained: dimensionality expressed purely through scale — it grows toward the cursor and presses down, no shadow or translate.

Canon · ships today

Colour state change only, standard ease. No movement.

Motion themes · agreed family

Tactile press (principle 7): a clean scale-down on press. Restrained.

Refined · scale physics

Dimensionality through scale: grows toward the cursor on hover, presses down on click. No shadow, no translate.

Unrestrained · ceiling

3D-ish depress (presses into the surface), hover lift, spring release, load→success morph.

Two button sets · when the morph applies

The load→success morph is only appropriate when the action processes in-page, without a refresh. Standard actions (navigate / submit-and-reload) must not morph — they'd flash a success that the page reload then discards.

Standard — navigates / reloads. No morph.
Processing — in-page async. Morphs to success.
Full-width · mobile option

Optional full-bleed for mobile — fills its container, keeping the 20px internal padding. Full-width buttons press but don't grow (a full-bleed grow would overflow the edges).

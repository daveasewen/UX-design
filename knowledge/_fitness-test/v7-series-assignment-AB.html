A — Two hues, complementary (follows the two-hue guidance most literally)

Blue ↔ orange in both modes: Midnight Blue + Burnt Orange steps in light; their pastel counterparts Sky Blue + Apricot Orange in dark. Colour-blind-friendly axis (blue/orange survives all common CVD types).

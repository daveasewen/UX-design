Bento parameter tuner

Dave, #217: “We need to promote all the various types of bento to canon, i just need to decide on some of the parameters like gutters etc”. This page is how you decide them. It is not the promotion — it mints nothing, edits no token and touches no canon rule. Turn the dials until the wall looks right in the themes you care about, then press Rebuild export and take the block at the bottom.

Every dial arrives on the value that is in force today on showroom/_foundations/photography.html and in the #216 showcases. Those values are PROPOSED, NOT RULED — they are a starting position, not a recommendation, and the dial marked · changed tells you which ones you have moved off it.

One state object drives both halves of the page: the preview you are looking at and the export block are rendered in the same pass from the same numbers, so the export cannot describe a wall you did not see.

What this page does not dial. The radius dial is the bento tile radius only. Component-level radii — and specifically the segmented-switch carve-out that the radius/corner tuner v2 is owed for — are a different decision on a different instrument, and nothing here touches them or appears in this export.

Where the aspect dials bite. The three aspect thresholds derive a tile's span from the photograph's own shape, so they move the photography bento only. The mixed-card bento carries authored spans, the way a dashboard does. Gutter, outer padding, row height, columns, radius, dense packing, the emphasis rhythm and the responsive bands move both.

What the export deliberately does not contain
  • No component radii, and no segmented-switch carve-out — a different decision on a different tuner.
  • No colour, no surface token choice. Which token a bento tile should paint with is a token decision and it is yours; this page uses surface/subtle for cards and surface/raised for photographs purely so the wall is legible on arrival.
  • No typography. Every text element here carries an existing canon composite.
  • No var() references. Where you left the radius on Theme token, the export carries the resolved pixel value per theme × mode, read live out of this document.

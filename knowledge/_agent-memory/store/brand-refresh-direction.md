HSBC is going through a **brand refresh** that may affect components eventually (not a concern now). Dave provided an 8-point cheat-sheet (2026-06-19), saved verbatim in `UX-design/knowledge/_BRAND-REFRESH-DIRECTION.md`.

**Scope rule (Dave):** bag it for continuity but apply it **only as experimentation when we reach full COMPOSITIONS and JOURNEYS** — never on individual components or the gated canon yet. Deliberately kept OUT of `guidelines/` so the component generation layer doesn't pick it up. "A refresh, not a reset" — consistency across the HSBC eco-system is principle #8.

Themes: elegant (larger/thinner) type with fewer weights; de-frame / avoid "boxes within boxes" (full-width, generous padding); refined/sparing asset & icon use (text over icons); blur ≠ "supercharge" (layout/spacing/hierarchy still required); flexibility over fixed layouts; breathing space / negative space; **logical tactile interactions** (buttons feel pressed, 3D-like motion — bridges to [[motion-themes-and-promotion]] theme "Tactile/press physics"); consistency is a constant.

Dave also wants to **play with the Swiss-design skill** at the composition level when appropriate.

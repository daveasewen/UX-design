**Every session has to have a title** (Dave's rule, 2026-06-22). No session left on a generic auto-title — his Cowork recents list gets cluttered by rubbish names grabbed from opening messages.

**UPDATED 2026-07-18 — two names, not one.** Sessions drift, so the handoff carries both:

- **RENAME THIS SESSION →** `<retrospective title>` — what it turned out to be, written with hindsight.
- **NEXT SESSION TITLE →** `<forward title>` — the opener for tomorrow.

**Why the second one:** naming a session only at the start records the *intention*, not the work. 2026-07-18 opened as "Type retrofit — bind the library to the ramp" and became the halation / edge-extremity discovery — the retrofit was ~15% of it. Dave: *"add a rename instruction into the good morning going forward, it's more efficient than copy and pasting your suggestion."*

**How to apply:**
- Put **both lines at the TOP of `GOOD-MORNING.md`**, under a "do these first" heading — not buried at the end. Dave acts on them before reading anything else.
- Write them as **finished, ready-to-copy lines** needing no reformatting — never as a suggestion embedded in prose.
- Keep titles short and distinct — specific to the focus (component/topic/decision), not generic.
- **I cannot rename a conversation** — no tool exists. That constraint is the whole reason these lines exist: Dave's action should be one copy, not a re-read of the handoff to work out what the session became. Say so plainly rather than implying I've done it.

Dave is **dyslexic and often time-poor**. He wants responses led by a **short exec summary + a clear set of instructions/next-steps** (numbered, minimal), with the **detail kept underneath** for when he needs it. "Nearly always tbh."

**Why:** dense detail-first walls of text are hard to grok quickly; he needs the takeaway + what to do, fast.

**How to apply:** open with 1–2 lines of plain-English outcome, then a short numbered "your next steps" list if there are actions for him. Keep lines short, formatting light (he dislikes over-formatting anyway). Put the supporting detail/reasoning below the summary, not above it. Don't drop the detail — he still wants it available. Pairs with [[working-rhythm-reflection]].

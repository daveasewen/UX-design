**Rule (Dave, 2026-07-15, firm — cost a reverted Tranche 6):** Reuse calibration is a **conversation, not a
solo call.** Before mining decisions from / rebuilding prior work, **ASK Dave what he liked and wants to
keep.** Do NOT decide mine-vs-fresh yourself and present it after — and do NOT default to "build fresh,"
which throws away good, already-reasoned, already-reviewed decisions. *"I didn't mean use everything we had
before — I meant let's not waste the work from before. I was expecting you to ask what I liked."*

**Why:** On Tranche 6 I mined the input-field/search references + tranche decisions, rebuilt the lot fresh,
gated it green, and reported the mine-vs-fresh split as done. Dave's read: *"we've just wasted work we did
in the last session to save work we did weeks ago — this isn't a gain."* The whole point of rule 13 is
**don't waste prior work**; defaulting to fresh inverts it. He reverted the build and asked to go through
changes one-by-one.

**How to apply:**
- At the START of any reuse/mining/rebuild task, ASK: "what did you like / want to keep from the previous
  work here?" Get his list before building anything.
- Default to **preserving** good prior decisions; rebuild only the parts that genuinely need it, and say
  which and why — for his sign-off, up front, not after.
- For a big change set, offer to go **change-by-change** so he can accept/modify each.
- This is the taste/reuse complement to [[feedback-verify-before-asking]]: **verify FACTS yourself, ASK
  about TASTE and reuse.** I mis-split it once (asked about a checkable fact, didn't ask about taste).
  Pairs with [[feedback-clarify-reflect-back]] and [[feedback-route-by-default]].

⚠ The ring defect, shown rather than described — the same group on a card

JL PA SO +3

The separator ring binds background/default, so on a card surface it is the wrong colour. In light mode the card is also white and the defect hides; in dark mode the page is #1A1A1A and the card is #1F1F1F, so the rings read as dark halos. Compare the two themes side by side on the review page. The fix is a surface-keyed token or a consumer-set ring — both are Dave's, and neither is invented here.

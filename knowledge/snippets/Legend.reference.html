A · Horizontal legend, placed top, wrapping

The default form. Swatch toggles the series; the label isolates it. Marks are ghosted, never removed, so the canvas never reflows under a toggle. Overflow is wrap: the rows re-flow onto a second line rather than widening the tile.

B · Vertical legend, placed right, with value, percent and delta — overflow scroll

The column form carries the optional numeric group: value, share of total, and the period delta. The delta numerals are the only coloured text on this page (s151-D1 two-red law — #DA1A00 on the light ground, #F6604C on the dark one). Overflow is scroll: the list keeps a fixed height and scrolls, which is what a legend of twelve series inside a bento tile has to do. Reset returns everything and is disabled while nothing is hidden.

C · Shared legend for a row of small multiples, placed bottom

One legend, three canvases. Toggling a series here dims it in all three at once, because dv-legend resolves its marks with host.closest('figure') and all four elements live in one figure — the shared legend is scope, not a new mechanism. The data-dv-controls attribute names the chart ids this legend governs; it is a CONTRACT the engine does not read yet, declared here so a dashboard composer can place a legend beside charts that are NOT its DOM siblings once the engine grows the lookup.

D · Non-colour swatches — shape and line style

Colour is never the only channel (WCAG 1.4.1). Each row keeps its series colour and adds a second, redundant one: a shape for area marks, a line style for line series. The shapes are angular — square, triangle, diamond, bar — because Badge and Avatar are the only round components in Apollo. The clip lands on the mark, never on the 44px target behind it.

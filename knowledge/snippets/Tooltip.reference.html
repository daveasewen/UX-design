Tooltip — canonical reference · icon at end of label · space-aware

The trigger sits at the end of a label (never inside body text). Hover or tab to the icon; the tip flips above/below and shifts left/right to stay on-screen. Try the ones near the top and right edges.

Available balance The funds you can spend right now, after pending transactions are deducted.
Interest rate (AER) Annual Equivalent Rate — shows the interest if it were paid and compounded once each year.
Sort code The six-digit number that identifies your bank and branch.

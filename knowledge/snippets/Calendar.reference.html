Live — a standalone month, in page flow

No field, no popover, nothing to dismiss. Tab reaches the grid once, then arrow keys move by day, Home and End go to the week's edges, Page up and Page down change the month, Shift with Page up or down changes the year, Enter or Space selects, and crossing a month edge with an arrow pages the view. Dates before today are disabled here so the disabled treatment is visible — that is a demo choice, not a rule.

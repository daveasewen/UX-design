Rates Fees Eligibility Protection

Rates

The rate shown is the annual equivalent rate and assumes interest stays in the account for a full twelve months. It is variable, so it can change after the account is opened.

Interest is calculated daily on the cleared balance and paid on the first working day of each month.

Fees

There is no monthly account fee. Charges may still apply for services outside the standard account, such as a same-day payment or a duplicate statement.

A full list of charges is set out in the account terms, which you can download at any time.

Eligibility

You need to be eighteen or over and resident in the United Kingdom. You can hold one account of this type at a time.

Opening the account will not affect your credit score, though we will run an identity check.

Protection

Eligible deposits are protected up to the current limit under the deposit guarantee scheme. The limit applies per person, per banking licence, not per account.

If you hold more than one account with us, the balances are added together when the limit is worked out.

What you get

A single view of your balances, a payment tool that remembers your regular payees, and statements going back seven years.

Everything here also works on the mobile app, with the same sign-in.

Setting it up

Most people finish in under ten minutes. You will need a form of photo identification and a recent proof of address.

You can save your progress and come back later without starting again.

Moving your payments

Standing orders and direct debits can be transferred for you. Nothing moves until you confirm the date.

Your old account stays open until the switch completes.

If something goes wrong

Report a problem from inside the app and you will get a reference straight away. Most cases are resolved within three working days.

Nothing you report here affects your ability to use the account in the meantime.

A custom 20px square bound to a real, focusable <input type=checkbox>. Tab to one and press Space. The top group shows every state; below, Select all drives three children — tick some and watch the parent flip to indeterminate live.

Radio group

atom

Real radios inside a fieldset / legend. Click one, or focus the group and use ↑ ↓ ← → to move the selection (native roving focus). The last option is disabled.

Switch

atom

A real checkbox carrying role=switch. The thumb slides, the track fills near-black when on, and the state text follows. Focus one and press Space or Enter; press and hold to feel the thumb stretch. The last one is disabled.

Segmented control

molecule

Single-select range. Click a segment or focus it and use ← → — the near-black indicator slides to your choice. Drag the Width slider below ~360px and it reflows to a stacked list.

Select

organism

A custom listbox — not a native <select>. Open with the trigger (the chevron flips), then ↑ ↓, Home, End, type to jump, Enter to choose, Esc or a click outside to close. The chosen option keeps a check.

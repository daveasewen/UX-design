Apollo DataViz Round-one kit

Round-one chart kit

4 types · gate-first

The whole round-one kit on one page: KPI stat card, bar & column, line, and donut. Semantic SVG + data/* tokens + CSS motion + a real <table> spine, all gated by _validate_dataviz.py (9 blocking + 5 advisory, both themes). Toggle Dark and High contrast top-right — the same charts rebind, no forks.

KPI stat card

kpi · metric · +delta · +spark

One number with optional delta and sparkline. Gain/loss uses the convention consciously (dv-019): sign and arrow and colour — three channels, so colour is never load-bearing alone. Deltas are data semantics, exempt from red-once-per-screen.

Column

column · single series

Vertical bars on a zero baseline (dv-bar-009). One series, one colour — the x-axis labels carry the categories, so colour is not doing redundant work. Grid + numeric y-axis from hand-rolled nice-ticks.

Horizontal bar

bar · single series

Long category labels read better horizontally. Negative values are not allowed on horizontal bars (dv-bar-007) — the gate blocks them; use a vertical column when a series can go negative.

Grouped column

grouped · 2 series

Two series side by side for direct comparison. Each series carries a letter (A, B) on the bars and in the legend, so the comparison survives without colour (§04.3). Legend items toggle series visibility.

Stacked column

stacked · 4 series

Composition over time. Segments are separated by a 2px surface-coloured stroke (dv-004) so touching fills never shimmer at the boundary. Fills are solid — the chevron texture is reserved for gauge-type charts (your call), not stacked series.

Line

line · marker styles

Markers carry the series colour as a border. Compare a solid white fill vs a background fill (adapts to the theme — shows most clearly in dark mode). All lines build together, each symbol landing as the line reaches it.

Line

line · single series

A single series over time. Straight segments between points (dv-line-011) — no curve smoothing, which would misrepresent the values between readings. Markers sit on each reading.

Multi-series line

line · 3 series

Up to five series, each with its own colour, letter (A–C) and marker shape (dv-line-002) — three redundant channels so the lines are told apart without relying on colour. Legend toggles a series in and out.

Sparkline

spark · inline trend

A wide, flat, axis-free single-series line for inline context (dv-line-009 aspect). It reads as shape, and the figures live in the table.

Donut with centre total

donut · composition · label variants

Composition, capped at six slices (dv-pie-009), summing to the stated total (dv-pie-010), separated by a 2px surface stroke (dv-004). A legend still needs the alphabetic label ON the chart — three variants to compare (switch below): letters on segments, spider-leg letters (letter on a short leader outside the ring), and direct labels (full name + value in place, no legend). Segments grow sequentially from the centre.

Every fill resolves to a data/series-* token (V7, semantic-colour.json). Gridline contrast is advisory (decorative); series-fill + axis/label contrast is blocking at ≥3:1, computed from the resolved hex in both modes. Gated by _validate_dataviz.py (step 22 of _build_all.py).

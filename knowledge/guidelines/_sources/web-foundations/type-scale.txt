Web type scale

Our type system adapts across devices and viewports to best display content and promote a clear hierarchical standard across the HSBC estate.

Sections: Scales · Responsive (Default) · Responsive (marketing) · Non-responsive · Sizes · Hierarchy · Line length

The default scale is comprised of 7 sizes and is anchored around a 16px baseline (1.000em). The 3 smaller text sizes are generally for standard text (eg body copy, links and other UI), and the 4 larger sizes are used for titles, section headers and sub headers.

Scales

The type scale has three variants called TScales, used to maintain a strong hierarchy as the viewport size increases.

TScale:1 used for all viewport sizes between 320px to 959px.
TScale:2 used for all viewport sizes between 960px to 1599px.
TScale:3 used for all viewport sizes 1600px and above.

The scales used dependant on the context. [sic]

Responsive (Default)

TScales:1-2 are used for the majority of applications at HSBC, with a maximum content width of 1280px.

Responsive (marketing)

For marketing sites with content width above 1280px, TScale:3 can be used in addition to TScales:1-2 in order to provide stronger hierarchy on 1600px+ viewports.

Non-responsive

Use TScale:1 only for non-consumer-facing projects with high content density.

Hierarchy

As well as our type scale, we use Light, Regular and Medium font weights to create hierarchy in our designs.

Titles, section headers and subtitles

Titles, section headers and subtitles use the S sizes from the scale and start from 19px and above. These S sizes can be applied as H1-H4 if semantically correct to do so.

Page titles and subtitles use our light and regular font weight.

Different subtitle sizes can help divide up sections of text.

Standard text

Font sizes 16px and below are used as our standard text sizes (body copy), with 16px being recommended for use on our public-facing websites. A medium font weight may be used to highlight specific words or phrases but avoid using it for longer paragraphs of text.

12px is reserved for terms and conditions, disclaimer text and legal copy (eg in the site footer).

Sometimes a medium weight can be used on standard text to mimic a subtitle without the need for a larger font size.

Line length

It is important to remember that a line length of 60 to 80 characters is recommended for an enhanced reading experience. Use the grid to help define suitable copy length.

Do keep line length shorter than full page width, improving legibility.
Don't make line length the full width of the page on wide layouts. This makes it much harder for users to read the content.

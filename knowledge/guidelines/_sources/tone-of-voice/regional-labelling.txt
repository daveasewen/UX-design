Regional labelling – China, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan
Labelling conventions to follow when referencing China, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan

Please note - the following guidance is written and owned by HSBC HK Legal. For further guidance, please contact HK Legal colleagues.

This is general guidance for all internal and external documents / platforms, including digital (eg, websites, mobile apps, HSBC-owned / sponsored social media platforms) and non-digital:
Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan should not be mistaken as countries / states / nations in any text, maps or graphics. Follow this overarching principle if the content in question is not covered by the following examples.
The term 'countries and territories' or 'countries and regions' should be used when Hong Kong, Macau and / or Taiwan are listed alongside other countries. Using 'markets' to indicate geographic coverage of a survey, for instance, also works.
Both terms ‘country and region’ and ‘country and territory’ are acceptable. The preferred term for new copy and content is ‘Country / region’ and ‘country and region’.
The term 'mainland China' should be used when in parallel mentioning with Hong Kong and / or Macau and / or Taiwan. Lower case ‘mainland’ is the preferred guidance when not used at the start of a sentence.
When Hong Kong and / or Macau is used in a sentence together with a list of countries that does not include China (e.g. Malaysia, Singapore, Hong Kong…) use 'Hong Kong SAR', 'Macau SAR', or firstly classify the list as a list of 'countries / regions' or 'markets' before listing the names (e.g., in the following markets: Malaysia, Singapore, Hong Kong…)
When Taiwan is used in a sentence together with a list of countries that does not include China (e.g. Malaysia, Singapore, Taiwan…) firstly classify the list as a list of 'countries / regions' or 'markets' before listing the names (e.g. in the following markets: Malaysia, Singapore, Taiwan…)
References to 'Hong Kong' or 'Hong Kong SAR / Hong Kong Special Administrative Region', and 'Macau' or 'Macau SAR / Macau Special Administration Region' are both fine.
Label country dropdown lists and any other similar references as ‘Country / region’ or ‘countries and regions’.
Avoid using pictures or emojis of all flags in any circumstance.
Note regarding T&Cs: Please consult the Asia Pacific and local legal teams if any references in T&Cs appear to contradict this guidance (including both internal and third party).

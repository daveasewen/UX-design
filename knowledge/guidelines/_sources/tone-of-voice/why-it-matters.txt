Why it matters — Supporting our brand, showing our values ? [sic] and saving time and money: why you should care about tone of voice.

== The business case for tone of voice ==

It's better for your bottom line. Investing in tone of voice saves us time and money. It makes our customers happier and likelier to recommend us. It helps our customers and colleagues understand us better, and trust us more.

You can start today, and tailor your approach — from targeted training to full rewrites to awards submission advice.

== Receipts ==

From letters to texts, to the tune of £1.2 million:
We looked at 150 letters, and realised it wasn't enough to rewrite them – we needed to look at what we were hoping to do overall. We kept it short and turned some of those letters into texts. The impact:
- **£1.2 million saved on just one project, including £381k on mailing costs alone.**
- **6x the response rate from customers.**
Tone of voice helped us look at the words, but also the structure and the medium of our communications.

Clearer letters saved us time and money:
We used to send a letter telling customers to go to a branch to pick up their debit card — branches had to store the cards securely for as long as it took customers to collect.
- **Just by changing 15 letters, we saved £89,212 from cutting postage costs.**
- Also cut calls to contact centres, improved card collection rates, less branch time handling cards.
So it wasn't just saving words – by making our message clearer, we saved our in-branch colleagues time and effort, and saved budget.

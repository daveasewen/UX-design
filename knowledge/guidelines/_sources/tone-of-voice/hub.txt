Tone of voice

Tone of voice guidance ensures we speak to our customers conversationally, just how we like being spoken to.

Key takeaways
- Don't use overly formally [sic] language and technical jargon, ensuring it's understandable, warm and human.
- Think about how to structure your copy. Put your main point first and highlight what's important.
- Don't be vague, get to the point quickly.
- Give clear signposting and solutions to whats [sic] happening next.

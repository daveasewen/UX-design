Accessibility
To be Level AA accessible, any part of an illustration that is essential to the information being conveyed must have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against adjacent colours.

Do use the brand reds to hero an object within a scene.
Don’t have illustrations without brand reds.
Do use HSBC Red as a background Hexagon or to highlight something meaningful or important within each illustration.
Don’t choose colour combinations that have too little contrast.
Don’t add new colours or tints not included in the Illustration palette.
Don’t allow the Illustration palette to dominate.

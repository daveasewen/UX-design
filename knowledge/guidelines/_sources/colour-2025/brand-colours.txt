Brand Colour palette
Our Brand Colour palette is made up of three separate palettes: Core, Complementary Red and Complementary Grey, and should be used throughout our communications. These colours are one of our signature foundations, with our HSBC Red being particularly evocative of our bold and confident brand.
Our flexible set of palettes allow for the creation of distinctive brand experiences.
Please note: red is used tactically within the digital experience for navigation, signposting and call-to-actions. For more information see the Design Toolkits.

Core palette usage
The ratio of colours is flexible depending on the format, messaging, orientation, type of Hexagon being used and where the piece of communication is going to appear.
When used in a predominantly clean white space:
reds, greys and black tones come through the imagery
When used in a full bleed image where the focus is on the black:
work the red through the Hexagon and include small hints of red within the image for balance.
Do feature our Core colour palette in all communications.
Don’t introduce colours outside of our colour palettes.
Do use combinations of our Brand Colour palette.
Don’t use black and white photography.
If you feel that you need an exception to the colour palettes, please get approval and guidance from the brand design team.

Complementary Red palette usage
The Complementary Red palette builds upon our Brand palette and it can be used together with the three colours of our Core palette and alongside our HSBC Red.
If using more than one colour from the Complementary Red palette, please make sure that there is enough of a colour contrast between them, eg Red 1 next to Red 3.
The following examples show how to use the Complementary Red palette in a variety of ways.
Here, the Hexagon in HSBC Red works with the background in Red 3 to create high contrast.
The white image balances out the reds.
In this example, the background in Red 3, with red images similar to HSBC Red, creates contrast. Hints of black break up the reds.
Here, the background in Red 3 compliments the tones of the photograph.
Do use combinations of the Complementary Red palette to create rich and vibrant environments with block colour, photography and texture.
Don’t create new Complementary Red colours.
Do use combinations of red alongside the Core palette.
Don’t use tints of the Complementary Red palette.

Do use lighter shades of grey as solid backgrounds.
Don’t use the Complementary Grey palette in isolation without any of the Brand palette colours.
Do integrate greys into our different Hexagons. The greys also work very well with our HSBC Red.
Do use greys for the background when other colours do not achieve sufficient colour contrast.
Do use either white or complimentary grey 8 for typography in digital experiences.
Don’t use any grey for typography other than complimentary grey 8.

## Provenance of the standard itself (from the overview page)

Based on 2019 research by Hassell Inclusion for the National Autistic Society:
desk research + expert interviews + 400-person survey (autistic people, with and
without learning difficulties and dyslexia, UK) + focus groups (19 autistic people).
"The first set of guidelines which are based on solid empirical evidence."
40 guidelines / 14 sections. Aids users with: autism, dyslexia, ADHD, learning
difficulties. Complements accessibility standards (many checkpoints cite WCAG
equivalents as "met as part of the accessibility criteria").

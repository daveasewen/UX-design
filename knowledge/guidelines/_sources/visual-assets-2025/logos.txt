The HSBC Hexagon
The hexagon is our most recognisable asset.
We want the hexagon to be the unmistakable, iconic mark of our brand and make it a symbol of prosperity around the world. Our new identity will allow many more Hexagon-only usages, as the brand modernises in the digital age.
The hexagon-only solution will require approval from Create Direct.

Masterbrand
Primary version
Our new Masterbrand logo prioritises the hexagon. The wordmark has been modernised with Univers Next for HSBC, making it more appropriate for the age of screens, whilst remaining a strong and confident signifier.
These two elements are combined in a fixed relationship, to form our Masterbrand logo.
This is a three-colour version, comprising a red hexagon, white infill and black HSBC logotype. It should be used wherever possible. It is used on white and light backgrounds.

For print work CMYK files
.eps – Encapsulated PostScript
EPS is a vector format that has been designed to produce high-resolution graphics for print. EPS vector files are also scalable to any size.
.tiff – Tagged Image File Format
TIF is a large raster file that doesn’t lose quality; it is usually used when saving photographs for print.
For digital / onscreen work RGB files
.png –Portable Network Graphics
PNGs are not suitable for print. While PNGs are “lossless”, meaning you can edit them and not lose quality, they are still low resolution. The reason PNGs are used in most web projects is that you can save your image with more colors on a transparent background. This makes for a much sharper, web-quality image.
.svg – Scalable Vector Graphics
SVG is an XML-based vector image format for two-dimensional graphics with support for interactivity and animation. SVG images and their behaviors are defined in XML text files. This means that they can be searched, indexed, scripted, and compressed.

Protected area and minimum size
Clear space rules
To ensure visibility, the Masterbrand logo should have a surrounding area clear of any other graphics or text elements. This is referred to as the protected area.
This protected area is highlighted in blue and is based on the height of the hexagon. This height is the required minimum area.
Minimum size
The Masterbrand logo must be clearly readable and reproduced consistently. For this reason, a minimum size has been set based on a minimum height of the hexagon of 7mm. The minimum sizes for the Masterbrand will affect minimum sizes for all brand, sub-brands etc, across the business.
Dimensions
105px (w) x 20px (h)
26mm (w) x 7mm (h)
Please contact Create Direct if you want to use the logo smaller that the recommended minimum size.
See smaller uses of the Hexagon in digital touchpoints such as favicons and app tiles, in the Common Toolkit.

Positioning
General
Ideally, the HSBC logo should be positioned in either the top-left or bottom-left corners, whenever possible. If not possible, eg on third-party collateral, care must be taken to ensure the protected area and minimum sizes rules are followed.
There are exceptions, eg. opening / end frames for digital communications and videos.
Depending on the media, the logo can be adjusted to the eye-line of the audience: eg. in out-of-home advertising. where the logo should not be positioned by people’s ankles. Here it can be top-left of mid-level, according to need.

Masthead logo behavior
Clicking on the brand logo in the top left of the masthead will always direct you to the root level of the business line you are currently viewing.
Users who have navigated to a specific page within a business line will have done so voluntarily, either through search or via the masthead. Returning users to the home page of the business line will help orientate them and avoids potentially confusing users by transferring them to a completely different part of the site. Users can use the masthead to navigate to other business lines if they wish.

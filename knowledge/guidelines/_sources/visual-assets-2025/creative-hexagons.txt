Types of Hexagon
As the HSBC Hexagon is our single most recognisable asset, it must be central to all our communications. It is the core DNA of our visual identity.
Our purpose is driven by ‘opening up opportunities’ and the variety of ways we use our Hexagon asset will demonstrate broad-ranging opportunities in compelling ways.
We have three distinctive Hexagons:
Iconic
Open
Cropped
Iconic Hexagon
This is our unedited, timeless and 'classic' Hexagon that reaches out to our audience in a creative way. It's bold, confident and always HSBC Red.
More about the Iconic Hexagon
Open Hexagon
A deconstructed Hexagon that offers a simple but powerful canvas to work with. It can act as a window, framing our content within our creative communications.
More about the Open Hexagon
Cropped Hexagon
This is an abstract Hexagon that provides a bold canvas. It frames assets (such as imagery, typography and illustration) while distinctly referencing the HSBC brand.
More about the Cropped Hexagon

Usage within the Brand Effect Model
The Brand Effect Model (BEM) is a visualisation system that breaks down the customer journey into points of communication. We can use the BEM to plot the best points at which to use each Hexagon type.
For example, our Iconic Hexagon is the most distinctive and should always be used at ‘prime’ level, where we engage with our audience in areas that are outside the immediate HSBC environment – for example, an airport advertising campaign.
When we have customers who are on the next stage of their journey with us, and within an immediate HSBC environment, such as in branch, we can afford to be slightly more abstract and use the Open or the Cropped Hexagons.
Put simply, the Iconic Hexagon can be used at every stage of the journey, as illustrated within the BEM, whereas the Open Hexagon and the Cropped Hexagon should only be used when switching from 'engage' to 'convert' touchpoints.
Do use an HSBC logo alongside our Creative Hexagons within a user journey.
Don’t obscure the Hexagon or make them hard to see.

Graphic treatments
Graphic treatments refer to techniques applied to Iconic, Open and Cropped Hexagons to add visual interest when photography or illustration isn't sufficient, offering greater creative freedom while preserving our brand recognition.

Mnemonic
Our core sound element.

The HSBC brand brings sound and motion together into one powerful combination for a richer brand experience. The five-second master HSBC mnemonic is the core sound element. Each of its five notes is played at a specific moment during the brand logo animation sequence, enhancing our motion design.
The HSBC logo animation uses four basic transition effects to reveal the hexagon – our brand’s hero.
More about our brand video guidelines

The five-second masterbrand HSBC mnemonic is our primary, preferred version of our sound signature. It embodies and celebrates the energy and spirit behind our brand personality. It's perfect for closing interactive visual messages and can also be used to emphasise actions or short events.
In exceptional cases, there are three- and two-second mnemonic versions available. They have been specially composed to accommodate short or non-standard media formats.

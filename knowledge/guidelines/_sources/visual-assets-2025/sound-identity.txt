Sound identity
Using sound to build upon our brand's distinctive assets.

In the modern world, visuals tell only half a brand's story. Sound is just as important as all the other senses, perhaps even more so. It’s an untapped means of building the brand’s distinctive assets and fame. It can add a human touch and emotion to help consumers understand the brand’s personality, as well as feel more connected to it.
A brand's sound identity can be more recognisable than logos or spokespeople. The power of music and sound transcends language, culture and all things visual, which makes sound branding a vital part of building recognition.

Sonic brand identity
The sound of HSBC
Our ambition for the sound identity is for it to be instantly recognisable and spontaneously associated with HSBC.
In situations where visuals are not available, the sound is what will identify HSBC to customers, clients, colleagues and members of the public.
The sound branding is inspired by:
Our brand personality
The feeling of a brand that is dynamic and growing
Our visual identity
The sound of HSBC consists of seven tracks and a sound signature.
Tracks
We created a bespoke musical piece, composed of seven different tracks – each reflecting different moments and mind sets linked to consumer touch points. The music has been composed to be locally relevant to all the markets in which the bank is present, by transcending language and culture.
Mnemonic
The sound signature or 'mnemonic' of HSBC is directly inspired by the main musical theme and is composed of five notes that are the sound equivalent of our visual logo. The mnemonic is used alongside the end frame animation on all video content.

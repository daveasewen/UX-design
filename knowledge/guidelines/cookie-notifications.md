# Cookie notifications

Cookie notifications are pop-up messages shown the first time a user visits a website that uses cookies, or whenever the cookies change and the user must re-consent.

## Cookie types
- **Strictly necessary cookies** — required for the website to function; always listed and explained in the Cookie Policy notice. (Always on; do not require opt-in consent.)
- **Optional cookies** — discretionary features that improve the websites and support advertising and social media. **They require user consent.** Always listed and explained in the Cookie Policy notice, and **always accompanied by controls that let the customer turn them on and off.** Optional cookies might:
  - make the visit more personal through optional features/services (the site still functions without them);
  - help improve the website via insights and data on how people use it;
  - support marketing by identifying what customers may be interested in (based on browsing of the website and social media), and which other sites directed customers here.

## Principles
- The notification must let users **consent or manage** their choices; optional cookies are off until the user opts in, with clear on/off controls.
- Content is paired with the Cookie Policy notice for full detail.
- The page includes do/don'ts for the notification's content and presentation (e.g. clear language, accessible controls) — REVIEW for exact wording if needed.

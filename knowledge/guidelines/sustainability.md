# Sustainability & the transition to net zero

> Source: create.hsbc brandhub (authenticated) — `foundations-and-identity/sustainability-and-the-transition-to-net-zero.html`. Captured 2026-06-18 for RAG. Summarised, not verbatim.

## Ambition

HSBC's ambition: **become a net zero bank by 2050** — by reducing its own emissions and **supporting customers to diversify, decarbonise and realise the economic opportunities** of the transition. Communications demonstrate how HSBC enables emissions reductions in the wider economy and provides financing that facilitates customers' transition plans.

## The governing document

The **Net Zero Transition Plan** (on hsbc.com) is the **go-to document** for the net zero strategy and implementation. **All communications and marketing outputs must align to its principles and language.**

## Key theme: cinematic *and* compliant

Sustainability/climate communications carry **regulatory and greenwashing risk**, so the guidance pairs creative quality with compliance:
- **Sustainability communications playbook** — communicate effectively while managing risk appropriately.
- **Visualising our climate ambitions** — how to be **both cinematic and compliant** in content.
- **Climate message house** — supporting customers on their transition to lower emissions (messaging framework).
- **Regulatory guidance** — communicating on climate/sustainability **compliantly**.

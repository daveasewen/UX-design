# Contextual help

Contextual help ensures the user has all the information they need to complete their task. Two platform treatments are documented: **browser** and **mobile app**.

## When it's needed
Reduce on-page information wherever possible, but provide additional information or clarification when it's essential to helping users achieve their goals — for example:

- Clarifying how the information the user is submitting will be used
- Ensuring users have the right documents in front of them
- Showing how the user's input should be formatted
- Managing expectations around application processing times
- Providing answers to frequently-asked questions
- Explaining the meaning of a specific label or term

This need is defined through good knowledge of the user, the pain points they encounter, and their understanding of the concepts in the process they're being asked to complete.

## Delivery
Contextual help is presented in a number of ways depending on the content, and differs by platform (browser vs mobile app). The page illustrates the methods via examples under a **Usage** section (e.g. inline help, info triggers, expandable help, overlays) — method specifics are shown as worked examples rather than spelled out as tokens.

> Platform split matters: the browser and mobile-app treatments are documented separately. When building, pick the variant matching the target platform.

## Purpose & positioning

- **Purpose:** *"Open up a world of opportunity"* for those we serve.
- **Brand strategy goal:** use HSBC's competitive advantage to be **more famous and create real value across the network** — reclaiming a position in the **top three global banking brands**.
- **Brand mindset / audience:** connecting with the **"Internationalists"** — HSBC is a uniquely **international** brand (a local bank serving international needs).

## Creative direction — the six design principles

HSBC's brand design is **angular, tactically red, clearly understood, internationally relatable, logical, and creatively considered.** These aid consistency and make the brand distinctive yet recognisable.

1. **Angular** — derived from the **90° and 45° angles of the Hexagon** and the **22° angle from the typography**. Geometry from the brand assets carries through into layout and composition.
2. **Tactically red** — HSBC is a **red brand** and the Hexagon is red, but red is used **tactically, not overtly**: as a considered accent for impact, not slathered everywhere. (Reinforces the token-level rule that brand red is an accent — never a page background.)
3. **Clearly understood** — all collateral should be **easily understood**: simple ideas, **singular focal points**, clear typography. Clarity over decoration.
4. **Internationally relatable** — assets should **accurately and diversely reflect** customers' and colleagues' real lives across markets.
5. **Logical** — many design decisions and processes are **rooted in logic**, functioning to make things easy and consistent.
6. **Creatively considered** — every aspect is **crafted and considered thoughtfully** to create beautiful, memorable experiences.

## How these map to the system

- **Tactically red** → the `color/primary` (#DB0011) accent-only rule, and the primitive-leak flag (Badge/Hero/Links/Tabs binding the red primitive — should be governed semantic accent usage). See `tokens/_manifests/_DESIGN-SYSTEM-GAPS.md` P5.
- **Angular** → Hexagon masks + the 22° type angle (see `guidelines/hexagon-masks.md`).
- **Clearly understood / Logical** → supports the component anti-patterns around clarity (single focal point, clear hierarchy, no decoration-for-decoration's-sake).

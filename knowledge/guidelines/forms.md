# Forms

Forms play a crucial role for HSBC's products and services — widely used across channels, so it's critical they're designed consistently and correctly. (Documented for paper and digital.)

## Design principles
A foundation for any form, to give customers, employees and partners a consistent experience across the HSBC ecosystem:

- **Prioritise readability over aesthetics** — use universal, familiar design patterns regardless of ability; aesthetic choices must never come at the expense of understanding or use.
- **Only ask for what you need** — value people's time; honed, tailored content keeps forms simple and aids success.
- **Guide people** — give confidence by guiding through the process with as much (or as little) help as needed, always moving toward the goal.
- **Designing for the format** — design for the intended format (paper vs digital); don't copy one format into another.

# Calls to action (CTAs)

> Source: create.hsbc brandhub (authenticated) — `foundations-and-identity/calls-to-action--ctas-.html`. Captured 2026-06-18 for RAG. Summarised, not verbatim. Marketing-CTA framework (complements component-level button/link labels in the component metas + UX-copy rules).

## Overview

CTAs are often crucial to a campaign but frequently considered too late. HSBC's **CTA framework** broadens thinking on CTA application for more effective, targeted results — moving beyond traditional CTAs to account for **customer mindset, channel, and business outcomes** (driven by personalisation expectations).

## Key concepts

- **Prime vs convert-led CTAs** — a core distinction: *prime* CTAs build awareness/consideration earlier in the journey; *convert-led* CTAs drive the conversion action. Choose by where the customer is.
- **CTAs across the Brand Effect Model** — apply different CTA types at different stages.
- **Online + offline** — CTAs are used effectively across both media types.
- **Measure effectiveness** — live-learn from the metrics of an active campaign and optimise.

## Structure of the guidance (sub-pages, pull on demand)
- **The role of CTAs** — what they are and why we use them.
- **Optimising CTAs** — the foundations of an optimal CTA.
- **Planning and implementing CTAs** — planning, crafting, executing, and live-learning from metrics.
- **Resources and key considerations** — CTA **do's and don'ts**, key takeaways, further reading.

## How this maps to the system
- Complements the **component layer**: Button (primary/secondary/tertiary/quaternary), Links (arrow/inline), and Hero CTA — this is the *strategy/wording* layer above those.

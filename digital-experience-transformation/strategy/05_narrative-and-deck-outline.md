# Leadership narrative & deck outline

*DRAFT v0.1 for review. The story arc for the pitch, slide by slide, plus the ask.*

---

## Executive summary

The pitch follows a simple, hard-to-argue arc: **the shift is real → the easy responses fail → value has moved → we've already built the engine → here's the new team, the plan, and the honest cost → here's the upside → here's what I need from you.** It opens from strength (evidence, not opinion), turns the corner early (we are not reacting to AI, we are ahead of it), and lands on a specific ask. Roughly **11 slides**, ~20 minutes, exec-summary-first so a busy room gets the whole story from the first two slides.

## The arc in one breath
> Design execution just became nearly free. Doing the old job faster is a trap. The value has moved to judgment, criteria and governance — and we have spent the last months building the exact machine that operationalises that move. Fund us to run the team on it, handle the people transition properly, and we become the team that owns experience *and* the infrastructure that guarantees it.

## Slide-by-slide

1. **The 20-second version.** One slide that states the whole case: the shift, the move up the value chain, "we've already built the engine," and the ask. (Respect the room's time; everything after is the backup.)

2. **The shift is already here.** The evidence: ~91% of designers use AI, most daily; ~half ship AI-generated code to production; teams ship 40–60% faster; the role is moving "from executor to orchestrator." Framing: *the artifact is no longer scarce.*

3. **The fork in the road.** Three doors: resist (lose on economics), shrink-only (a smaller obsolete team — the booby prize), transform (move up the value chain). We are choosing door three.

4. **Where the value went.** When execution is free, scarcity moves to judgment, criteria and governance — all human-led, all rising as AI improves. Everything else falls toward zero.

5. **Delivery → Experience.** Why the rename encodes the strategy: from owning *output/throughput* to owning *outcomes* and the *infrastructure that guarantees quality at scale*. Cross-disciplinary by design.

6. **We've already built the engine.** The Smart Design System. Its principles *are* the new operating model: craft scored / taste judged; automate around the taste call; criteria-as-checks; gated canon. *This is the slide that flips the story from defensive to offensive.* Show the working proof on the lighthouse journey.

7. **The new operating model.** The three layers — Machine, Standards, Humans. What the team stops doing; what it starts doing. The new metrics that replace "artifacts shipped."

8. **The new team.** The from→to role map. Smaller, more senior, with a new engineering spine. The capabilities we net-gain.

9. **The plan.** Prove → Encode → Shift → Scale, each gated by proof, run in parallel, tied to a metric. Emphasise: *capability gates the people change, never the reverse.*

10. **The honest part.** The people transition: production roles reduce; here is the conversion map, the protected capabilities, the reskilling runway, and the humane process for exits. Say it straight — it builds credibility for everything else.

11. **The upside and the ask.** New value a delivery team could never offer (compliance-at-scale; the parked contextual-dashboard vehicle, flagged as horizon-3). Then the specific ask (below).

## The ask (make it concrete)
- **Mandate** to transform — not just permission to use AI tools, but a remit to re-charter the team around the new model.
- **A protected lighthouse** — one product area / journey to run the new model end-to-end, shielded from BAU pressure.
- **Investment in the spine** — funding for the design-engineering capability and the reskilling runway.
- **Time and air cover** — the parallel-running period in Phase 2, and patience measured against decision gates rather than the calendar.
- **A named executive sponsor** — someone senior who owns the transformation outcome with you.

## Delivery notes
- **Open and close on the ask;** keep slide 1 and slide 11 tight.
- **Lead with evidence, not vision** — the room trusts the proof slide (6) far more than the trend slides (2).
- **Don't oversell the upside.** The horizon-3 material is a *bonus*, explicitly flagged; the core case stands without it.
- **Bring the proof, not just the deck** — a live or recorded walk of the Smart Design System on the lighthouse journey is worth more than any slide.

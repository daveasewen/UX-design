# Phased roadmap — prove → encode → shift → scale

*DRAFT v0.1 for review. Timelines are indicative and tied to the Smart Design System's maturity, not the calendar.*

---

## Executive summary

The transformation runs in four phases, deliberately paced to the maturity of the Smart Design System so the team change is always **backed by working proof, never by promise**. **Prove** (now) establishes that the engine works on one real journey and gathers the internal evidence. **Encode** (≈0–6 months) turns the team's standards into the system and seeds the new roles from existing people. **Shift** (≈6–12 months) flips the operating model on a first product area and runs old and new in parallel — this is where role changes and redundancies become real and must be handled deliberately. **Scale** (≈12–18 months) rolls the model across the portfolio, formalises the Digital Experience identity, and opens the new-value story. The golden rule: **never restructure ahead of proven capability.** If the proof slips, the people changes slip with it.

## Phase 0 — Prove *(now → next ~3 months)*
**Goal:** demonstrate the loop end-to-end on one real banking journey (the payments work already underway). Generate undeniable internal evidence that artifact cost has collapsed and quality has held.
- **Capability:** one journey taken brief → criteria-bound spec → generated, gate-verified prototype → handoff. Gated canon proven on real components.
- **People:** no restructure yet. Quietly identify the individuals who could grow into canon steward, experience engineer, governance lead.
- **Metric:** time-to-trustworthy-prototype on the lighthouse journey vs the old hand-built baseline; compliance findings caught automatically.
- **Decision gate → Phase 1:** does the proof hold on a real journey, with quality at or above the manual baseline? If no, iterate here — do **not** advance the team change.

## Phase 1 — Encode *(≈0–6 months)*
**Goal:** turn the team's standards into the system, and seed the new roles from existing people.
- **Capability:** expand the gated canon to cover the lighthouse area; author the first real criteria-as-checks with BA/PO; harden the gates; stand up the engineering spine.
- **People:** begin reskilling. Name (provisionally) the canon steward, the experience engineer, the governance/DesignOps lead from within. Start measuring the team on outcomes and governed-quality, not artifact counts.
- **Metric:** % of the lighthouse area covered by certified canon; first criteria authored; reskilling plan agreed per person.
- **Decision gate → Phase 2:** is enough of the area covered by canon + gates that a real workflow could run on the new model? Is the reskilling map credible?

## Phase 2 — Shift *(≈6–12 months)*
**Goal:** make the governed system the **default** path for one product area; redirect human hours to criteria, research and judgment. Run old and new in parallel and measure.
- **Capability:** generation-from-canon + gates is the standard way work happens in the pilot area; humans operate at the Standards and Human layers, not the Machine layer.
- **People:** **this is where role changes and redundancies become real.** Convert what converts; be honest and supported about what doesn't (see `04_people-transition.md`). Protect research and judgment capacity — the classic mistake is cutting those to keep production.
- **Metric:** share of the pilot area's UI shipped via the new model; reuse rate; outcomes vs the parallel old-model control; team-hours redistributed toward research/criteria/governance.
- **Decision gate → Phase 3:** does the new model match or beat the old on quality and outcome while freeing senior time? Are the people changes handled well enough to scale with trust intact?

## Phase 3 — Scale & re-charter *(≈12–18 months)*
**Goal:** roll the model across the portfolio, formalise the **Digital Experience** team, and open the new-value story.
- **Capability:** the harness becomes shared infrastructure other teams build on; canon and gates cover the major journeys; the compliance bar is enforced continuously and auditably across products.
- **People:** the Digital Experience charter, role definitions and metrics are formalised. The team is publicly the owner of experience *and* the infrastructure that guarantees it.
- **New value:** begin the credible upside — compliance-at-scale as an organisational service, and the parked **contextual dashboard / run-time** vehicle explored as a growth bet (flagged honestly as horizon-3, not imminent).
- **Metric:** portfolio coverage; other teams consuming the infrastructure; governed-quality rate across products; first new-value pilot scoped.

## The discipline that holds it together
- **Capability gates the people change, never the reverse.** Each phase advances only on proof from the last.
- **Run old and new in parallel** through Shift, so the comparison is evidenced, not asserted.
- **Protect the scarce capabilities** (research, judgment, design-engineering) at every cut — they are the destination, not the savings.
- **Tie every phase to a metric and a decision gate**, so leadership sees a controlled transformation, not a leap of faith.

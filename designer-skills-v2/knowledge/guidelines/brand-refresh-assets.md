## Logos (2026)

- **An HSBC logo appears at least once on every piece of communication or customer
  journey.** [BLOCKING-derivable at journey/screen level — a composition gate candidate
  for the journey tranche; the payments-journey proof is where this bites first]
  {#logo26-001}
- **Originals only** (Global Brand Design supplied); never recreate/edit; always in
  entirety; never alter sizing relationship or positioning; must remain legible; TMLA
  process for third parties. [IN FORCE by discipline — the icon-source rule's logo
  analogue: never draw a logo, use the asset] {#logo26-002}
- **Masterbrand variants + selection rules:** full colour (red/black/white — LIGHT
  backgrounds) · full colour negative (red/white — DARK backgrounds) · single-colour
  variants (legibility-driven; NO white infill) · mono reversed (print only, dark/red) ·
  mono black (print only, light; never on HSBC Red). Never full-colour on a red
  background (hexagon disappears). [component-relevant — Headers carries the logo; dark
  theme should use full colour negative. Receipt: webf-008's 2021 phrasing ("alternative
  Masterbrand + white wordmark") = this variant, name evolved] {#logo26-003}
- **Regional/bilingual versions** (Trad/Simp Chinese ± English) follow identical
  sizing/positioning rules; region-text lockups restricted to legal-requirement cases.
  [reference] {#logo26-004}
- **Proposition logos** (Asset Management, Private Bank, Life, Innovation Banking) —
  never create one outside Group Brand agreement. **Identifiers** signpost products/
  departments/programmes in support of the Masterbrand. [structure] {#logo26-005}
- **Brand Promise "opening up a world of opportunity" is MANDATORY at Prime and Engage
  stages** (lock-ups with Masterbrand and proposition logos). [composition-level rule —
  marketing stages, not product UI; relevant only if generation ever targets
  Prime/Engage surfaces] {#logo26-006}
- **Partnerships:** hexagon alone where HSBC is well known; full Masterbrand where less
  known. End frames (logo + Brand Promise + sonic) close video content. [reference]
  {#logo26-007}

## Photography (2026)

- **Style: cinematic, immersive, human, authentic** — frame stories; human perspective
  required, people optional; muted, sophisticated tones (never too vibrant); red appears
  tactically and NATURALLY (warmth in lens/scene, not forced accents); lead with
  photography as the principal creative medium. [TASTE — register vocabulary for
  imagery-bearing compositions] {#photo26-001}
- **HARD bans: no Generative AI, mixed media or CGI-rendered elements in imagery; no
  montages/composites; no staged/posed/clichéd shots; no text as a mask.** ⚠ The gen-AI
  ban is PIPELINE-CRITICAL: a generation engine must never fabricate photographic
  imagery — photography comes from the Global Image Library/approved sources only.
  Direct kin of the gen-AI governance regime (gai-*); Apollo's imagery story =
  retrieval from licensed libraries, full stop. [BLOCKING-derivable at asset-provenance
  level — generated experiences may only reference library assets; pairs with the
  icon-source rule] {#photo26-002}
- **Shot vocabulary:** cinematic · people (real moments; diversity; disability shown
  positively; natural warmth in skin tones) · landscapes/cityscapes (lived-in viewpoints,
  not landmark clichés) · objects (lower-funnel, curated, product-linked) · textural
  (macro) · abstract (mood, photography-based). [TASTE — briefing vocabulary for image
  selection at composition time] {#photo26-003}
- **Sourcing ladder: Global Image Library 2026 first** (pre-paid, perpetual, any
  platform/channel) → Getty (agreement in place) → commissioned shoots (Untold Fable,
  La Plage). [structure — the engine's legal image sources, in order] {#photo26-004}

## Creative Hexagons (2026)

- **Three types:** Iconic (classic, unedited) · Open (deconstructed frame/window) ·
  Cropped (abstract bold canvas for imagery/type/illustration). Hexagons anchor the
  viewer; NEVER replace the logo; the shape must stay recognisable (no over-obscuring
  or distorting). [composition vocabulary — fenced to the composition/journey strand]
  {#hex26-001}
- **Two REFRESH DELTAS with asset impact:** (a) **the Iconic Hexagon is now used at a
  smaller size than previously**; (b) **the Cropped Hexagon is reduced to one-edge and
  two-edge crops — three- and four-edge crops are RETIRED.** ⚠ Any 2025-era hexagon
  assets/masks in the repo need a vintage check before reuse (`hexagon-masks.md` is
  legacy-era). [REVIEW — audit hexagon assets at the composition strand's first touch;
  don't reuse 3-/4-edge crops for refresh work] {#hex26-002}
- **Funnel logic:** Iconic leads at the start of the journey (airports/OOH always
  Iconic); further along, Open/Cropped take over (branches prefer Open/Cropped to avoid
  hexagon overload; social/email/Zoom = any, per use case; card category determines
  Iconic vs Open). [composition/journey vocabulary] {#hex26-003}
- **Hexagon-within-hexagon** where brand equity is low: Iconic may sit inside Open/
  Cropped visuals — never replacing the Masterbrand logo requirement (logo26-001).
  [composition vocabulary] {#hex26-004}
- **Graphic treatments (3D Hexagon, Graphic Hexagons, Keyline Hexagon…): guidance "will
  be released soon."** ⚠ Source gap, same class as the grey palette (col26-001) —
  recheck cadence. [REVIEW — source gap; no treatments until published] {#hex26-005}
- **Hexagons in motion: opening and advancing types only** (of the motion equation's
  three — Emulating not offered for hexagons). [receipt → motion-standards.md structure]
  {#hex26-006}

## Findings

   refresh set** — it draws the line between generating EXPERIENCES (our business) and
   generating ASSETS (banned for photography). Alongside gai-008 it defines Apollo's
   imagery posture: library retrieval + provenance, never synthesis. Worth a line in the
   fixed/flex charter's fixed column when next touched. [REVIEW — charter annotation at
   next touch] {#bra26-001}
2. **Logo-per-journey (logo26-001) is a free journey-gate check** — the payments-journey
   walk already surfaced Headers as a gap; when Headers lands, the logo rule rides along.
   [structure note] {#bra26-002}

   upgrade job is now scoped to "verify against 2025 pages if 2025-standard work ever
   needs them" (the refresh is the forward direction). [structure note] {#bra26-003}

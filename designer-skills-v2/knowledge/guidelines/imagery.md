## Photography (2026 refresh)

**Role & feel:** photography is HSBC's **principal visual communication medium** — used to tell **authentic human stories**. It should **frame stories with a keen eye and portray the world cinematically**: immersive, human, and **above all authentic**; **curated rather than generic**, leaving a lasting impression.

### Key takeaways (rules)
- **Red is used tactically** throughout — warmth in the lens, appearing naturally in the scene.
- You **don't need to show people directly**, but every image should be **captured from a human perspective**.
- **Always lead with photography** as the principal creative medium.
- **Don't** use overtly staged, posed or clichéd photography.
- **Don't use Generative AI, mixed media or CGI-rendered elements** in imagery. *(Notable hard rule.)*

### Do / Don't
- *Do* use photography to capture a moment/scene that supports the narrative.
- *Don't* use staged/posed shots — it should look naturally captured and authentic.
- *Don't* use photography that's too vibrant — the palette is **muted and sophisticated**.
- *Don't* use mixed media, CGI or overlays; *don't* use montages/composite images (more than one shot); *don't* use text as a mask over images (hurts legibility).

### Types of cinematic shot
- **Cinematic** — elevate the everyday into carefully crafted frames; distinctive presence.
- **People** — relatable, grounded, emotionally resonant: capture **real people in genuine moments** (no artificial poses); portray **diverse backgrounds, ages, cultures** (reflecting HSBC's value of difference); let **natural warmth** come through skin tones and ambient light.
- **Landscapes & cityscapes** — shift from generic landmark shots to **human, cinematic perspectives**; wide immersive framing; authentic (un-retouched) environments; textural/architectural detail.
- **Objects** (lower funnel) — curated, storytelling objects with context; cinematic natural lighting; material/texture for a premium look.
- **Textural** — macro/abstract textures; immersive close-ups; authenticity (no artificial enhancement).
- **Abstract** — evokes emotion/atmosphere; sets mood; ambient and non-distracting; always based off real photography.

### Cultural relevancy
Style adapts per region: **Europe & North America; Middle East, North Africa & Türkiye; Eastern Asia; Southern Asia; South America** — capturing authentic local moments/diverse stories.

### Usage
- **Marketing** — cinematic style differentiates from competitors (see *We're There* campaign).
- **Internal comms** — elevates internal messaging; engaging, relatable, brand-aligned.
- **Digital channels** — cinematic photography creates a consistent look across the digital ecosystem.

### Sourcing
- **Global Image Library 2026** — pre-paid, globally-licensed-in-perpetuity, diverse imagery (`assets/collections/global_image_library2026.html`).
- **Stock:** Getty Images (royalty-free photo + stock video; lightbox/research service).
- **Shoots / spec shoots:** Untold Fable, La Plage.

## How this maps to the system
- **No Gen-AI / CGI / mixed-media; authentic, un-composited** — a brand-integrity rule for any generated or sourced imagery in the pipeline.
- **Muted, sophisticated palette + tactical red** — aligns with the colour-usage "tactically red" rule and the cinematic feel.
- **Diversity & inclusion in people shots** + complementary illustration skin/hair tones — ties to the accessibility/inclusive-design layer.
- **Don't use text as an image mask** — complements the Hero rule (text over media needs a contrast panel, not masking).

# Report a problem

Copy this, fill it in, send it. Four boxes. Do not diagnose anything — that is our job.

The one thing worth knowing before you write: most reports that read as *"the design system
got it wrong"* turn out to be work that was never built on the design system at all. The
assistant started with no rules loaded, improvised, and nobody said so. Boxes 2 and 3 are
what separate the two, which is why they are here and why they are short.

---

**1. What happened.**

> What you asked for, and what you got instead. One or two sentences is plenty.

**2. What lane was declared.**

> Look at the assistant's *first* reply in that conversation. Did it say **on-canon** or
> **freestyle**? Paste the line. If it said neither, write **"nothing declared"** — that is
> the single most useful answer in this whole template.

**3. What the brief says.**

> Paste the brief for this task from `briefs/`, whole. It is short. If there is no brief,
> write **"no brief"** — also useful, also common.

**4. A screenshot.**

> Of the thing that looks wrong. Easier than describing it, and better.

---

*Optional, only if you happen to have it: which theme you were in, and anything the
assistant refused to do.*

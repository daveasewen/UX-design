# Payments journey — stakeholder questionnaire
*Short async survey for the *spread* of people with an opinion (not the one colleague — that's the interview).*
*Purpose: turn "everyone has a view" into data, and surface where success was never agreed. ~5 min to complete.*
*Doubles as a prototype of the questionnaire-led intake mode — note which questions produce signal vs noise.*

**Design intent:** divergence in the answers IS the finding. If people give different answers to Q3 and Q4, success was never defined. Keep it to ~5 min or response rate dies.

---

## Intro line (put at the top of the form)
> "5 minutes, no wrong answers. I'm mapping how we're all thinking about the payments journey so we can spot where we're not aligned. Be candid — divergence is exactly what's useful."

## The questions

**1. Your relationship to the payments journey.**
☐ Product / BR  ☐ Design  ☐ Engineering  ☐ Leadership/sponsor  ☐ Ops/support  ☐ Other: ___

**2. In one sentence: what is the payments journey meant to achieve?** *(open)*
> *Reads: spread here = no shared goal.*

**3. Who is it primarily for?**
☐ New customers  ☐ Existing customers  ☐ Internal/ops  ☐ A specific segment: ___  ☐ Not sure
> *Reads: disagreement = unclear target user.*

**4. What does "success in 6 months" look like to you?** *(open, one line)*
> *Reads: compare against Q2 — and against each other. Divergence = missing success criteria.*

**5. How confident are you that we actually know what users need here?**
1 — guessing  ·  2  ·  3  ·  4  ·  5 — strong evidence
> *Reads: low + clustered = shared awareness of the gap; low + scattered = some think they know and don't.*

**6. What are decisions mostly based on today?** *(tick all that apply)*
☐ User research  ☐ Analytics/usage data  ☐ Support tickets  ☐ Competitor parity  ☐ Regulatory requirement  ☐ Instinct / experience
> *Reads: heavy "instinct", thin everything else = the core problem, quantified.*

**7. What's the single biggest risk to this going well?** *(open)*
> *Reads: cluster the answers — recurring risks become candidate gates.*

**8. What are we assuming is true that we haven't verified?** *(open)*
> *Reads: direct riskiest-assumptions input.*

**9. Where do you most disagree with how this is being approached?** *(open)*
> *Reads: names the fault lines safely; pairs with interview §B.*

**10. If you had to pick one to protect, which matters most right now?**
☐ Speed to ship  ☐ Rigor / getting it right  ☐ Scope / feature completeness
> *Reads: spread = no agreed priority, which is why it feels slow and contested.*

---

## How to read the results (after responses come in)
- **Q2 + Q4 disagreement** → success was never defined. This is the headline finding.
- **Q6 dominated by "instinct"** → quantifies the "no discovery, just vibes" claim.
- **Q5 low scores** → shared or hidden evidence gap.
- **Q7 + Q8 clusters** → your riskiest-assumptions list and candidate gates.
- **Q9 + Q10 spread** → the unresolved priority conflict driving the slowness.

*Together with the interview, this gives you breadth (where everyone stands) + depth (what's really going on) — the two inputs for the payments retrofit gap report.*

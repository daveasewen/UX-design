## ② WHAT THE GAP ACTUALLY WAS — the two chart families the generator could not reach

The value half of `s212-D1` was already implemented. What was **not** enacted is that in **two** chart
families the value was a **hand-typed literal outside the generator's reach**:

**Probe (quoted, the emitting lines):**

- `knowledge/snippets/Chart-bullet.reference.html:90` — `--data-control-label-disabled:#9D9D9D; --data-control-swatch-off:currentColor;`
- `knowledge/snippets/Chart-bullet.reference.html:104` — `--data-control-label-disabled:#808080; --data-control-swatch-off:currentColor;`
- `knowledge/snippets/Chart-candlestick.reference.html:85` / `:94` — the same pair.

…and **neither file's `#token-manifest` bound `--data-control-label-disabled` to a token path**, while
the other twelve chart snippets did (e.g. `Chart-boxplot.reference.html:302`
`"--data-control-label-disabled": "data/control/label-disabled/color"`).

**The consequence, measured in the generated artefact.** `gen_snippet_tokens.project_canon` rebases a
manifest-bound literal onto its canonical var; an unbound one it cannot see. So `canon.css` shipped:

| chart scope | light declaration in `canon.css` |
|---|---|
| `.cn-chart-bar` and 9 others | `--data-control-label-disabled: var(--data-control-label-disabled-color);` |
| `.cn-chart-bullet` · `.cn-chart-candlestick` | `--data-control-label-disabled: #9D9D9D;` ← **literal, theme-deaf** |

And because `gen_theme_cascade.py`'s component tier re-projects **manifest** vars only, those two scopes
received **no `[data-apollo-theme="supercharge"]` override at all** — Dave's ruled recessive step was
silently pinned to Mono's hex in two of fourteen chart families. **This is the fixture-hardcoding class,
and it is exactly what `s212-D1`'s "lands in the GENERATOR, never a hand-patch" clause is aimed at.**

### The fix — in the generator's INPUT, not in any value

Two lines added, both to a `#token-manifest`, no colour typed anywhere:

- `knowledge/snippets/Chart-bullet.reference.html` — `"--data-control-label-disabled": "data/control/label-disabled/color",`
- `knowledge/snippets/Chart-candlestick.reference.html` — same.

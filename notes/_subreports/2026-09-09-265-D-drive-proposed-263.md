   light `bg rgb(255,255,255) / ink rgb(0,0,0) / accent rgb(218,26,0)` = `#DA1A00`;

   inert. On `<body>` it yields `bg rgb(26,26,26)`, `ink rgb(255,255,255)`, and **`accent`

   `prefers-color-scheme` path does not have; the sticky bar, the `blockquote.rec` panel and the
   `pre.out` panel all stay light-painted, because `--bg`/`--panel`/`--rule` are never
   re-declared under the attribute. See

## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS

1. **Does the 44px target floor bind the sticky control bar, or only the ruling rows?**
   Observed: `button.rb` and `input.note` are 44.0px at ≤760px; `#exportBtn`/`#clearBtn` are
   34.2px, `label.tog` 35.0px, the `#decide` checkbox 13×13px.
   (a) Floor binds every interactive target on the page — one line in the `@media
   (max-width:760px)` block raising `.bar button,.bar label.tog` to `min-height:44px` and sizing
   the checkbox; (b) floor binds ruling rows only, and utility chrome is exempt by kind;
   (c) leave as shipped. **Recommend (a)** — it is one CSS line, it costs nothing anywhere else,
   and the page's whole purpose is a thumb ruling eleven rows on a phone. Not ruled here.
2. **Should the `[data-theme="dark"]` block at line 196 be deleted, or completed?** It changes
   nothing today (no toggle) and, if any later page wires the attribute, it half-paints and
   breaks TWO-RED LAW `s151-D1`. (a) Delete lines 196–208 from this page and let
   `prefers-color-scheme` be the only theme path; (b) complete it — move the whole dark custom-
   property block under `:root[data-theme="dark"]` as well, so both paths give identical
   `#0C0C0C`/`#F6604C`; (c) leave it. **Recommend (b) if the attribute arm is meant to be the
   house pattern across review pages, (a) if it is not** — this is a house-pattern call, not a
   page call, which is why it is here and not in Findings.
3. **`#1A1A1A` or `#0C0C0C` for a dark review-page ground?** The file declares both — `#1A1A1A`
   at line 196 (annotated `ds-033 / #108-D1 (Dave): dark bg = declared #1A1A1A token`) and
   `#0C0C0C` at line 224 as the `prefers-color-scheme` `--bg`. Whichever wins, the loser should
   go. Recommend nothing — the annotation cites Dave's own token and only Dave can say whether
   the review-page ground departs from it deliberately.

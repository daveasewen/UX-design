# #313 lane C1F: the catalogue takes a bound data value

COUNTS: catalogue selftest GREEN 15 of 15 (T1.10 new) · `--check` OK v0.1.2 · A2UI-valid 17/17 dashboard, 133/133 all · mutations caught 5/5 · gates selftest 26 of 26 T3.2 cases, failed none (B1–B4, N2c1 and B1m new) · chooser selftest GREEN 12 of 12 (it was RED 11 of 12 at base) · test_gates 39/39 · nothing committed.

Headline: a data setting now takes a binding. The catalogue's object-typed data settings were `oneOf [object, array, DataBinding]`. A `{"path": …}` binding is also an object, so it matched two branches and was refused (lane C3's F2). The generator keeps `oneOf` and stops the two branches overlapping. The literal-object branch is now `{"type": "object", "not": {"required": ["path"]}}`.

I chose this over `anyOf` because `anyOf` would let a malformed binding through as literal data, for example `{"path": "/x", "probe": 1}` or `{"path": 5}`. With the branches kept apart, an object that names `path` is a well-formed binding or it is refused. Every refusal from before still holds, and malformed bindings are refused too.

One loss: a literal data object whose own top-level key is `path` is now refused. None of the five settings has such a member.

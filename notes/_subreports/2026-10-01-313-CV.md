## What the patch holds (all new but the README)

- `apollo-launchpad/verify/worked_example.py` — the verifier. For each request in `mock/requests.json`: `choose()` (C4) → compose an A2UI v0.9.1 surface from the picked tiles and `mock/data.json` (a stand-in for the agent; not a renderer, not emitter E1, `s311-D9`) → `gate_surface(splice=True)` (C3) → the same two surfaces through `gates/server.py` over stdio → the record of spec § 8. Each screen is also composed with its data in the surface's data model and its settings bound to it (A2UI DataBinding), which is how a host is meant to send data; that leg is reported `BLKD`, never as a pass or a fail. Then five bad screens, one bad page, and one probe of the catalogue id. Writes nothing unless `--write`. Exit 1 on any scored failure.

## Findings

1. **ISO forbids the thing the brain does.** *Probe:* `iso.org/copyright.html` footer, fetched: "no ISO content may be used for any machine learning and/or artificial intelligence … (ii) prompt or otherwise enable artificial intelligence or similar tools to generate responses", excepting only the Open Data page. A principle node a generator reads is a prompt. Consequences: (a) purchasing ISO 9241-110 grants reading, not prompting — any plan line that prices ISO as "buy, then import" is wrong; (b) lane G's seven principle names (held via Molich's DS-permitted draft reproduction) are the one artefact in the repo this lane cannot tier by itself — flagged as UNPROVEN 2 and ruling-shaped question 2; (c) the only ISO material with a grant is Open Data metadata (reference, title, scope) under ODC-By, which is enough for a pointer node. **This is the only family of the 13 with an AI clause** (`tiering.json#tally.families_with_ai_clause`).

2. **The EAA is *safe now*, and the legal set is whole.** *Probe:* Decision 2011/833/EU Article 4(a) and Article 6(2)(a)–(b), read from the King's Printer copy at legislation.gov.uk (the EUR-Lex copy returned only a resolved-URL line, three routes); Commission legal notice "Copyright notice" section (CC BY 4.0 for EU-owned web content); legislation.gov.uk's own footer declaring it reuses EUR-Lex content under that Decision. Conditions are two — credit and fidelity — and fidelity is a licence *condition*, which is the right constraint on an obligation node anyway. Residual: the EUR-Lex legal notice's own sentence is UNPROVEN 3; lane G proved a browser client reads that host.

3. **R1's four "UNPROVEN: no licence statement" families split three ways, and the method explains the split.** *Probe:* the receipts' `probe_for_licence` fields. Inclusive Design Principles: a CC badge + href in the footer (BY-SA 2.0 UK image, BY-SA 4.0 link) — invisible to a tag-stripping fetch. Norman: a plain-text footer sentence (CC BY-NC 4.0) *and* an in-article ACM paragraph — both non-commercial. Butterick: no notice on the chapter, a full notice on `legal.html`. Shneiderman: nothing on the rules page or the site root. R1's "no licence found" was correct about what its fetch saw and wrong about two of the four sites; the lesson is the one lane G learned at #237 — the client, not the route.

4. **RFC 9413's licence turns on one header field.** *Probe:* the RFC's own header "Stream: Internet Architecture Board (IAB)" and Copyright Notice (the Alternate-Stream form, no derivative-works legend); TLP 5.0 §8.e (IAB stream adopts the TLP) and §8.c.iii ("shall not be limited to the IETF Standards Process", of the §3.a grant). For an IETF-stream RFC the answer would be "unmodified copies only"; for this IAB-stream RFC the §3.a grant — copy in whole or part, translate, modify — applies outside the standards process, with §3.d.ii still requiring the notices. R1's steward field "IETF / IAB" was carrying legal weight nobody had read. The reading is flagged (UNPROVEN 8); the attributed-unmodified-quotation posture does not depend on it.

5. **The right primary for a licence is not always the page that states the rule.** *Probe:* `support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858` — footer links only Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, no content licence, and the page itself defers to Material's guidelines; `developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility/apps` — states "at least 48dpx48dp" and its footer binds the page to `/license` (Apache 2.0 for documentation; CC BY 2.5 for the rest; brand features and linked content excluded). The "≈ 9 mm" conversion that `s114-D3/D5` cite lives only on the unlicensed Help Center page; a node should state 48 dp and convert in Apollo's words. Material (`m3.material.io`) returned nothing to this fetcher, as it did to R1's two attempts.

6. **ShareAlike makes verbatim cheaper than paraphrase for one family.** *Probe:* CC BY-SA 4.0 legal code §2(a)(1) (reproduce and Share, in whole or in part; produce Adapted Material) and §3(b)(1) (the Adapter's Licence "must be a Creative Commons license with the same License Elements"). A generator that rewrites an Inclusive Design principle and emits it into a client brief has made Adapted Material and owes BY-SA on that passage; a node that quotes the principle verbatim with credit owes attribution only. The permits column for this family therefore reads *reproduce* first and *paraphrase* with a rider.

7. **The retyped-count class fired inside this lane, and the instrument caught it.** *Probe:* `_check_receipts.py` first run: 10 problems — Fogg 17 words, ISO Open Data 17, RFC 15→16, and seven stated counts off by one (Android, CWV, EAA, IDP among them). Every figure in this report's COUNTS line is copied from the script's output, and the script is filed beside the receipts so the conductor can re-run it.

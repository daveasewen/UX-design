**Two steps, and the first one is not decoration.** The value lands on the **theme carrier** and only
`[data-theme="dark"]` consumes it. That is what makes a **pinned** specimen answerable: on
`bento-rails.html` the mode lives on `<body>` and the theme is pinned on the card
(`.br-spec[data-apollo-theme="console"]`), so canon's own compound idiom
(`[data-apollo-theme="x"][data-theme="dark"]`) **never matches there**, and a plain descendant pair
would let the page switcher's theme leak into a card pinned to another. An inherited custom property
answers at the nearest carrier, which is the pin.

## Findings

1. **The mint had to be keyed on the SURFACE, not just the word.** `darkgrey` is *also* a section
   ground (`data-bento-bg="darkgrey"`, s219-D3(5)). A mint on the word would have repainted every
   dark-grey **wall** as well as the caption inside it — a change nothing was ruled about. The key is
   `(theme, mode, word)` and the table is consulted by `caption_*` and by nothing else. Probed: bite
   `C4e`, and the `$bentoBg` sweep row for console dark still reads `#1A1A1A` / 17.40:1.

2. **⛔ A MANIFEST THAT REPORTS THE RAMP WHILE THE PAGE PAINTS THE MINT IS THE WORST KIND OF GREEN.**
   `_bento_edit_rails.json` is what the library page reads (s219-D3(6)) — it does **not** reach the
   Python. Left alone it would have printed **17.40:1** for a card measuring **13.01:1** and no gate
   would have fired ([[no-gate-parses-the-artefact]]). Both `contrast` and `resolved` now come
   through the caption chain, and the member carries a `caption_ground_mint` row naming the
   primitive, the status, whose decision it was and the value it replaced. Enacted consequence:
   `bento-rails.html`'s *Capsule — Dark grey* card now prints **"light 17.4:1 · dark 13.01:1"**.

3. **⛔ NEW CLASS — CANON'S OWN THEME×MODE SELECTOR IDIOM DOES NOT REACH A PINNED SPECIMEN.**
   Canon writes theme-dark blocks as
   `[data-apollo-theme="x"][data-theme="dark"], [data-apollo-theme="x"] [data-theme="dark"]` — both
   forms require the theme to be **on or above** the mode. On `bento-rails.html` the mode is on
   `<body>` and the theme is pinned **below** it on the card, so **neither form matches**, and
   console's dark block does not apply to a console-pinned specimen there at all. My mint sidesteps
   it with an inherited custom property. ⚠ **The wider consequence is NOT mine and is not fixed
   here:** canon's console dark block declares radii, RAG hues, segmented sizes and badge colours,
   and by this reading **none of them reach a pinned specimen on that page**. It has never been
   measured. Price to prove: one probe over the four pinned cards, ~2K tk.

4. **`_raises` cannot catch a generator's refusal, and the first cut of the arm proved it by
   dying.** The selftest helper catches `Exception`; a `SystemExit` is a `BaseException` and walked
   straight past it, killing the whole selftest instead of registering a red
   ([[a-crash-is-not-a-fail]]). The helper is left alone; both new arms name what they catch.
   Worth knowing before anyone writes the next "this must raise" bite in either file.

5. **My hand-computed contrast was wrong and the instrument caught it.** I wrote `12.94` into bite
   `C4e`; the measurement said **13.01**. Corrected to the measured value. A planning arithmetic is
   not a measurement ([[planning-estimate-is-not-a-measurement]]) — and the bite was written to fail
   loudly rather than to be tolerant, which is why one keystroke of my arithmetic could not ship.

6. **The mint reached three pages nobody named in the brief, and that is correct.**
   `grids-dashboard.html`, `grids-display.html` and `grids-gallery.html` embed the explorer's
   stylesheet wholesale, so they picked up the block too — **+15 lines each, additive, no other
   diff**. Checked line by line rather than assumed, because a diff arriving in a page you did not
   expect is exactly how a sibling lane's change gets attributed to yours.

7. **⚠ THE SAME DEFECT EXISTS IN MONO AND LEGACY, AND NOTHING HERE FIXES IT.** In dark, mono's and
   legacy's `darkgrey` caption ground is `#1A1A1A` on a `#1A1A1A` page — **1.00:1, ΔL\* 0.00**, the
   identical vanish. They are only spared because s219-D3(3)/X6 does not offer them the ground. And
   **supercharge's is worse in kind**: `#13110E` on `#1A1A1A` is ΔL\* 4.10 in the **wrong
   direction** — a caption ground *darker* than the page it sits on. All four rows are drawn on the
   proof page, on their own page grounds, so the shape is visible rather than described.

brief's chief question is `3`.)*

1. **Is `#313131` the rung?** It is `color/neutral/5` — one grey point off his `rgb(48,48,48)`,
   ΔL\* ≈ 0.4 lighter. If it reads wrong beside the page, the ramp's neighbours are **neutral/4
   `#1A1A1A`** (which is where it was — no help) and **neutral/6 `#484848`**, which the `#220` ladder

   exists between neutral/5 and neutral/6** — the next answer down is a derived value, and a derived
   value with no primitive behind it is a promotion.

2. **Does a SEMANTIC token get minted for it?** Today the binding reads the **primitive** directly.
   ADR-0014 says semantic roles alias `color/neutral/*` and **consumers read the semantic role** — so
   this is a declared layering exception, honest for a PROPOSED value with no role yet, and wrong as
   a permanent shape. (a) Leave it on the primitive while it is proposed. (b) Mint a caption-ground
   role with a light value and a dark value together — **a promotion, Dave's**. ⚠ Under (a) the
   exception is invisible to anyone reading the token tree; under (b) the mode-inversion problem
   comes back as a token-design problem, because the pair has to answer light too.

3. **⬛ THE BRIEF'S CHIEF QUESTION — does the same bind extend to legacy and supercharge capsule
   captions in dark? NOT ENACTED.** Finding 7 says the collision is there and the numbers are
   drawn. But **neither theme has a capsule chord at all** — s219-D3(3) scopes both chords to
   console gallery — so extending the bind would mean extending the CHORD first, which is a wider
   ruling than a colour. (a) Rule console alone and book the rest. (b) Extend the mint to all four
   themes' `darkgrey` caption ground now, so that whenever a chord reaches them the ground is
   already right. (c) Rule the ground ramp itself. ⚠ Under (b) three themes get a value change
   nobody can currently see, which is the shape of a change that surfaces months later.

4. **Does the shipped console DEFAULT move onto this ground?** It does not today: console gallery

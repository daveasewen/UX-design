## ③ ⛔ THE DEFECT: DAVE'S PAGE COULD NOT RENDER

`render_page()` does `lead_title, lead_plain = GROUP_LEAD[gk]` — a **bare subscript**. N3
declared a new `gumdrop` group (correctly, and for good stated reasons) and there was no
`GROUP_LEAD["gumdrop"]`. Every group card is emitted in that loop, so the first group with no
lead takes down **the whole page**, not one paragraph.

The 149-bite selftest was green through it. It could not have been otherwise: `GROUP_LEAD` was
a **local inside `render_page`**, unreachable from the selftest, and nothing in the selftest
renders a page. [[green-tests-cannot-see-scope]] [[no-gate-parses-the-artefact]]

Fixed at cause, three moves:

1. `GROUP_LEAD` **hoisted to module scope**, with a comment saying why it is not a local.
2. `GROUP_LEAD["gumdrop"]` written — *"Day one — the cold start"* — and `memento-clean-cut`'s
   lead rewritten so the two cards read as a pair instead of contradicting each other.
3. Two new bites, both mutation-driven:
   - `page/every-group-has-a-lead` — a group declared in `groups()` with no lead. **Driven:
     deleting the gumdrop lead goes RED naming `['gumdrop']`.**
   - `page/no-orphan-leads` — the other direction, prose for a group that no longer exists.

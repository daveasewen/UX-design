**Derivations — printed, never written** (s251-D6, ISO 25964):
- **`instead-of` grades.** Co-providers of one role are substitutes (same FFF *function*).
  The grade compares the other two faces the metas carry — `shape` and `span` —
  `equivalent` (shape equal AND span overlaps) · `near` (one face) · `distant` (neither).
  The ISO 25964 **preferred term** of each set is the highest `priority`, printed on its own
  line; direction runs lower-priority → preferred.
- **`not-with` reciprocals** — exclusion is symmetric, so B not-with A is derived from
  A not-with B; a target that is a ROLE rather than a meta prints `NOT DERIVABLE`, and one a
  meta already declares prints `already-authored` (never "a duplicate to add").
- **`with` reciprocals** — derived but printed as `suggests` and flagged NOT ASSERTED: a
  complement is one-way until Dave says otherwise (the Liskov clause of s251-D6).

**Cross-check, report-only.** `roles.json` `providers` membership vs the metas' `provides`.
While zero metas carry the field the output says so in as many words —
*"ZERO metas carry `provides` — the cross-check has NOTHING to compare. This is a REPORT OF
ABSENCE, not agreement: read no green from it."* — so an empty diff can never be misread as
agreement. It never fails, per s252-D1's own wording (the metas become canon; this list
becomes the cross-check, not the source).

## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS (for Dave — nothing here is ruled)

1. **Does `priority-unique` bind `roles.json` too, or only the metas?** As written it checks
   the metas only, per s252-D1's "the metas are canon, this file's lists are a cross-check".
   `roles.json` today has five `chart-panel` providers at priority 60 and 26 `input` providers
   with no priority. Options: (a) leave as-is, illustrative values never checked; (b) extend the
   check to `roles.json` and re-space those numbers; (c) drop priority from `roles.json`
   entirely once the metas carry it.
2. **When do the non-chart `answers` words enter `chart-intents.json`?** They must be RULED in
   before lane A's metas can be green. Lane A's review page is presumably where they get
   proposed — but the ordering (page → ruling → metas, or metas authored red and greened after
   the ruling) is a call, and it gates the wave.
3. **Is `intent` retired once `answers` exists?** The #253 call kept both and made the validator
   assert equality. That is two homes for one fact, which ADR-0017 exists to prevent — tolerable
   as migration, not as a resting state. A ruling to drop `intent` (and retire
   `_validate_intent_resolve.py` into this one) would close it.
4. **Should `shape` get a store and a resolver?** It is the one axis of the eight with no home
   and no check; the validator can only list its values. Lane A's page will show the vocabulary
   it invents. A `shapes.json` on the `chart-intents.json` pattern is the obvious shape if you
   want it fenced.
5. **`_validate_fit_physics.py`: wire it or exempt it BY NAME?** It has been an orphan since
   #250 and it is the only thing standing between the wiring gate and green. Not lane C's call.
6. **The 18 `knowledge/_tmp/` help-gate failures — fix, exempt, or delete `_tmp`?** They abort
   the full runner at step 8 for every lane, and (finding 4) they turn any full-build attempt
   into a compliance-artefact strip. This is the highest-leverage red in the repo right now.
7. **Should `with` be allowed on a meta at all?** s251-D6 puts complements on the ROLE. The
   schema seat exists so the resolver has a shape to read, but an unused seat is a place for
   drift. Options: (a) keep it optional as built; (b) remove the seat and let the validator read
   `with` only from `roles.json`.
8. **Does an `aliasOf` meta get to carry the eight terms?** The alias size fence
   (`maxProperties: 14` plus a `not/anyOf` list) does not name any of them, so an alias could
   carry `provides` today and stay inside the fence. Deliberate or an oversight to close?

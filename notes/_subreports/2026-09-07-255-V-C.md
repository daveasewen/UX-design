## RULING-SHAPED

1. **The replacement of the divisibility leg is COMPELLED in its core, but BROADER than compelled
   in its reach.** `s255-D1` rules the 4+2 wall legal and says the divisibility leg "was lagging the
   rulings". Keeping a per-tile `cols % span == 0` alongside the new readers would still red the
   wall (`[6, 4, 2]` at 6 cols: 4 ∤ 6), so that leg *had* to go for the ruled grid. What the ruling
   does not say is that `grid-auto-flow: dense` becomes a source of legality for every page. That is
   the lane's own extension.
2. **The concrete thing the old leg caught and the new one accepts:** mutant (d) — `[4,4,2,2]` in 6
   columns with `row dense`. It packs into two whole rows only because dense backfills tile C into
   row 1 *ahead of* tile B, i.e. the rendered order departs from DOM order. `C4` does not see it
   (`C4 · 0 reordering declaration(s)` on that page — C4 reads `order:`, `*-reverse` and explicit
   line placement, not `dense`), and `C8`'s contiguity leg is the UNPROVEN rendered one. So this
   page is green with no condition covering the reorder. The real artefact does **not** depend on
   this: all four of its grids flow into whole rows sparse as well as dense.
3. **A literal `repeat(<n>)` opts a grid out of the band model anywhere on the page** (the lane's own
   flag 2). Confirmed by K5 and by (a): the count is derived, not named — which is right — but no
   rule says *which* scopes may write a literal. That is a ruling, not a fix.
4. **`--selftest` is still not a `_build_all.STEPS` entry** (open since #245). The 20-arm harness
   that carries this change runs only via `_validate_screen.py` and the pack probe.

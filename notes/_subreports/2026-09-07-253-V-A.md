They are additions to lane A's eight page calls, not restatements of them.

**V-Q1 — is a vacuous currency clause a pass?** The brief's check 7 says `£` never `$`. This page shows no
prices, so it neither obeys nor breaks the rule; the four `$` are `$composes`/`$note` sigils that the canon

by accident and a future page that names a JSON sigil would trip a literal check. Worth deciding whether the
gate reads currency-in-prose or the character.

**V-Q2 — should the `when` field vocabulary be gated before it grows again?** My independent parse of the
predicate halves reproduced the page's 18 fields exactly, which means the grammar is *currently* regular
enough for a script — `field <op> value` joined by `AND`. That regularity is unenforced and undeclared. It is
the cheapest moment to freeze it (which is lane A's page call 7 from the other end: split `when` into two
fields). Left open.

schema and prints `reciprocal NOT DERIVABLE`. It is the only such edge in 24 metas. Either the derivation
learns to expand a role into its providers, or role targets are ruled out, or the asymmetry is accepted and
named. Lane A raises this as its call 6; I confirm it is a population of exactly one.

### 4 · F12 + F19 — a refusal that could not reach its own legal form ⛔

  line and exit 77.

  this repo's own sandbox convention (`PYTHONPATH=/var/tmp/pylibs-s<n>`), which exists on nobody's
  laptop. Both now lead with `pip install playwright && playwright install chromium` and keep the
  runbook named only for the seat that needs the staged form.

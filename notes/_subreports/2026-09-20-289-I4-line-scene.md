# 289 · I4 — the line scene: two robots, one conveyor

`notes/_lanes/289/illustration/line.html` · renders `line-rest.png`, `line-orbit.png`
(600×420 card, device scale 2). Dave: *"two robots either side of a conveyor belt."*

## What it is
A conveyor running diagonally — shallow bed, four legs, two end rollers as cylinders,
painted centre dashes — a manipulator each side spaced along it, three boxes riding it.
36 primitives, 544 vertices, 172 faces drawn / 172 culled, 498 edges inked, 19 free lines.
**The arm is a function:** `makeArm(Fbase, pose, tag, ord0)` takes a placement frame and a
pose, so the two robots are the same drawing; poses are *solved* by `reachPose(x, z, c)`, a
closed-form 2-link IK, elbow-up root picked by comparing elbow heights. Simplified against
I3: no bolt lugs, prisms 12/10/8-sided not 24/20, only J1/J2/J3 carry axes, `HARD` 0.65 so
a coarse prism still inks no flats.

## The three things that made it work
1. **Mirror the second arm; don't turn it round.** A 180° yaw flips the sign of every step
   along the shared J2/J3/J5 axis, and those steps are the only reason chain order is exact.
   Negating the placement frame's y builds a left-handed twin: the reach still points at the
   belt, both chains step toward the camera, and the pair reads as a mirrored cell.
   `lineView()` confirms both axis depths at +0.687.
2. **A long solid's centroid lies.** Global centroid-depth sorting is sound *between*
   non-interpenetrating objects, but the bed sorts nearer than a box on its far end and v4's
   bed white-filled two workpieces away. Fixed as each arm's chain is: conveyor before
   workpieces, re-seated into the slots the global sort gave them.
3. **A line behind someone else's metal is hidden, not dashed.** I3 had two classes; with four
   objects that laid three long grey diagonals across the bed and made it look like glass.
   Now: open → dash-dot, inside its OWN object → dashed, behind another → not drawn. The belt
   axis still shows 281.7 px buried in the bed — the run that explains it.

## Ink, per the idiom
`#111` hairline · `#BDBDBD` dashed for own-metal runs only · `#9B9B9B` dash-dot in the open,
including the one construction axis along the belt's centre · **ONE** red `#DA1A00`: the
workpiece under the reaching arm (I3's red elbow rim is gone). Base plate outline under all
of it. Rest yaw 35 / pitch 20, orbit ±25 / ±12, τ = 1.0 s, stays put. Idle: reaching arm
rocks 2.5° at shoulder and elbow on 13 s, centre dashes creep; both suppressed under
prefers-reduced-motion (verified `running:false`, no errors). 0.7 ms/frame.

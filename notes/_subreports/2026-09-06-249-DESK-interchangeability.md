## Findings

1. **The design world encodes "instead of" as a sentence and "related" as a folder.** Figma: "related components are components that share the same hierarchy … Figma also groups related components organized using the slash naming structure" [S9]. Carbon: "When not to use — Do not use buttons as navigational elements. Instead, use links" plus a hand-written table of button-group "combinations to avoid" [S17]. DTCG's only relation is alias/reference with a deprecation string "Please use {button.activeBorder} instead" [S5]. Apollo's 25 files of `$differsFrom…` under 8 spellings are the same maturity.
2. **The one graded edge in the design tradition grades confidence, not similarity.** Alexander: "the asterisks represent our degree of faith in these hypotheses" (0–2), and the network is bracketed numbers inside prose [S13]. Material 3 grades *emphasis* (filled > tonal > outlined > text), which is Apollo's `prominence`, within one family only [S22].
3. **SKOS refuses to chain the weaker match.** `closeMatch` = "sufficiently similar that they can be used interchangeably in **some** information retrieval applications … to avoid the possibility of 'compound errors' … `skos:closeMatch` is **not** declared to be a transitive property"; `exactMatch` is transitive and a sub-property of `closeMatch`; both symmetric (S44) [S1]. A 0–1 weight multiplied along kpi → stat → status is exactly the compound error SKOS legislates against.
4. **Thesaurus standards author one direction and derive the reciprocal, and allow one-for-many.** ISO 25964: if A BT B then B NT A; if E USE F then F UF E; compound equivalence `USE A + B` / `UF+` [S11][S11b]. Part 2 §11 grades matches "exact, inexact and partial" — three words [S11]. Apollo's validator does not derive reciprocals today (META finding 2: 13 edge kinds, 1 declared).
5. **Form-fit-function is the FFF-exact analogue of `fits` / `data-shape` / `answers`.** "if F3 for two components have the same set of characteristics … same shape or form, same connections or fit, and perform the same function, they can be substituted one for another"; the concept exists so the customer can "second source the part" — competition between suppliers, i.e. Dave's three solutions [S6]. Substitutability is *derived* from three axis comparisons, not authored as a pair weight.
6. **TI's cross-reference ladder is FFF as an ordinal grade**: DROP-IN (same function, same pinout, parametrically equivalent or better) · PIN-FOR-PIN (same function, same pinout, different parameters) · SAME FUNCTIONALITY (same function, different pinout) · SIMILAR FUNCTIONALITY [S18]. Each rung drops one axis; "or better" makes it one-way. Proposed Apollo ladder: `drop-in · same-shape · same-answer · similar`.
7. **Debian names the role, keeps three relations apart, and puts the condition on the edge.** `Provides: mail-transport-agent` makes any provider satisfy the dependency; providers of a one-at-a-time facility add `Conflicts:` + `Replaces:` on the virtual name; "Having similar functionality … is not sufficient reason to declare Breaks or Conflicts"; `Recommends` ("found together … in all but unusual installations") vs `Suggests` are a two-rung complement; `foo [!i386] | bar [!amd64]` is a per-alternative condition [S2]. `update-alternatives` adds an integer priority compared only within one alternative group, overridable by the administrator (manual mode) [S3] — the home for Apollo's number, and `s248-D4` "the choice is the designer's" is manual mode.
8. **Substitution is directional and needs a declared interface.** semver: "you first need to declare a public API" [S14]; Liskov: S substitutes T, never the reverse [S21]; TI "or better". `kpi-tile.meta.json` `$differsFromStatCard` ("A KPI tile with no series is a Stat card") is a one-way Liskov statement and should not be made reciprocal.
9. **Amazon needed two graphs and 144M reviews.** McAuley et al.: substitutes ("purchased instead of each other") vs complements ("in addition to"), conditional on stage ("while a user is looking for mobile phones … recommend other phones, but once they buy a phone … batteries, cases, or chargers"), weights from supervised link prediction over 9M products / 237M links / 144M reviews [S4]. Apollo has 137 files and no behavioural data: a float there is an opinion with a decimal point. The *two-relation* structure transfers; the weights do not.
10. **Every source that grades uses 2–4 named rungs; none puts a continuous weight on an authored edge.** SKOS 2 · ISO 3 · TI 4 · Alexander 3 · semver 3 · Debian complement 3 · Wikidata "said to be the same as" as a named hedge with an explicit "different from" negative [S8]. The recurring structures (page §05): FFF triad · ordinal grade · condition on the edge · substitutes ≠ complements ≠ conflicts · name the role not the pair · author one way, derive the reciprocal, forbid chaining.

**Vocabulary proposed (page §06), each traced:** `answers` (FFF function; Alexander problem-first) · `shape` (FFF fit; Liskov interface; Figma slot) · `span` (FFF form; TI pinout) · `provides: role` (Debian virtual package; WordNet synset; ISO preferred term) · `instead-of {slug, grade, when}` (TI ladder; SKOS exact/close; Debian `[restriction]`; tritone "resolves to I") · `with {slug, recommends|suggests}` on the role (Debian; McAuley complements) · `not-with {slug, when}` slug required (Debian Conflicts; Wikidata "different from") · `priority` integer in-group, overridable (update-alternatives). **Example A** resolves to three providers of `status-surface` ranked by priority, grades derived from FFF comparison, kpi-tile excluded by a slugged `not-with` — k = 3 with no pair weight. **Example B** makes reflow/compress two providers of the alternative `band-1100`, owned by the band, `when` as a predicate over `prominence`/`span.cols`, `mode: manual`.

**Not worth copying (page §07):** 0–1 weights on authored edges; any transitivity; naming/folder-derived relatedness (Figma); the full WordNet/Debian relation sets; prose `when`; semver (versions ≠ substitutes, and the word is taken by pack releases).

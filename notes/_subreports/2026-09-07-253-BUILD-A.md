## Findings

**F1 — cross-lane priority collision at chart-panel 60, reconciled from this side.**
roles.json's illustrative ladder gives chart-bar, chart-line, chart-donut, Chart-histogram and
Chart-scatter all `60` — a five-way tie the ROLES page left for this pass. Lane B broke it into
a step-of-4 ladder (chart-line 88 · chart-donut 80 · Chart-histogram 76 · Chart-scatter 72 ·
chart-stacked-area 64 · **Chart-boxplot 60** · chart-combo 56 · chart-pie 48 · Chart-candlestick 44
· Chart-butterfly-h 40 · Chart-butterfly-v 36 · chart-sparkline 30) and assigned 60 to
Chart-boxplot. chart-bar at the illustrative 60 would have failed check 5 `priority-unique`.
84 is the free rung immediately below chart-line and keeps chart-bar in the top tier the ROLES
page put it in. Neither lane could read the other's file mid-wave; this was fixed on my side only,
touching no lane-B meta. Post-fix: `dupes: []` across all eight roles in use.

**F2 — the two lanes wiped each other's work with `git checkout --`.**
I ran `git checkout -- knowledge/components/` to undo my own reformat. Shortly after, all eleven of
my authored metas were back at HEAD while lane B's thirteen were present — i.e. lane B ran the same
command over the same directory. Both lanes recovered by re-running their generators. Nothing was
lost permanently because both lanes author from a script rather than by hand, but **the wave came
within one un-scripted edit of losing real work**. A concurrent-writer lane must never `git
checkout` a shared directory; scope any revert to the specific files that lane owns.

**F3 — `summary`'s role membership and its own purpose prose disagree.**
roles.json places `summary` in `status-surface` with the illustrative `when` "n × count as a
key/value list, 6–12 cols". Its meta `purpose` says "A key/value list for confirming details before
or after an action (a payment review, a receipt)". The brief says ground every value in the meta's
own purpose, so `answers` is `details-before-commit` — which is a confirmation question, not a
counting one. Either the membership is right and the purpose prose is stale, or the purpose is
right and summary is a second-role candidate (the ROLES page §14 call 3 already names summary as
the first multi-provides candidate). Raised as Call 2 on the page; not ruled.

**F4 — `not-with` targeting a role gets no derived reciprocal.**
The validator prints `status-surface not-with kpi-tile -> reciprocal NOT DERIVABLE (target is a
ROLE, not a meta)`. The schema permits a role slug, and the ROLES page words the exclusion that
way, so the authored value is legal and faithful. But it is the only exclusion in the corpus that
gets no symmetric partner, so it is invisible from the status-surface providers' side. Raised as
Call 6.

**F5 — two resolvable `mustNotNeighbour` entries were left unpromoted, deliberately.**
chart-bar's `edges.mustNotNeighbour[0]` is `component:stat-card` — fully resolvable, and exactly
the shape `not-with` was promoted for (schema: "`mustNotNeighbour` PROMOTED to a typed edge"). The
brief authorised only the one exclusion the ROLES page names, and the ~50 prose entries are a
declared migration backlog (ADR-0017 archive-never-delete), so nothing was promoted. Raised as
Call 5. Note the validator already DERIVES `chart-bar not-with Chart-histogram` and
`chart-bar not-with chart-stacked-area` from lane B's authoring, so the reciprocity machinery is
demonstrably live.

**F6 — the `shape` axis has no natural value for two of the eleven.**
`button` and `layout-utilities` carry no data of their own — the schema calls `shape` "what shape
of data the component takes". They were given `no-data` and `arbitrary-blocks` rather than being
left absent, because absence is indistinguishable from an un-authored meta. Raised as Call 8.

**F7 — the vocabularies, all three, resolve against nothing.**
11 `shape` values and 18 `when` fields from this batch alone (22 shape values across both lanes,
per the validator's own listing). Neither is checked by anything; both are listed on the page.
This is the declared UNPROVEN the schema names, not an oversight.

**F8 — roles.json ↔ metas membership agrees for all 24.**
The validator's report-only cross-check: "roles.json membership and the metas' `provides` AGREE for
every meta that carries the field (24)". No membership was changed by either lane.

## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS

Numbered as on the review page §14 (eight calls, strikeable). The four that most want a word:

`one-number · details-before-commit · status-of-one · where-does-it-go · where-am-i · what-is-this ·
what-can-i-do · where-can-i-go · what-holds-the-page`. They are phrased as the reader's question, to
match the five existing intent words. Ruling them in turns 10 red metas green with no meta edit.
Ruling them out means re-cutting them.

**Q2 (page call 2) — `summary`: is the role membership right, or the purpose prose?** See F3.

**Q3 (page call 4) — is chart-bar at 84 the right rung?** See F1. The alternative is that chart-bar,
not chart-line, is the role's preferred term and takes the top rung; the validator currently prints
`PREFERRED TERM chart-line (priority 88)` for chart-panel.

**Q4 (page call 7) — should `when` be two fields rather than one string with an em dash?**
The predicate half is C9-readable; the sibling-comparison half is prose by construction (R2 requires
it). Splitting them is a schema addition and would let the field collector run over a clean grammar.

Also open, asked on the page but lower stakes: whether `answers` may be a two-word list on a
non-chart meta (kpi-tile, call 3); whether the resolvable `mustNotNeighbour` entries migrate now
(call 5); whether a role-targeted `not-with` is legal at all (call 6); whether `shape` should be
absent on data-less components (call 8).

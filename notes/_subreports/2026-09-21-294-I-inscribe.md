The arm exists to plant a JSON-equivalent reindent OUTSIDE the target span and prove that only A2
(reconstruction) can see it. Its plant was the literal `'\n "ruled":'` — **one** space, i.e. array
elements at indent 0. Measured against the live store: **`'\n "ruled":'` occurs 0 times;
`'\n  "ruled":'` occurs 148 times** — elements sit at indent 1 and keys at indent 2, which is what
`compose()` itself writes. `str.replace` on a string that is not there returns the string
**unchanged**, so the plant silently never fired, the amend then succeeded *correctly*, and the arm
read that success as *"A2 is dead"*. The suite was reporting **a defect in the tool where there was
only a defect in its own fixture** — and in the shape where the literal *would* have matched, it was
measuring an indent the store never uses.

**Fix:** the plant is now **located in the head by search, never typed**, over
`'\n  "ruled":' / '\n  "date":' / '\n  "by":'`, and **its firing is ASSERTED** — an unfired plant
now appends an explicit `armA1: UNMEASURED` failure, because unmeasured is never a pass. The arm now
does what it always claimed: the amend is refused, and refused with `not textual`.

## What happens to the old family entry, and why
It **stays as a family meta with no spec of its own**. Retiring it would break four things:
1. the snippet manifest names "Selection controls", so the coverage gate would call the snippet an orphan;
2. the family is the `input` provider in roles.json;
3. data-grid's selection-checkbox is `containedBy` it;
4. its catalogue entry and showroom page hang off it.

Keeping the four-tree anatomy as well would put the same spec in two homes. So the spec moved, and `$split` fences it against being re-drafted.

## 2. What the design said and what the build did differently (each under Found-not-fixed or here)

- The design's § 6 says the gate's `_carry_items` reads the rendered line. The function does; the RESOLVER does not: `_capture_gate._resolve_residual_pointer` looks for `**residual → #M:**` inside `_CARRIES.md`, so a delta-only section would fail the blocking 2c carry gate. The runbook therefore keeps the `roll` + `strike` path until the one-call gate change lands (Found-not-fixed 1); `delta` may be appended as well, harmlessly.
- The memory view is ONE file with `## INDEX LINE`, `## FRONT BLOCK`, `## BODY` headings the placer cuts at (limit 11); the hand-written hook plus four payload files wrote the body three times. The replay's baseline for `memory` is the five files concatenated (`replay.py` does this); its heading count differs by design.
- The banner's ① names where his word is (a time, an export) and not the quotation, to stay under `s241-D2`; the quotations live in the delta, the handoff and the dossier. Its ③ takes the first `@problems` line, the CI reds, the fill verdict and the push state. The #310 banner reads 802 cl100k against the hand-written 741.
- The carries count on the banner is the measured count at the wrap (`facts.carries.items`, the section BEFORE the roll) with the probe for #N+1, not a typed post-roll figure; the #311 banner already dropped the number.
- `d.commits_range` is first..last of the measured commits (`7bc6b0c5..69173275`), as the hand-written COMMIT STATE wrote it, not `since.range` (which starts at the previous wrap's sha).
- The report's headings follow the brief's form (`## Found, not fixed`, `## Ruling-shaped questions`, `## UNPROVEN`) rather than the #310 report's `## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS`; the replay's HEADINGS grading will say "differ" for the report by design.
- The summary is regenerated at stage post (its push and CI line sits under Outputs) but is exempt from the byte-prefix assertion: it is chat text, not a by-addition record.
- `--post`'s `chain_tk_after_5b` is null, declared ("not re-taken, the #241 rule"); the wrap gate's verdict at the commit comes from `--gate-log` when a log was saved, else `--gate-wrap` typed from the committer's log and marked so (none of #309–#311 saved one).

## Findings

1. **`$finding-opacity-is-not-hierarchy` — A GATE GAP, NOT A FOOTER PROBLEM.** #204 muted its
   copyright line with `alpha-60`. Composited, `#1A1A1A` at 0.60 over `surface/subtle` `#F0F0F0` is
   `~#706F6F` = **4.41:1** — below 4.5, on 12px legal type. No gate caught it: the snippet gate reads
   *declared token pairs*, and the declared pair passes at full strength. **An alpha on a text
   element defeats the contrast check silently.** Fixed here by removing muted text entirely — every
   string is `text/default`, and the band is quiet by type size and 1px pips.
2. **`$finding-24px-floor` — THE RULED FLOOR HAS NO TOKEN.** `tokens/layout.json` carries only
   `target/min` (44px); the dial-down floor of 24 lives in a `$description` string. So this file
   hardcodes `--target-floor:24px` and declares it *out* of the manifest rather than invent a leaf in
   a shared file or bind a 44px token to a 24px box. Every future dense component will do the same.
3. **The pips were invisible.** `divider/border/subsection` resolves to `#F0F0F0` in light — i.e.
   `surface/subtle`, the band they divide. Caught by eye on a driven screenshot, not by a gate.
   They take `divider/border/section` instead; `subsection` now has no consumer here.
4. **A `flex:1 1 auto` provenance cluster ate the row.** MEASURED: 995px of a 1600px row, pushing
   the legal links to a second line at *every* width. `flex:0 1 auto` + an auto margin on the links.
5. **The vocabulary collision got WORSE, not better.** #204 declared it: "footer" already means a
   container's action/pagination row, and gated Action-bar calls itself "A footer bar…". After this
   redesign the two share a surface, a width and a one-row shape at the bottom of the same screen.
   ⛔ Renaming is Dave's; nothing was renamed.

## Ft3

1. **The 41 is gone and the whole component is on the grid.** Dave: *"One 41px row — I should never see a figure like 41, we have a 4px grid."* 41 was `min-height:40` + a 1px `border-top`; both removed (a reversed-out plate separates itself by value, so the rule had no work). Driven in Chromium at 1920/1600/1440/1280/1024/768/420/375: default **56** one-row, 92 two-row, 128 three-row, 224/228 stacked; sticky 56; **minimal 48**. Every height at every width is a multiple of 4 — `notes/_lanes/261Ft3/_w2.py` and `_table.py` assert it. Two mechanisms hold it: `min-height:56/48` on `.ft-inner` (border-box, so the figure is the tape figure) and a 24px seat on every cluster and leaf — without the second a wrapped line collapsed to a 10.48px line box and put 78.48, then 171, on the tape. `vertical-align:top` on the link removes the last baseline descender (49 → 48). Hairlines (1px pip, 1px chip border) are the only non-multiples left.
2. **The band is reversed out in BOTH modes, on ruled tokens.** Dave: *"The footer should be reversed out in both light and dark. Do we have reversed-out tokens, a modifier for light mode?"* — **Tokens: YES.** `surface/action-primary` (#1A1A1A light / #FAFAFA dark; supercharge warms it to #13110E / #F0EFED through the ADR-0014 ramp) with `text/on-inverse` (#FFFFFF 17.4:1 / #333333 12.0:1) — the one semantic surface whose lightness inverts between modes, and its ruled label. **A light-mode inverse surface MODIFIER: NO, and its absence is already logged** — `knowledge/_COMPONENT-GAPS.md` row "Inverse / hero surface", 2026-06-30, verbatim *"no light-mode dark-band role"*. So `--ft-band`/`--ft-ink` are **composed here from the two ruled tokens and flagged, not promoted** — no charcoal invented, which is the failure that gap row names. Pips and a new 1px status-dot ring take `border/strong`, the one neutral holding an edge on both plates (4.41:1 / 3.79:1); the ring is required because s122-D2 made the RAG glyphs mode-invariant, so the green dot measured ~1.9:1 on the dark-mode plate and vanished. ⚠ **For Dave's eye:** a true inverse plate in dark mode is a LIGHT band. Drawn honestly rather than softened unasked; if he wants it quieter the softer ruled pair is `surface/action` + `text/reverse-secondary` (6.1:1 / 5.32:1), one token swap.
3. **Spacing and the notice.** Dave: *"Be more generous with the spacing and add the copyright notice."* Inner pad 8/16→16/24, cluster gap 8/24→12/32, identity 8/12→8/16, provenance 4/12→8/16, pip 12→16, env chip 20→24 tall and 6→8 wide. `© HSBC Group 2026. All rights reserved.` — the repo's existing string, unaltered — sits last in the regulator's cluster, sharing one `.ft-end` box with the legal links so the two travel together on a wrap, at full ink strength ($finding-opacity-is-not-hierarchy forbids the alpha #204 put on exactly this line). **PRICE, reported not hidden: the one-row width moved 1440 → 1600.** The notice costs ~200px on the right; 1440 now wraps to two 92px rows.

## Findings

1. **`$finding-opacity-is-not-hierarchy` — A GATE GAP, NOT A FOOTER PROBLEM.** #204 muted its
   copyright line with `alpha-60`. Composited, `#1A1A1A` at 0.60 over `surface/subtle` `#F0F0F0` is
   `~#706F6F` = **4.41:1** — below 4.5, on 12px legal type. No gate caught it: the snippet gate reads
   *declared token pairs*, and the declared pair passes at full strength. **An alpha on a text
   element defeats the contrast check silently.** Fixed here by removing muted text entirely — every
   string is `text/default`, and the band is quiet by type size and 1px pips.
2. **`$finding-24px-floor` — THE RULED FLOOR HAS NO TOKEN.** `tokens/layout.json` carries only
   `target/min` (44px); the dial-down floor of 24 lives in a `$description` string. So this file
   hardcodes `--target-floor:24px` and declares it *out* of the manifest rather than invent a leaf in
   a shared file or bind a 44px token to a 24px box. Every future dense component will do the same.
3. **The pips were invisible.** `divider/border/subsection` resolves to `#F0F0F0` in light — i.e.
   `surface/subtle`, the band they divide. Caught by eye on a driven screenshot, not by a gate.
   They take `divider/border/section` instead; `subsection` now has no consumer here.
4. **A `flex:1 1 auto` provenance cluster ate the row.** MEASURED: 995px of a 1600px row, pushing
   the legal links to a second line at *every* width. `flex:0 1 auto` + an auto margin on the links.
5. **The vocabulary collision got WORSE, not better.** #204 declared it: "footer" already means a
   container's action/pagination row, and gated Action-bar calls itself "A footer bar…". After this
   redesign the two share a surface, a width and a one-row shape at the bottom of the same screen.
   ⛔ Renaming is Dave's; nothing was renamed.

## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS

*(these are the questions that arose — the heading is the `s218-D7` / `s294-D2` machine-read form,
narrowed from "RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS THAT AROSE" by lane C2 at staging; no item was changed.)*

1. **Does a TIMED-OUT step belong in a verdict ledger?** 6 of the 146 are `errored` purely because I capped per-step time at 15–20s to fit the host's call cap. The survey is right that "nobody asked it and it never said why" is not a refusal — but a ledger that records runner impatience as a build fact will understate green forever. Options: record the timeout value per step (it already is, per record), exclude timeouts from the sentence, or give a timed-out step its own bucket in the published line.
2. **The host's call cap is now a measured constraint on the build's own verdict.** A full non-mutating pass **cannot be taken in one call at this seat** (measured twice, cut at 178s and 72s). So the project's most-read sentence is permanently assembled from chunks. Is a chunk-assembled verdict allowed to drop the `(assembled from consecutive chunks)` qualifier once coverage is total, or does it always ride?
3. **The `size:` line's unit word vs the tier.** GM's K-form says `tape` while the parentheticals beside it say `real` — two tiers on one line, inherited. The generator now writes the tier it measured in, which will rewrite `tape` → `real` the first time it runs on a seat with the real tier reachable (this seat measured GM on **cl100k**, so it would write `tape`). **Whether the front page's headline figure should be the `real` tier or the `tape` tier is Dave's** — `#82-D1` says real is the unit, `ds-021` says tape is what tiktoken counts, and the stamp currently says both.
4. **`VERDICT_SHA` and the whole #62 clause may now be retirable.** Its only remaining job is "how much of the build has never been in *that* verdict". With a live ledger the more useful question is "how much has never been green in *any* recorded run" — which the ledger can answer once it has more than one tree in it. Retiring a clause on the most expensive text in the repo is a ruling, not a tidy-up.
5. **The ledger has no cap and no rotation.** One line per survey run, forever, in `notes/`. Nothing in the repo rotates it yet.

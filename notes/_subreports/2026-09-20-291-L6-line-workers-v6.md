## WHAT LANDED

**1 · "the top oval facet can just be removed" — the COLLAR BAND at z 85 is
deleted; `band(85, 1.025, 0)` is gone from `yokeSeams()`.** The first thing
checked was whether the yoke still had a cap to ink, because that is what the
note sounds like. It does not: v5 closed the yoke on a POLE at z 88 — one
vertex, a triangle fan, no cap polygon — and `push(bury(yok, yok.capLo,
yok.capHi), 4)` buries what caps remain. So nothing on the solid could have
drawn that ring. The **pixel diff against v5 proves it**: the arc that
disappears from each figure's neck is exactly the collar band's facets and
nothing else. It was the one seam in the drawing emitted with `half = 0`, so
it ran all the way round instead of stopping at the shoulder line, and a
closed curve round a neck on a figure this size reads as a hoop, not a
collar.

What stays: the **YOKE SEAM at z 79** (`half = 1`, so it is an arc across the
BACK of the shoulders — not a ring, and it is the seam the garment drawing
actually has), both halves of the **FRONT OPENING** climbing from the
placket's top at z 70 to z 85, the placket, the three buttons, both chest
pockets, the waistband, the back centre seam and every trouser line. The
front opening now ends in open air at 85 rather than meeting a band; on the
page it reads as a lapel V, which is what it is. **`YOKE`, `YOKE_N`,
`yokeAt`, `yokeH`, `TOR_HB`, `NCK_LZ`, `NCK_R` and `HEAD_RINGS` did not
move** — no proportion changed and the 16.7 units of visible neck L5 won are
still 16.7.

**2 · "the 'corners on the torso that face us can be removed" — `o.silo` on
the ribcage block and the pelvis; 4 edges off the page, 395 -> 391.** The
rule in `prepare()` was

```
ink = (f1 < 0) ? v0 : ((v0 !== v1) || (v0 && v1 && dot(n0, n1) < HARD))
```

— a silhouette clause and a crease clause. A prism's vertical edges are
90-degree creases (dot 0, far under `HARD` 0.65), so all four inked, and the
one between the two faces that both face the camera is the line down the
front of the chest. The rule is now

```
ink = (f1 < 0) ? v0
    : ((v0 !== v1) || (!P.o.silo && v0 && v1 && dot(n0, n1) < HARD))
```

and `taperP` is called with `{silo:1}` for both `tor` and `pel`. **`HARD` is
unchanged at 0.65 and no other primitive carries the flag**, so the yoke, the
deltoids, the arms, the legs, the boots, the head, the conveyor, the rollers
and the three workpieces ink exactly as they did in v5 — the flag is opt-in
and two prisms opted in.

Measured by rebuilding the page with the flag disabled and counting: **395
inked edges with the crease clause live, 391 with it suppressed on those two
prisms, at rest; 394 -> 390 in the orbit.** Four edges, both views — ONE per
prism per figure, which is the arithmetic a convex prism gives you when its
top and bottom are buried and two of its four side faces face the camera.
Nothing else went. The silhouette clause is untouched, so the outline of the
chest and the hips is drawn exactly as before and the figure keeps its width;
what left the page is the interior crease. Checked on BOTH figures in BOTH
views: A is seen from behind and B from the front, and neither shows a box
corner in either.

The whole-page stat is **396 -> 391**. The fifth edge is not the silo: it is
the mitt, which turns on its own axis when the grip moves and presents one
fewer silhouette edge at this camera.

**3 · "hide the box-hands behind the box" — GRIP_X = WK_D - 32 = 72,
GRIP_Z = 194, and a new probe to prove it.** B's reach was solved to
`[WK_D, 6, REACH_Z]` = the belt's own centre line at z 237. The red part
stands on the belt at 183 and is 42 tall, so its top face is at 225: the mitt
was solved twelve units ABOVE it and drawn over it. B stands on the belt's -y
side, which at this camera is the FAR side, so the grip moves ACROSS to the
part's far face — which stands at belt y -25 — and DOWN into the part's lower
band.

`window.lineGrip()` is the point of this part of the pass. Two things have to
be true at once for a box to hide a box in this pipeline and it checks both:
the mitt must sort BEHIND the part on centroid depth, which is what makes the
painter fill the part white over it, and every one of the mitt's eight
corners must project INSIDE the part's projected convex hull. `out_px` is the
largest distance any corner sticks outside that hull, in CSS px, so 0.00
means nothing of the hand shows in front of the red outline. It also reports
`into_part` — how far the mitt reaches inside the part's own half-spaces,
because this file's rule is that nothing interpenetrates — and `z_lo`, the
mitt's lowest corner, against the bed's top face at 180.

## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS

1. The oval was the coverall's COLLAR band, not a facet of the solid. With it
   gone the figure has a yoke seam across the back and a front opening up the
   chest, but no collar at all. Is no collar what you want, or should a
   collar come back as an ARC that stops at the shoulder line — the way the
   yoke seam already does — rather than a ring?
2. The front opening's two curves now climb to z 85 and stop in open air,
   because the band they used to meet is gone. Should they stop lower, at the
   top of the chest, or is a lapel V ending at the neck right?
3. `o.silo` is opt-in and only the ribcage and the pelvis have it. The boot's
   sole block and the four conveyor legs are prisms too and still ink their
   front corners. Do those read as boxes as well, or is a box correct for a
   box and only the BODY should stop reading as one?
4. Suppressing the crease clause suppresses ALL of a prism's creases, not
   just the vertical ones. On the torso that costs nothing today because its
   top and bottom are buried. If the torso ever needs a horizontal crease —
   a chest shelf, a waist step — the flag would have to become a
   per-direction rule. Is silhouette-only the rule you want for the body from
   here on, or a crease-angle threshold tuned per solid?
5. The hand hides at rest and shows 4.03 px of wrist in the orbit. Is the
   orbit sliver acceptable, or should the grip go further round the part —
   which means either more of the mitt inside the part than 3.49, or a
   deeper part?
6. 3.49 units of the mitt are inside the red part. Physically that is a hand
   gripping a face and it cannot be seen, but this file's standing rule is
   that nothing interpenetrates. Is a grip allowed to enter what it grips, or
   should the mitt be held strictly outside and the 1.98 px of wrist at rest
   accepted instead?
7. The part is a plain box, so "behind it" is the only place a hand can hide.
   Would you rather the hand hid behind the part or came back on TOP of it
   with the part made taller, so the reach reads as lifting rather than
   steadying?
8. A's far hand is on his hip and his near hand leans on the rail; neither is
   near a box, so the note was read as being about B's reaching hand only.
   Did "box-hands" mean all four mitts — that the hand shape itself reads as
   a box and wants replacing — rather than the one overlapping the part?

## Findings

1. **rB's level warning, enacted.** Probe: `grep -c "level varied: nested tile grid" reviews/ROW-HEIGHT-RENDERS-2026-09-03-v1.html` → 1 (the one template string the page script stamps into all 24 column heads at load); `grep -c "outer wall grid-auto-rows is auto (level HELD)  ->  auto" notes/_subreports/assets/2026-09-03-245-L4-row-height-renders/drive-transcript.txt` → 64 (24 baseline + 40 across the control and mutation arms; zero non-`auto` readings). Canon's fixed outer rule (`canon.css:1091`) is real but the dashboard role overrides its unit to `auto` (`canon.css:17225`), so an "outer fixed" render would be a render of a state that does not ship; it is Question 2, not a column.
2. **The three columns are identical in content, and the drive proves it.** `wall markup byte-identical across the 3 columns of every frame (8 frames)` → PASS (innerHTML compared per frame). Only `data-model` on the column differs.
3. **Under FIXED, the shipped rail rung is a mis-rung already.** Rail tiles measure `[clientHeight, scrollHeight]` = `[184, 247]` and `[184, 200]` against the 184px rung, in all four themes, both modes, and at all four bands (band sweep, `band-sweep.json`). That is the 8 baseline FAILs (`FIXED: tiles fit the shipped rung (0 overrun) -> 2 overrun`, one per theme/mode). Under the shipped FLOOR the rows grow to 277.7 / 223.8 px and nothing overruns — the floor is doing load-bearing work in the shipped dashboard today, silently.
4. **Under FIXED the #217 dead band is measured, not remembered: 8px.** Chart group 380px vs rail group 2×184 + 4px gap = 372px; the outer `auto` row stretches the rail container to 380 and its fixed rows do not follow → `rail … dead band 8 px` in all 8 FIXED renders (flagged red on the page).
5. **FLOOR changes the chart's height too, not only the rail's.** Under `minmax(rung,auto)` the chart row renders at 505.5px (its rung is 380) because the outer row is sized by the rail group (277.7 + 223.8 + 4). The span vocabulary survives; the rung 380 is not what paints. Probe: `measurements.json`, `floor/chart tracks [505.5]` in all 8.
6. **`auto` and `1fr` as the floor's max are NOT the same render.** Control arm: `[col2=1fr] … rail every row track >= rung -> [277.7, 277.7]` and `chart -> [559.4]`, versus `auto` → rail `[277.7, 223.8]`, chart `[505.5]`. `1fr` equalises the rows of a group to its tallest content and lifts the whole outer row by 54px; `auto` grows each row to its own content. Both pass the "≥ rung" assertion (0 FAIL); the difference is the eye's, and it is Question 4.
7. **The mis-rung under the floor heals silently — and exposes the KPI headroom.** Mutation 2 (column 3 flipped to FLOOR through its own radio): `kpi every row track == wrong rung -> [155.8, 149.4]`, chart `[505.5]`, rail `[277.7, 223.8]`, `0 tiles` overrun — column 3 becomes visually column 2, and the KPI rows settle at 149–156px, i.e. the shipped 196px KPI rung carries ~40–46px of headroom that only a fixed or floor rung, never the content, produces. 56 assertions bit.
8. **Mutation 1 bites at the CSS, not the label.** Column 2 secretly forced to FIXED via an injected `!important` rule: 40 floor assertions FAIL (`computed grid-auto-rows == minmax(380px, auto) -> 380px`; `rail dead band <= 1px -> 8px`; `rail no tile overruns -> 2`), reverted → 0 FAIL. Mutation 3: pointing at column 1 in the console/dark frame yields an export whose line 4 is `pointed at: column 1 — FIXED unit` and whose penultimate line is the question (`export-sample.txt`).
9. **Zoom is an instrument caveat, declared with its size.** The fit view scales a 1440px stage to the column at zoom 0.306.

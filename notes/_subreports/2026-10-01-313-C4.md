## [spec] choices changed or added, as § 1 of the spec asks a lane to say
1. `chooser/choose.py` is a fifth file beside the spec's four: it joins entitle, evaluate and rank into the `choose()` tool. 
2. Entitlement is per data item, not per part. The spec's two sentences — "a part whose tool was refused is out before choosing" and the analyst's expected screen, which both refuses the cash position (`metric`) and shows `metric` for the five payments in repair — only hold together if what is refused is the item (the cash position), and the `not_chosen` row names the part it would have taken. That part is named from the item's contract (its shape and declared facts), never from its data.
3. The request carries `asks`: the items the agent wants on the screen for the question. The agent's planning is mocked in `mock/requests.json`; the chooser does not decide what to ask for.
4. A tool's output depends on the role's scope (spec § 6: "payments awaiting approval (full) or the region's queue by stage (status only)"), so each data item names its tool and scope, and a scope mismatch is refused `scope-not-granted`. The treasurer's positions at `all-entities` return the cash position and the 30-day series; balances by company are what positions returns at `summary` (the relationship manager's), which keeps the treasurer's screen at the four tiles § 9 expects.
5. `mock/roles.json` `supplies`: `dashboard` supplies `one-state × dot-and-label` (the cut-off, derived from the clock, spec § 6) and `ancestor-path × links` (the call-22 fixture); `payments_queue` also supplies `one-measure × value-and-delta` (the five in repair); `records` stands for the record lists, which have no shape in `shapes.json`.
6. The `choose()` output keeps the spec's keys and adds `frame`, `chosen`, `tiles` (per item: context, part, reasons, its own ranked eligible list and its gate-false list, the metric reading, settings, payment flags) and `catalogue`. `eligible` is the flat list of every tile's eligible rows, each with its `item`.
7. The data picks its part from every published part that provides a role; the wall then accepts or refuses it by what it provides. A pick is not re-routed to the best tile when the wall refuses it.
8. The ranker's time limit is 200 ms; the spec names a limit, not a number.
9. Two rows are added to the W table beside R5's twenty and kept out of its count: W3b (the with-trend reading as the spec's alias rule states it) and W18b (R5's grid row with `needs = sort`). Two vocabulary moves in the table are rulings, not fits: `chart-panel` → `chart` (`s308-D41`), and stat-card / kpi-tile expectations → metric without / with its spark (`s308-D42`).

## RENDERS — six, all read back by eye before being cited

Render-verify runbook § SIXTH STRATUM, driven end-to-end. **One correction to the recipe, worth
carrying:** `TMPDIR=/var/tmp` **fails** — `/` is at 100% and playwright's `mkdtemp` ENOSPCs inside
`launch()`, reading as `BrowserType.launch: ENOSPC`. **`TMPDIR=/dev/shm` is what works** (512 MB
tmpfs, 0% used). Also: **`/dev/shm` does not survive a bash-call boundary** — it is a fresh tmpfs per
call — so scripts must live on the mount and `mkdir -p /dev/shm/fccache` must be re-run every call.

Working env, all in one bash call:

```
export PYTHONPATH=/var/tmp/pylibs
export PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=/var/tmp/pw-browsers-220
export LD_LIBRARY_PATH=/sessions/<session>/mnt/outputs/syslibs/usr/lib/aarch64-linux-gnu
export TMPDIR=/dev/shm                       # NOT /var/tmp — see above
export FONTCONFIG_FILE=<scratch>/fonts.conf  # farm + cachedir /dev/shm/fccache
```

## REPLAY-THESE

- ⚠ **`TMPDIR=/var/tmp` is dead** while `/` is full — playwright's `launch()` ENOSPCs on `mkdtemp` and
  reports it as a launch failure. **`TMPDIR=/dev/shm`.** Worth a line in the render-verify runbook
  beside the SIXTH STRATUM block, because the reading points at chromium rather than at the disk.
- ⚠ **`/dev/shm` is per-bash-call**, not merely per-session — a script written there is gone by the
  next call. Scripts go on the mount; `/dev/shm` is for temp the browser makes itself.
- ⚠ **A segmented specimen without the page's `moveInd` script renders `.ind` at width 0** — a
  silently thumb-less control that still looks plausible. Copying the markup is not enough; the
  behaviour script is part of the artefact.
- ★ **The picture is what caught both defects on this lane** (the unstyled search mock, the invisible
  thumb). Neither would have failed any gate, and neither was visible in the HTML. *Read the PNG
  before citing it* earned its keep twice in one lane.

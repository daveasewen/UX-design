## What changed
- **One source:** `knowledge/canon/click-or-tab.js`. It guards itself (`window.__clickOrTab`), so a page that carries several copies runs it once. Its listeners sit on window in the capture phase. Tab marks the page keyboard. A mouse press, a pen or a touch marks it pointer. It listens for `pointerdown` as well as `mousedown` because the video seek bar cancels pointerdown, and that suppresses the mousedown.
- **Distribution:** a new behaviour-only group in `knowledge/component-types.json`, `"click-or-tab"`, follows the ADR-0015 behaviour-partial precedent (dv-behaviour). `gen_component_partials.py` injects the source between `AUTO-BEHAVIOUR click-or-tab` markers before `</body>`. The 17 hand copies are deleted (`notes/_lanes/316/CT/migrate.py`).
- **Composed pages:** step 5 of `apollo-spider/skills/generate-from-canon/SKILL.md` and step 2 of `knowledge/_RUNBOOK-compose-from-canon.md` now say to load `knowledge/canon/click-or-tab.js` once beside canon.css. `dashboards/international-banking-dashboard.canon.html` now does.
- **Two parts that failed on their own** (LA's evidence) now get the pointer rule the fields have: the filter bar's chip remove button (`.tag .x`) and the video seek bar (`.scrub`).
- **Gate, so the copy cannot come back:** `gen_component_partials.py --check` reads two new group keys.
  - `$noHandCopies` refuses a hand-written flag setter anywhere in `knowledge/snippets/*.reference.html` outside AUTO-BEHAVIOUR markers.
  - `$membersIfCss` refuses a snippet whose live CSS reads `data-modality` but which is not a member, because that snippet would ship canon's rule with nothing to fire it.
  - The selftest has four new bites, and it is green.
- **Scope fixes found on the way:**
  - `_validate_behaviour.py` charged a member that sits in two groups with the other group's blocks (Template-dashboard and Template-report are in dataviz too). It now compares only its own group's blocks.
  - It also demanded exactly one resize listener per group. A group whose sources all declare `"$resize": "none"` now owes zero, and one listener still refuses.
  - Selftest bites were added and `_BEHAVIOUR-GATE.md` was refreshed.
  - `_compose_slice._token_tier_map` read every registry group as a token group, which would have minted `token:click-or-tab`. A group marked `$notATokenGroup` is now skipped. `gen_kg_tokens --check` stays in sync.

## Findings

1. **Where each of the six falls through — one line, from the code.** The page's draw predicate is
   `const EON=e=>famOn[FAMILY[e.type]]&&…` (`knowledge/_kg_explorer.template.html`, the `EON=` line).
   `FAMILY` is a literal object of type→family pairs. For a type that is not one of its keys,
   `FAMILY[e.type]` is `undefined`, `famOn[undefined]` is `undefined`, and `EON` is false — at every
   chip setting, for ever. The six lines that were missing from that object are exactly:
   `answersIntent` (28 lines, component→intent) · `hasDataShape` (26, component→shape) ·
   `providesRole` (108, component→role) · `yieldsTo` (45 = 43 component→component + 2 declared
   nulls) · `defaultActive` (1, icon→null, declared) · `obeys` (168 = 154 component→rule + 14
   component→ux). The same predicate gates the aside (`renderNode`'s `groups` loop), the dig
   (`applyFocus`'s `nb`) and the shortest path, so the relation was invisible in all four places.
   It was never suppressed and never declared — it was never addressed.
2. **SECOND fall-through, found by the probe and not in the brief: three node types had no chip.**
   Giving the edge a family lit nothing at first. `NODEON=n=>typeOn[n.type]&&famOK(n)`, and `typeOn`
   is built from `TYPES.concat(TYPES2)` — a list that named 20 of the graph's 23 node types.
   `shape` (23 nodes), `intent` (14) and `role` (12) were not in it, so `typeOn[t]` was `undefined`
   and the dots were not drawn whatever their edges said. Their palette entries `--c-shape`,
   `--c-intent`, `--c-role` have been in the stylesheet all along, so this was an omission, not a
   decision. Probe: the first render of v1.21-with-FAMILY-only drew **1,222** edges at the defaults
   where the census predicted 1,384 — the 162 missing were every line ending on one of those three
   types. The three names joined `TYPES`; the second render drew 1,384, which is the census figure
   to the digit. Lane OC's census could not have caught this: its `measure()` models the family
   chips and takes "every type chip is on" as given.
3. **THIRD defect, in INSPECT's edge door: `EFAMFILE` named the wrong file for every base wiring
   edge.** `const EFAMFILE={… rules:'knowledge/_rule_nodes.json' …}` — but `rules` is the BASE
   WIRING chip, whose edges are authored in the component metas, and `_rule_nodes.json` is the HSBC
   rule family, whose key is `guidelinerules`; the builder says so in `RULE_FAM`'s own comment
   (`# NOT 'rules': that family key is taken by the base wiring chip`). So `governedBy`, `consumes`,
   `triggeredBy`, `drivesConsumer` and `mustNotNeighbour` have each told the reader the wrong
   storage file since v1.20, and #281 would have put five more types through the same door. Fixed:
   the wrong value removed, `guidelinerules` added, and the "stored in" row now asks first whether
   the edge carries its own `fam` (read out of a family file) or not (a base edge, authored in its
   source's own record) — which is what makes `obeys`, family `guidelinerules` but stored in a
   component meta, name its meta. Probe, before: `providesRole` → `knowledge/_rule_nodes.json`.
   After: `knowledge/components/Chart-boxplot.meta.json`. Both in `inspect.json`.
4. **All six already had a verb; `_kg_verbs.json` needed no edit.** `answersIntent`, `hasDataShape`
   and `providesRole` all read through `provides-answers`; `yieldsTo` through `yields-to`; `obeys`

## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS

1. **Where is an obligation on a UX principle drawn?** The 14 `obeys`→`ux:` lines are held, not
   dropped, and the page says so. `s277-D8` fixed Design governance at three provenances (WCAG
   `sc:`, HSBC `rule:`, rulings a design cites) and a `ux:` target is none of them. Options:
   (a) a FOURTH provenance sub-chip inside the obligation box — cheapest, one chip, but it reopens
   a count Dave settled at three; (b) draw them in **Explanation**, where the `ux:` node already
   lives, as the one edge that enters that view from outside — honest about the force being the
   edge's, but it puts an obligation in the "why" view; (c) leave them held, permanently declared.
   Recommend **(a)**: the force is the edge's, not the node's (`s277-D8`'s own words), an obeys is
   an obligation wherever it points, and the obligation box is where obligations are read. Price:
   one line in `FAMILY` plus one sub-chip, about the size of this lane's own change.
2. **Should the five new System types be visible at the page defaults, or behind their own chip?**
   They landed in `rules`, the base wiring chip, which is ON, so the default canvas gained 205 lines
   and its md5 moved — the brief said it would. If Dave wants the default picture to stay as close
   to v1.1 as the additive families do, they could take a sixth System chip of their own, off by
   default. Recommend **as shipped**: these are the component's own wiring, authored in the
   component's own meta, and the orphan plan's block 03 called this "the free win".

What the deck tail adds, the same three manners:

- **draw-in-view** — an `IntersectionObserver` at `threshold:0.05` on `#s4`
  (`ioW` / `secW`, named apart from the robots' `ioX` / `secEl`), plus the
  `visibilitychange` / `blur` / `focus` trio, with focus re-starting only if
  `#s4`'s rect is actually on screen. The lane file's `if (!reduce) start()`
  and its always-restart `visibilitychange` are dropped.
- **orbit spring that stays put** — carried over untouched from the lane file:
  same `YAW_R` / `PIT_R`, same critically damped `updateView`, same
  `pointermove` + synthetic-`mousemove` pair. Both scenes keep their listeners
  inside their own IIFE, so nothing is shared and nothing collides.
- **print snap** — `snap()` bakes the resting frame to `#lwPrint`, preserving
  and restoring `yawOff` / `pitOff` so the orbit does not jump; wired to
  `beforeprint`, to the `print` media query, and to `setTimeout(snap, 1200)`,
  the pattern every other drawing uses. Exposed as `window.lwSnap`.
- **resize** — a 160 ms debounce on its own `rt`, inside the IIFE.

What was deliberately NOT ported: the lane instrumentation `window.linePage()`,
`window.lineGrip()` and `window.lineClear()`, and the lane card's caption. The
rest of the probe surface is kept, namespaced `lw*` so it cannot collide with
the robots' `line*`: `lwStats`, `lwView`, `lwPose`, `lwAxisRuns`, `lwOrder`,
`lwStill`, `lwSetTime`, `lwSetMouse`, `lwStop`, `lwStart`, `lwSnap`. Verified
at runtime: `typeof window.linePage === 'undefined'` (likewise `lineGrip`,
`lineClear`); `lwStats` and `lineStats` are both functions and return
different scenes.

**Namespacing, checked.** ids `lw` / `lwPrint` vs `ln` / `lnPrint`; observers
`ioW`/`secW` vs `ioX`/`secEl`; print media query `mqW` vs `mqX`; resize timer
`rt` is IIFE-local in both; snap hooks `lwSnap` vs `lnSnap`. Every helper the
two scenes share by name (`add`, `sub`, `scl`, `mkPrim`, `sx`, `sy`, `render`,
`size`, `still`, `start`, `stop`, `snap`) is `var`/`function` inside its own
IIFE and never reaches `window`.

**Untouched:** slide 5 in full — `#ln`, `#lnPrint`, its IIFE, its copy and its

## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS

1. The gearbox is now displaced from the deck entirely — slide 4 was the only
   card it drew on, and nothing else consumes `gbPrint`. Is the gearbox
   retired, or does it want a card of its own somewhere in the story?
2. Slide 4's copy still reads "An assembly line is only as fast as its parts
   bin, the skill of the assembly engineer, and the rigour of QC" — written
   against a gearbox. It was kept verbatim per the brief. Does it want
   re-cutting now that the picture beside it is two people at a conveyor?
3. Slides 4 and 5 are now the same scene twice — the same conveyor, the same
   plate, the same red workpiece, the same 3/4 view — with the arms swapped
   for figures. That is exactly what was asked for ("so two drawings one with
   humans and one with robots"). Is the sameness the point, and should the two
   cards sit at the identical camera so the swap reads as a substitution, or
   should 4 shift its view a little so the two do not look like a render bug?
4. Slide 4's workers face the belt from the far side; the robots on 5 stand
   on both sides. Should the two be composed to the same staging?
5. `#lw` has no callout and no scale bar, matching the robots. Does slide 4
   want a figure caption of the kind the lane card carried?

Nothing here has been shown to Dave beyond the v6 drawing itself; none of the
above is settled.

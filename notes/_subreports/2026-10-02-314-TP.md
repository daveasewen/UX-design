## 1. The premise — what "a valid 'element' of the design system" must mean

Dave's words (s313-D72 comment, verbatim): "We need to regenerate the templates too BTW, everything in the library should be a valid 'element' of the design system". The phrase is his, not a defined term. Read against the tree it can mean one of two things.

**Reading A — a template is a component like any other.** It stays a self-contained snippet (its own theme blocks, its own CSS, projected into canon.css under `.cn-template-*`), and "valid" means it passes the snippet gates and sits in the gallery. This is the reading the state row's close condition leans on ("passes the gates every component passes, and the canon gallery can show them"). Its strongest case: every one of the 125 other snippets is built this way; the partials tier (ADR-0013) exists precisely so a snippet can borrow an atom's rules without sharing classes at runtime ("Runtime class-sharing stays REJECTED inside the KB — source-of-truth inversion"); and the twelve already passed the snippet gate, the a11y gate, the icon gate and the coverage gate at HEAD. Under this reading the only defect was the gallery break.

**Reading B — a template is a PAGE, and a page is made only of parts.** The record says this three times in Dave's own words and once in the compose runbook. s305-D57 (half A, his "yes"): "THE PAGE NEVER SIZES A PART, WIDTH INCLUDED — a page's own style may place a part (which tile, which span, which order) and nothing else". s307-D75 (his click, verbatim 'Rebuild the shared stylesheet'): "The shared stylesheet is rebuilt (a page places parts and never sizes them), rather than carrying the rules on the page". s313-D46 (#313 A6, kept by him): the twelve "are whole composed pages". And `knowledge/_RUNBOOK-compose-from-canon.md`: "a composed screen cannot silently drift because it has no component CSS of its own … The screen's own `<style>` is harness only — no `#hex`, no `.c-*`/`.cn-*` redefinitions." The #288 handoff is the motive: he called the template path "a dot-to-dot book" because the agent traced an existing file instead of composing from the graph.

Measured on the tree at HEAD, the twelve are neither. They are pages built as snippets by copying: of 1,542 CSS rules across the twelve, 1,218 are copies of other components' rules (the `.btn` ladder, Form-layout's whole field language, Data-grid's toolbar and table, Summary, Timeline, Stepper, Secure-entry, Selection-controls, Segmented-control, Breadcrumbs, the nav shell). Their own headers say so: "COMPOSED FROM, declaration-for-declaration", "class names KEPT so a diff proves the borrow". canon.css then projected each copy under `.cn-template-*` — 2,113 rules in canon that are a second home for parts that already have one, which is the drift vector the compose runbook names. Reading A cannot make that valid: a snippet is self-contained by design, so under A a template can only ever be a copy. I took Reading B. A valid element of the design system, for a template, is a page whose snippet links canon.css, places each part as its own `.cn-<slug>` scope with the part's own current markup, carries placement rules only in its own style, binds the few tokens the page itself reads, and carries a meta that passes the schema like any other component. Its "element" in canon is then its placement grammar alone (3 to 16 rules each, against 92 to 256 before).

Two consequences I want stated rather than discovered. (1) The templates are now the first snippets that `<link>` canon.css; one gate read a linked sheet's own `var()` uses as the page's and went red for all eleven — fixed in the gate with two new bites (§5). (2) A page cannot restyle a part, so where a part is wrong at rest the template now shows it; those are listed as library findings (§6), not patched on the page.

**The gallery cause was misdiagnosed.** #313 A6 reported "two of them bring their own page styles in the body". Measured: `gen_gallery.py`'s body locator `re.search(r"<body[^>]*>(.*?)</body>")` ran on raw bytes, and in Template-auth (line 71) and Template-wizard (line 56) the first literal `<body` sits inside a `<head>` prose comment ("the face arrives via `<body class="t-ed-body">`"). The match began in the head and swept the whole `<style>` into the section: 65 and 77 hexes, which is A6's 145 minus the three real ones. A locator defect of the `gen_token_ramp` #211 class, not a template defect. Fixed (`snippet_body()`, comment-masked locate, raw slice). With that fix and the rebuilt eleven, a trial gallery with all twelve appended passes compose (144 canon classes, 0 rogue hex, 0 sized parts), icon-source and a11y: the gallery CAN show them. The shipped gallery stays component-only, which is what Dave chose by click (s313-D72).

## 3·P. Affordance parity — every affordance, state and specimen in the BEFORE render, and where it is in the AFTER

## WHAT LANDED

**1 · "set the shoulders down the body a little" — SH_LZ 88 -> 76, yoke crest
97 -> 88.** Twelve units, a fifth of a head. The pivot was ON the proportion
sheet's shoulder plane (1.29 heads from the crown), which put the arm root
level with the base of the neck; at 76 the deltoid's widest point is below the
top of the shoulder mass, which is where a shoulder joint is. **It could not
go the full third of a head (18.7).** The yoke's bottom ring IS the ribcage
block's own rectangle at `TOR_HB = 72`, and the block's top is fixed by the
coverall's chest pockets, which top out at z 69; dropping TOR_HB to 58 would
have left the pockets drawn on a face that no longer exists. So the block did
not move, the yoke's two lowest rings did not move, and the drop was taken out
of the yoke's TOP instead. **The lap improved.** The shoulder plane is now
z 76, where the torso is ay 39.65 (v4 measured ay 35.0 at z 88), against an
arm whose inboard surface is still `SH_Y - DEL_R` = 31.4: a lap of **8.25**,
against v4's 3.6. `SH_Y` did not move. Every arm axis is still outside its own
ribcage — 6.4, 3.1 on A and 6.3, 5.5 on B, all positive.

**2 · "the first body is clashing with the conveyor" — WK_DA 126 -> 190, and a
new probe to prove it.** v4's `lineClear()` said A stood 14.9 clear and it was
telling the truth in three dimensions. The drawing is flat. At this camera
(yaw -35, pitch 20) the bed is 560 long and fills the frame, and A stands on
the CAMERA side of it, so his silhouette lands on the belt's projected band
whatever the 3D air between them. **`window.linePage()`** measures it where
the reader sees it: every worker primitive and every conveyor primitive gets
its vertices projected with the live view matrix, a 2D convex hull, and a SAT
separation — negative is page overlap in CSS px, positive is the gap. The hand
is excluded, as in `lineClear()`, because it is meant to touch.

Stepping back carries A down and left on the page while the belt's projected
band at his new screen column rises, so the two separate at about **0.42 px of
page gap per unit of standoff**. Swept at 126, 145, 160, 175, 190, 205:

**3 · "I don't think we need the flat facet on top of the shoulders" — a pole
on the yoke, a rounder crown on the deltoid.** Two solids, two different
faults.

The **YOKE's** top ring was 13.5 x 20.0 at z 97. That is a real ring, so
`latheP` capped it with a POLYGON, and v4's claim that the cap was "buried
inside the neck" could not hold: a 27 x 40 plate does not hide inside a
20-diameter neck. That plate's rim IS the flat facet in the crop. The yoke now
runs to a **POLE at z 88** — one vertex, a triangle fan, no cap polygon and no
rim to ink — exactly as v3 closed the head for Dave's "we don't need the flat
facet of the top of the head". The five rings between the widest ring and the
pole are an **ellipsoid cap** on that ring: `ax = 22.60*f`, `ay = 40.00*f`
with `f = sqrt(1-u^2)`, u the fraction of the cap's 10-unit height, and e
eased 4.6 -> 2.1 across it, so the curvature is even and nothing inks across
the rings either. The crest falling 97 -> 88 is what makes the neck read: the
trapezius SLOPES into it instead of plateauing at it, and the neck's own
cylinder (r 10) emerges at about z 87.3 rather than z 97 — **16.7 units of

## RULING-SHAPED QUESTIONS

1. The shoulder pivot came down 12, not the 18-19 that a full third of a head
   would be, because the ribcage block's top and the chest pockets under it
   are what the yoke is built on. Is 12 enough, or should the pockets and the
   block's top move down too so the shoulders can go the rest of the way?
2. Worker A's reaching arm is now STRAIGHT — at a 190 standoff the belt's
   near edge is past his reach, so he leans on the rail rather than resting a
   bent hand on the belt. Is a straight leaning arm the right read, or should
   he come back in to about 165, where the elbow bends again but the belt
   crosses him at the waist rather than the hip?
3. At 190 worker A covers the UPSTREAM ROLLER completely in the resting view:
   the conveyor's left end now terminates in a plain bed face with no
   cylinder. Does the belt still read as a belt from one roller, or should the
   roller be uncovered again — which means moving him along the belt, not
   across it?
4. Page overlap cannot go to zero at this camera; A's legs still cross the
   conveyor's legs and base plate, correctly, in front of them. Is crossing at
   the hip and below the acceptable line, or is any crossing of the bed a
   clash?
5. The yoke's crest coming down to a pole at 88 gives 16.7 units of visible
   neck where v4 had 7. Is that the amount of neck you want, or has it gone
   the other way and become long?
6. The deltoid's crown is rounder but `DEL_H` and `DEL_R` did not move, so the
   sleeve head still sits just UNDER the trapezius rather than proud of it.
   With the shoulder now 12 lower there is room for the arm mass to stand
   proud, as a padded coverall would. Do you want that, or is under right?
7. `linePage()` is a new kind of probe for this file: it measures the drawing,
   not the model. Should the other lanes' clearance claims be re-stated in
   page pixels too, or is 3D clearance the right language everywhere else?
8. The collar band is at z 85 now, a fraction of the dome rather than a fixed
   height. If the shoulders move again it will move again. Is riding the dome
   the right rule for the garment seams, or should the collar keep a fixed
   distance below the chin?

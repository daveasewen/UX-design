David Rice
Chief AI Officer, HSBC

Compiled 2026-09-08 (#257) from public sources. Demo audience corrected from "Sutherland" (a #246 inference) on Dave's word.
01

Who he is

HSBC's first Chief AI Officer, effective 1 April 2026. Nearly 20 years at HSBC (joined 2006); previously COO of Corporate & Institutional Banking, earlier COO of Commercial Bank (US). Operations-and-transformation background, not a technologist by trade — he was promoted from the business side, which is unusual: most banks fold AI under the CTO.

Remit: enterprise-wide AI adoption — gen-AI tools to all staff to simplify processes, procedures and policies; AI capability for customer-facing colleagues to deliver personalised service in real time at scale. CTO Mario Shamtani owns the platform underneath (core modernisation, a central AI platform giving colleagues access to a range of models, strategic partnerships).

02

What he is measured on

Elhedery has tied gen-AI to the group RoTE target of >17% for 2026–28, through automation and efficiency. 85% of staff already have gen-AI productivity tools. Reports in March put up to 20,000 job cuts (~10%) under review, mostly non-client-facing service-centre roles. Rice's job is to turn tool access into measurable process change — and to do it with "human judgement, decision-making and accountability at the core" (Elhedery's framing, repeated).

Verdict
He will judge Apollo as an operator, not a designer.
  • Lead with throughput. One prompt → a compliant dashboard, in one shot, is exactly his metric shape (process simplified, colleague equipped). Show the frozen prompt running cold; the gates catching a real defect will land harder than any pixel.
  • Governance is a selling point, not a caveat. Rulings store, gates that refuse, provenance on every claim — that is the "safely, with accountability" clause made mechanical. Name it in his language: auditability, controlled scale.
  • Platform fit. He does not own the model platform; Shamtani does. Expect "which models, where does it run, how does it plug into the central AI platform." Have a one-line answer ready (model-agnostic engine + skills pack; runs where the designer's tools run).
  • Sutherland matters to him only as scale. Component-library fidelity is the designer's concern; for Rice it is "does the output land in the real codebase without rework." Frame the library binding that way.
  • Avoid design-system vocabulary as the headline, token/typography detail up front, and anything that reads as a pilot. He was hired to move past pilots.
03

Open questions to settle before the room

  • Who else is in the room (his team, Shamtani's, design leadership)? Changes how much platform depth to carry.
  • Is the ask sponsorship, a pilot site, or a platform slot? The one-shot dashboard proves capability; the ask decides the close.
  • Any HSBC constraint on external LLM use for design artefacts — a likely first governance question.

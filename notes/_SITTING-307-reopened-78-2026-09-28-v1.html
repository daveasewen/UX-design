How this sitting works

Ten themes, quickest first

The themes run from the quickest to settle to the ones that most need your eye. The first four are mostly old machinery that can close in a click. The last six are design calls: colours, sizes, parts, charts and the dashboard.

  1. Old release checks, now history

    11 questions. Questions left over from building the early releases (v1.0.1 and v1.0.2). Those releases shipped long ago, so most of these can close in a click.

  2. How each session is recorded and started

    9 questions. The wrap that ends a session, the record it leaves, and how much a new session has to read to start. Your wrap redesign at #306 settles or overtakes several of these.

  3. Which checks become rules

    8 questions. Checks that only warn, or that nobody has decided how far to trust. Most are Claude's to settle inside your rules; a few want your word.

  4. What goes in the designers' pack

    8 questions. What ships to your designers, under what licence, and how the frozen copies are kept.

  5. Theme colours and captions

    6 questions. Caption grounds, chords and colour defaults across the four themes. One question here is truly yours: mono's dark caption.

  6. Sizes, corners and spacing

    6 questions. The segmented control, corner radii, and the row heights on the bento dashboard. Your later re-rules cover much of this.

  7. Parts waiting for your yes or no

    9 questions. Components built but never promoted, and questions about how they behave. Several want pictures before you rule; the recommendations say which.

  8. Charts

    8 questions. Stacked charts, fitting charts to their space, and the newer chart types. Two cards ask the same stacked-area question.

  9. The dashboard

    8 questions. What the dashboard shows above the fold, your own improvements to it, and the test of whether a fresh session can reach your result.

  10. Building pages from parts

    5 questions. How a page takes its parts: linked or pasted, and where the styles live. Plus the bento editor, which is not built yet.

Fills every card you have not answered. You can change any of them.

## 4 · Recommendation for the redraw

**Build a new self-contained HTML. Do not re-run `_build_kg_diagram.py` and ship its output.**

Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **The existing generator draws the wrong graph.** It reads *only* `graph-index.json` — 38 SCs ×
   134 components × 826 compliance edges. The KG "as it is now" is the **component KG: 888 nodes,
   1,261 edges, 5 node types, 16 edge types**, and the generator cannot see a single one of them. Re-running
   it gives Dave a *fresher stale picture*, not the redraw he asked for. This is the whole reason to
   start again.

2. **The story the compliance diagram tells is the wrong story for Rice.** Its punchline is *"only
   4 of 38 claims are actually verified"* — a picture of a **hole**. That was exactly right for an
   internal edge-triage conversation on 2026-07-19. In front of a CAIO whose brief is *"turn tool
   access into measurable process change"*, opening on our own unverified gap is a self-inflicted
   wound. The component KG tells the opposite story with the same drama: **1,261 authored relations
   the machine can traverse, none of them invented.** Same visual language, inverted punchline.

3. **Nothing is lost by starting fresh, because the good part is transplantable.** What makes the
   July diagram work is not its code — it is three moves: **hover-to-trace** (dim everything not
   connected), **colour-as-verdict** (the node's fill *is* the finding), and **inline data, zero
   deps**. All three are ~60 lines of vanilla SVG/JS, already written, in a file we can read.
   Lifting them costs less than adapting the generator's bipartite column layout to a five-type
   graph it was never shaped for.

**On d3 from cdnjs — allowed, but I recommend against it.** The existing diagram's power is that it
is *deterministic*: the same data draws the same picture every time, which is what makes it safe to
put in a deck and safe to screenshot. A force simulation settles differently on every load, can't be
reasoned about at 888 nodes without heavy tuning, and — decisively — **a force-directed hairball at
this size reads as noise, not as structure.** The compliance diagram is dramatic *because* it is
legible: two ordered columns, and you can follow one thread with your eye. Keep that discipline.

**Suggested shape (for Dave's word, not a decision):**

- **A deterministic radial or layered layout**, not a force sim: components on an arc, patterns and
  contexts on outer rings, snippets and rulings as terminals. Deterministic seed, no library.
- **Colour by edge type**, with the 16 types collapsed to four families the room can hold:
  *composition* (`hasPart` / `composedOf` / `containedBy` / `partial`), *usage* (`usedInContext` /
  `commonPattern` / `family` / `groupsWith`), *contract* (`consumes` / `reuses` / `renderedBy` /
  `drivesConsumer` / `delegatesTo` / `triggeredBy`), *governance* (`governedBy` / `mustNotNeighbour`).
- **Keep hover-to-trace verbatim** from the July file. It is the move that makes people lean in.
- **One live number on the face of it:** 138 components · 1,261 relations · 0 invented. That is
  Rice's metric shape, not a designer's.
- **The reveal, if a beat is wanted:** start with one component lit, then let its edges bloom
  outward. The ↻ loop from the designer-community deck is the same idea and the two slides merge.

**Prerequisites and cautions before any build:**

- ⛔ `_validate_kg.py` is **FAIL** (§2d). Four fails are `filter-toolbar-bar.meta.json` declaring
  unregistered edge keys. If the redraw counts `composedOf` / `delegatesTo` / `drivesConsumer` as
  real edges — and it should, they exist in the data — the diagram will be drawing edges the gate
  says are not legal. **Name this before showing it, or get the schema registered first.**
- ⛔ **Never run `gen_kg_edges.py`** to "refresh" the data. It wipes and rebuilds every `edges`
  block, and its resolutions input is already stale (the fifth fail). The redraw should **read** the
  metas, never regenerate them.
- ⚠ Accent must be the ruled two-red value (**`#DA1A00`** on white, `#F6604C` elsewhere, s151-D1),
  not the decks' inherited `#DB0011`.
- ⚠ The 85 `ref:null` edges are *declared but unresolved*. Either draw them as dashed/ghost
  (honest, and it makes the "awaiting a human ruling" point beautifully) or exclude them and say
  1,176 rather than 1,261. Do not silently mix.
- ⚠ Every component figure in the August deck (75 / ~135 / ~300) is **unratified** and the "~135
  near future" is already overtaken by today's 138.

## Not done / unsure

- **The `mentions` count is 284, not "~150"** as the brief estimated, and only **78** carry a
  proposedType, so the ratification page has 78 rows. I did not widen the 80-char window or the
  verb list to reach 150 — the brief named both, and a wider net would be my invention.
- **Found, not fixed (outside the fence):** a base component node whose id is referenced by
  another component's edge BEFORE its own meta is read keeps the SLUG as its label, because
  `extract()`'s `add()` passes `label` positionally and `setdefault` never overwrites it. 43
  component names (Button, Cards, Banner, Form layout, Tabs…) are affected. It is a v1.0 bug,
  cosmetic, and fixing it would move labels while the chips are off — so I left it and matched
  `applies_to` against the meta files' own `name` field instead of against node labels. Worth a
  one-line fix in a later lane (`n['label'] = label or n['label']` inside `add`).
- **Layout chrome moved:** the legend grew a third row, so `.scrub` went from `bottom:104px` to
  `bottom:158px` and the legend gained a `max-width`. Chrome only; no data or interaction changed.
- **Approximation, stated in the script docstring:** only the NODES of the two new families are
  dated from history. Their edges take `born` = the later born of their two ends and die with the
  first of them — the day a ruling first named a particular artefact is not recorded anywhere.
- **Not snapshotted into the day list:** `knowledge/compliance/rules` is read at each snapshot but
  does not contribute days, so an SC-only commit adds no snapshot. That is what keeps the count 51.
- `session`/`artefact`/`evidence` and the guidelines scaffolding nodes inherit their born date from
  the ruling or SC that introduced them; a ruling's first day is standing in for theirs.
- Generator for the ratification page was a one-off in the session scratchpad, not committed —
  the page is the deliverable and the derivation lives in `_build_kg_explorer.extract_extra()`.

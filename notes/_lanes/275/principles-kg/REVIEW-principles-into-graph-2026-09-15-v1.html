Decisions

Six decisions. The recommendation is first in each.

Nothing here is ruled. Every option stays open; the marked option is the lane's recommendation and carries no weight beyond that.

RP-1

The node, and what it carries

A register row becomes one node: ux:<id>, e.g. ux:pr-fitts. 145 nodes. The prefix is not in question — s269-D2 ruled it, and this lane re-measured it free: the live graph carries 20 prefixes and ux: is not one of them, while principle: and guideline: are WCAG's. What is open is the payload.

Why (a): the register exists because a principle read without its scope conditions is a principle misread — Fitts taken as ‘make buttons big’ is the register's own example. A node that carries the statement and drops the misreadings reproduces the problem. The cost is 123 KB against an explorer page that is already 2.9 MB.

RP-2

Which edge types land in wave 1

Six edge types are measured: 111 edges across 175 nodes. Nothing is invented — an unresolvable target is null plus the evidence, and there are 27 of those.

Why (a): every one of the 111 is read from a field or from a generated file with a declared derivation rule. Nothing is parsed out of prose, which is what made cites the weak one at #274. (c) saves three words but spends s238-D6, which ruled the link typed precisely so that the type carries the claim. (d)'s evidencedBy is the one type that already exists — and 0 of its 53 URLs join a live evidence: node, so the word would come to mean a chat receipt in one family and a journal citation in another.

RP-3

The polarity: a node, or only an attribute on an edge

30 polarities. 18 of them already derive a pairwise edge into _generated/polarity-edges.json, and this lane re-derived all 22 from polarities.json and got the same set — the view is fresh, proved, not assumed. The either/or the #269 inventory left open is whether the polarity itself is a node.

Why (a): s238-D1 already ruled the shape — the polarity's home is a node with N typed parties that must resolve and typed out-links, and the pairwise edges are derived, never authored. This is that model, in the graph. It also passes the s274-D9 test rather than dodging it: a hub that carries nothing earns no edge, and this one carries a mediating-variable sentence, a derived status, 21 typed citations and 17 parties no pair edge can express.

RP-4

The 20 principles that restate a standard we already hold

20 rows sit in standards families — fam-wcag22 11, fam-coga 6, fam-aria-apg 1, fam-en301549 1, fam-eaa 1 — and the graph already holds those standards as 4 principle:, 38 sc:, 12 guideline:, a standard: and a policy: node. Measured across all twelve fields: 0 rows name any of them. 4 ids do contain a WCAG principle's name — and that is a name match.

Why (a): a join drawn by matching the word ‘perceivable’ is the thing s274-D12 refused yesterday for 27 rule → component candidates, and the reason was good. (b) is defensible for the four, and is the honest follow-up in the shape RK-4 took — a first, then c.

RP-5

Component metas citing the six laws: which lane, and when

s269-D5 says a component's meta cites the principles it rests on, starting with the six A-grade laws. Measured: 0 of 137 metas names a pr- id, and 0 names any of the six laws verbatim. So this is authored work end to end, not a generator's. P-274-3 already parks the identical shape for rules (s274-D12's second half).

Why (a): the two are the same edit to the same file — a component meta gaining a citation array. Splitting them costs two schema diffs and two reviews for one change. Which components go first is yours; P-274-3 already records that.

RP-6

Where the nodes live, and who reads them

--land writes one new file, knowledge/_ux_principle_nodes.json, in the shape of _rule_nodes.json. Measured at run time, not asserted: _build_kg_explorer.py names _rule_nodes.json and does not name _ux_principle_nodes.json.

Why (a): #274 asked this exact question and the answer held — the rules chip is live in explorer v1.11 and reads a landed file. A landed file no builder names is an instrument without a consumer, which is the failure your RK-5 note put your finger on.

4 · The environment badge, and why it is the loudest thing here

Acting on user-acceptance data in the belief that it is production is the single most expensive mistake a banking console permits. Production is a quiet outlined chip, because a permanent alarm is no alarm. Every non-production environment takes the ruled amber fill with black ink — 9.16:1, mode-invariant, the “amber is always black text” rule. Use the Environment and System controls above; the chip and the dot are painted by CSS from the footer's own data-env and data-system attributes.

6 · Open, and yours

  1. Mint target/floor = 24px? The dial-down floor is ruled and is named verbatim in tokens/layout.json, but it has no leaf token — so this component hardcodes 24 and declares it, and every future dense component will do the same. Recommendation: mint it.
  2. Retire the doormat, or park it? Retired here, out of scope, with its markup and both its findings preserved in the meta under $retired. Recommendation: keep it retired; it comes back as its own component when a marketing pass needs it.
  3. The name. #261 made the collision with Action-bar worse, not better: the two now share a surface, a width and a one-row shape at the bottom of the same screen. Renaming is yours.
  4. Sticky's default. Off here. On a genuinely infinite grid the opposite argument is that the operator never sees the footer at all.

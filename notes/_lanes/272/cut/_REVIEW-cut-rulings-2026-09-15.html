what the systems said — verbatim

GOV.UK — Avoid using range slider questions, where the user needs to click and drag

Fluent 2 — Use a slider for imprecise values.

Atlassian — Don't use a range if choosing a specific value is important.

IBM Carbon — Do not use for extremely large ranges, for example, 1-1000.

Material 3 — Changes made with sliders must take effect immediately

Adobe Spectrum — used when the upper and lower bounds to the range are invariable.

NN/g — Sliders or knobs are continuous controls.

Apple HIG — Consider supplementing a slider with a corresponding text field and stepper.

USWDS — no slider component

OURS — slider.meta antiPatterns[2] (REVIEW, inferred): 'using a slider where precise numeric entry is required (pair with an input)'

GOV.UK rejects the interaction outright on accessibility grounds (WCAG 2.5.1 pointer gestures) and ships no slider. Six systems ship one with conditions. M3 adds a condition none of the others do: the change must take effect immediately — which would make a slider inside a submitted form illegal.

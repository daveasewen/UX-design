# Brief — CEO international-banking prototype (grill, 2026-09-26)

Source: the designer's prompt, SETTINGS block ("already decided, do not ask"). Nobody was available to answer further questions; every default below was taken and said out loud before the build.

## The six
1. Theme — Common (`data-apollo-theme="common"`), as stated.
2. Light, dark or both — both, with a theme switch, as stated.
3. Density and width — comfortable, wide desktop; overview on bento, as stated.
4. Brand assets — the supplied HSBC masterbrand and Apollo tokens; no other assets, as stated.
5. Data — placeholder: realistic entities and currencies, GBP reporting with explicit illustrative FX rates, 30-day series, enough rows to sort, filter and page, as stated.
6. Fixed / off-limits — Apollo accessibility defaults and existing patterns only; invent no components, variants, colours or icons; components at their own size; no live banking connections or credentials, as stated.

## Discovery (questions I would have asked, default taken)
- Which client group is the placeholder? — default: an invented "Northwind Group" with eight entities in five regions.
- Is the CEO an approver? — default: yes, one of two approvers; rejections need a reason; nothing is sent.
- Should "sub-pages" be separate files? — default: one page, ten hash-routed views (#/overview … #/settings), so filters and state are shared.
- Bento on sub-pages too? — default: each sub-page opens with a bento of charts on the grey ground, records below on the page ground.
- Dashboard bento — is that right? — skipped, so yes (rule 7a).
- discovery ended by designer after 0 questions (none could be asked).

# #267 lane K — KG explorer v1.9: the dig is a sphere again

**Dave's word:** "you can spin it but its just on a flat plane, it wast before". Confirmed and reverted.

## DONE

1. **Geometry named.** v1.8 (`6d3b61e`) placed halo neighbours on a **camera-plane ring** via
   `basis()` + `placeHalo()` (HEAD~ template `:283-287`), called from `tick()`. v1.9 restores
   v1.6's (`9ee4fb7`) **world-space sphere**: depth-1 `ph=((i%5)-2)*0.32`,
   `tx=cx+cos(a)cos(ph)r · ty=cy+sin(ph)r · tz=cz+sin(a)cos(ph)r`; depth-2 `r=R2*1.5, ph=((i%7)-3)*0.25`.
   `basis()`/`placeHalo()`/`HALO` deleted; `tick()` no longer re-places the halo per frame.
   Focus zoom restored to `R1*1.5 + (depth2?225:0) + 200`.
2. **Sector labels ride the same sphere.** `sectors[].dir` = the **mean world offset of that sector's own
   nodes × 1.25**, computed in `applyFocus()`; drawn as `proj(base(focus) + s.dir)`. No camera basis.
3. **Draw order.** v1.8's rule (halo dots above node-label plates) STAYS and is extended: the
   sector-label block now runs **before** the halo-dot pass — sector text was painting over node centres.
4. **Occlusion mitigation (stated, measured):** per-frame **screen-space radial nudge, capped at 12px**
   (`NUDGE_MAX=12`, `scr()` in `draw()`), applied to any halo dot whose projected centre lands inside the
   focus disc; a dot still inside after the nudge is painted **after** the focus. World positions untouched.
   The drawn centre is exported as `n._sx/_sy` so the sweep probes what is actually painted.
   No camera-plane ring re-introduced.

predicted is **real in v1.8 and moot in v1.9**: the sphere is world-space, so `mousemove`'s bare `draw()`
has nothing to re-place. No `placeHalo()` call was added.

D-2

Does chart-donut share the pie file?

The precedent is icon-button, which takes 6 of the buttons spec's 14 rules because that spec names icon-only as one of its three structural variations. The pie file names the doughnut harder than that: its '## Types' section lists Doughnut as one of two types, dv-pie-009's normative text reads 'both pie and doughnut', and dv-pie-003 is a rule that binds the donut alone. Measured the other way: chart-donut.meta.json already cites dv-pie-009 and dv-pie-010 by id in its own antiPatterns, and chart-pie.meta.json says it was ported from the donut with the inner radius dropped to 0. There is no family edge between them in the graph — 12 of 137 metas carry one, and neither chart does; what does exist is chart-pie's not-with and yieldsTo pointing at chart-donut.

Yes — chart-donut takes data-visualisation-pie-charts.md as its home spec because the file itself names the doughnut as one of its two types, so author all 11 pie rules on the donut and only 10 on the pie: dv-pie-003 (doughnut centre) binds the donut alone and stays off chart-pie.

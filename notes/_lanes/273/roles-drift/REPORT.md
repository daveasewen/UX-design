## Divergences from the brief, and why

Three figures in the brief's prose do not match the data. The brief's own ⛔ rule ("a number that is
not in DRIFT.json does not go on the page") decides it, so the page carries the computed value:

- **Legal `when` field names: the brief says 25, `legal_when_fields` has 26.** The page says 26 and
  lists all of them.
- **RD-2 option (a): the brief says "the 49 silent providers of the 9 roles".** 49 is right; the
  role count is **8** — `headline-metric` and `chart-panel` have zero silent metas, so excluding
  `record-list` and `input` leaves 8 roles, not 9. The page says 8; the 49 still sums correctly.
- **RD-3: "the 14 field names".** There are **14 entries across 9 metas but only 13 distinct
  names** — `entry` appears on both `input-fields` and `textarea`. The page states both figures.

Numbers the brief mentions that are NOT in `DRIFT.json` were **left off the page** rather than typed
by hand: `FAIL(6) → FAIL(20)`, the 15 inscribed `when`s of #272, and the "8–12 metas per lane"
sizing. RD-3 therefore says the resolver "gained reds at `b46ea90`" and names the older reds
(`data-grid` `with` entries without a `slug`, #261) without counting them. If Dave wants those
counts on the page, they need to come from a script that emits them.

**Gate 2 — numbers check.** `python3 notes/_lanes/273/roles-drift/_numbers_check.py` →
**29 distinct numbers checked, 2 FAIL (exit 1)**: `FAIL 404` and `FAIL 500`, both "not in DRIFT.json
and not derived". Every other number PASSES, including the three derived figures.

The two reds are a SCOPE DEFECT IN THE CHECKER, not a number typed by hand. Both come from the
single string `cause in (404, 500)` — the new `illegal_when_clauses` clause for `template-error`,
rendered verbatim in section 5. Gate 2 declares its scope as `headline, decisions, perrole`, but its
`Slice` HTMLParser counts a `<section>`'s depth on start tags and never sees an end tag for VOID
elements: the 15 `<input type="radio">` in `#decisions` leave `depth` permanently above zero, so the
slicer never closes and swallows sections 4, 5 and 6 as well. The leak was always there; it was
silent until now because before this rebuild `template-error`'s cell said "clause not carried in
DRIFT.json" and carried no digits. Proof: the extracted text contains section 6's headline
("Where every figure came from"), which is outside the declared scope.

The number itself is legitimate — `404` and `500` are characters of a string inside `DRIFT.json`,
and `allowed_from` only collects INTEGER values, so even in scope they would not match. Fixing the
checker (close void elements, and/or allow digits that appear inside a DRIFT.json string) is a
change to gate tooling that would turn a red green; it was NOT made here — the write fence for this
follow-up was `_build_page.py` and the output page. Named, not worked around.

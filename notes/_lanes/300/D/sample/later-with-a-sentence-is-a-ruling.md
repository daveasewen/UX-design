At #263 three of eleven items came back LATER. D7's note said "park — keep the doormat as a dormant variant"; D8's asked "is this the chart legend? if so leave it at 44"; D9's asked for a plain explanation. Reading each note back and answering the embedded question turned all three into rulings within one exchange ("dormant variant", "accept", "accept contract-only").

**Why:** the button he pressed was LATER; the words he typed were the ruling. His words outrank the widget, and filing the LATER would have carried a decided item as open (the [[conflated-fix-guarantees-recurrence]] shape in reverse).
**How to apply:** on any deferral with a note, restate the note as the decision it implies and ask for one word; give the plain explanation he asked for in the same reply. Related: [[decide-fast-dave-is-the-bottleneck-254]], [[eleven-defaults-ruled-tenth-release-gate-263]].

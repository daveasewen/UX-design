1

Bento spans are real

The board is a 12-column grid and every tile declares a span the meta already legalises: 3 for compact and default, 4 with a target, 6 for hero. Two breakpoints re-span rather than reflow, which is what s248-D3 asks for now that an edit mode is coming.

Before, the board was a single auto-fill of 240px minimums and every tile was the same size.

2

The slot reservations are gone — K3

Your words: "The type lock-up needs to be as tight as the original". K added a min-height to the label, value and delta rows so neighbouring tiles would line up even when one had no delta. It cost 18px between the value and the delta line — a 48.00px step where the original has 30.02px — and you saw it. K3 removes all three.

Measured in Chromium, both files against the same type.css, every default tile paired by name: label to value 22.00px original / 22.00px K3, value to delta 30.02px original / 30.02px K3. Nought apart. Tile-to-tile alignment is a board problem and comes back, if it comes back, on the board's own grid rows — not by padding every row of every tile.

3

The rotated direction arrow

Your words: "please use the arrow from the assets I think its called 'direction' there is only up down left and right, maybe we can rotate to get the 45 degree angle". Enacted in K2. The glyph is now assets/icons/global-controls/direction-up.svg, direction-down.svg and direction-right.svg, byte-matched, and the diagonal is a CSS transform on the icon: rotate(45deg) for a rise (up-right), rotate(-45deg) for a fall (down-right), 0° for flat. A scale(.78) keeps the rotated box inside its 16px seat. The HSBC trend mark of #261 K is dropped; nothing new was drawn.

3b

The original text layout, and no end dot

Your words: "I prefer the text layout in the original" and "can we remove the dot at the end of the spark line". K2 puts the comparison period back INSIDE the delta line — "▲ +12.4% up  vs February" — instead of on a fourth stacked row, and restores the Amount-display value recipe (no inter-part gap, no dimmed unit). The sparkline's end-point circle is deleted, not hidden. The slot alignment of note 2 survives: it is spacing, not typography.

4

A target row with no hue

Track is border/subtle, fill is standing ink, the pace tick is one pixel, and the caption spells out every figure the bar draws. Whether 48% against a 60% target is a problem is the reader's judgement, not the tile's — so no RAG rung is bound anywhere in the row.

5

Four states, drawn

Loading is a skeleton with aria-busy and a pulse that reduced-motion kills. Empty is an em-dash plus a sentence saying why — never a zero, because a zero is a measurement. Error names what its 44px control will load. Stale is a NEUTRAL chip, not an amber one.

6

One seat now — you closed it

Your words: "We need to use the dark versions of the colours on the arrows". Since #203 the tile carried two green/red seats: the arrow wore Stat-card's FILL green #66CC8D (1.980:1 on a white card) while the sparkline wore the INK green #137F3C that s182-D3 rules for this atom. They disagreed in seven of eight panes, and three themes put the light-mode arrow under the 3:1 floor.

K3 moves the arrow onto the ink seat. Light: #137F3C up, #DA1A00 down — the two-red law's red-on-white. Dark: #66CC8D up, #F6604C down. Driven and read off the DOM in all four themes and both modes: arrow and stroke are the same value in all eight, and the arrow is now theme-invariant like the stroke, because canon declares rag/*-ink once per mode with no theme override. Switch to Console light and the two greens are one.

Stat card has not moved. It still wears the fill seat, and the two must travel together — that is the owed follow-on, not something this lane could take on its own.

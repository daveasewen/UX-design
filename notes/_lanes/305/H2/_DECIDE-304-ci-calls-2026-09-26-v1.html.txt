 The nav count badge: 8px, or an exemption from the grid? 
 The badge is a pill, at least 24px wide. With 7px of padding a single digit already fills that width, so it does not move at 8px. Two digits grow by 2px, from 27.9px to 29.9px. Three digits grow from 34.9px to 36.9px. In the top nav the item grows by those 2px; in the sidebar the label gives them up. Photographed from the real snippets in the HSBC face, not redrawn:

 Recommend 8px , because the change is two pixels on two-digit counts and none on one, and an exemption would be the grid check's first spacing exception.
 The forks: declare them as meant, or make them one value? 
 In mono, a filled bar and a thin sparkline line take different tones for the same status. The bar takes the lighter graphic tone, the line takes the darker ink tone. Contrast against white, computed from the values the check itself resolved:

 Negative

 Sparkline line #1A1A1A · 17.4 to 1
 Unifying on the graphic tone would leave the thin line at 3.1 to 3.4 against white, just over the 3 to 1 floor for graphics. Unifying on the ink tone would darken every bar. Neutral is the odd one: blue on the bar means "information", default ink on the line means "no change". Declaring says that out loud. The fourth fork is the compact data grid using 4px cell padding against the plain table's 10px, which is what compact means.
 Recommend declare all four , because each difference does a job: fills take the lighter tone, thin lines need the darker ink to be seen, and compact rows are meant to be tighter.
 The package copies: port the chain script now, or at the cut? 
 The pack carries the Memento package, and its two copies of the chain script date from before 2 September. Porting is a copy of one file into two places. The v1.0.14 cut is planned for Tuesday either way. Porting now means the candidate is tested with the file it will ship, and CI stops carrying a red nobody reads.
 Recommend port now , because it is the same copy either way and doing it first lets the candidate be tested with it.
 Seven one-off slide checkers from the deck work: retire them, or keep them? 
 They checked deck v7's slides between 11 and 13 September. Run 1 guarded them so they no longer turn the build red. Nothing else in the repo calls them. Retiring means moving them to the retired folder, not deleting them.

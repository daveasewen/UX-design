Your review · the active twins

15 icons each have two or three “active” drawings. Which one is the twin?

Every icon in the library is meant to have one inactive drawing and one active twin. For these 15, the export brought back 31 drawings all named “… Active” — 14 icons with two, 1 with three. The file names below carry a -2 or -3 only because two files could not share a name; the order says which one the export met first, nothing more. Nobody has yet looked and said which drawing is the twin.

So this sheet makes no suggestion. Each row shows the base and its candidates at the two sizes they are used at, on a light chrome and a dark one. You decide per row: name the twin, say none of them is, or flag a drawing that is its own icon and needs an inactive version drawn. Answers stay in this browser as you go; Export at the end writes a file to hand back.

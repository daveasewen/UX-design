## 2. Why the legend moves (the judgement EV asked for)
The stage is 879 × 744 at 1280. The base is portrait; every family column is a wide band. No single legend position serves both: a bottom legend halves the portrait base, a side column halves the wide band. So the legend follows the graph's shape — column for portrait, strip for landscape — and the fold gives the whole stage back in one click either way. The move happens only on a family chip toggle, at the same instant the graph re-fits; the chips keep their order and labels. Translucency alone was rejected (above). A phone has neither axis to spare, hence the sheet over the aside.

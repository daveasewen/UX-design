The problem

The parts bin

An assembly line is only as fast as its parts bin. Everything we own is in ours — and nobody knows all of it end to end.

So a build is slow, no two builds come out the same, and quality is whoever reached into the bin that day. Speed, consistency, quality — one picture.

03 / 13

The breakdown · light root cause

Three problems.
Mostly the same causes.

Speed

The governance process.

A two-stage process — Figma, then the code library — everything built twice.

Consistency

Nothing of its own.

Everything under it is shared with the other two.

Quality

Lack of data and research.

AI generation quality — slop.

Under all three

A small component library.

Lack of design standards and accessibility knowledge.

Low and variable skills.

Consistency is not a fourth problem to solve. It is what the other two leave behind — which is why the three in the grey band are the ones worth attacking.

Source: our own problem breakdown, 19 September 2026.

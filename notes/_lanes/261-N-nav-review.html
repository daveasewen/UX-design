Nav family — #261 N2

Dave's ask, verbatim: "tackle the nav design". No reference was supplied ("we'll prompt this"), so everything here is designed by judgment and every choice is proposed, not ruled.

The three nav surfaces are now one family, and the family claim is mechanical: a shared item block (.nv-item / .nv-ic / .nv-label / .nv-count / .nv-chev) that is byte-identical in all three snippets, and a shared token block that is byte-identical in two of them and extended by exactly two search-field vars in the third. If the surfaces drift, the blocks stop matching and it shows up in a diff.

Read the theme switcher honestly: the projected cascade injected into these frames is built from the NEW --nav-* var names, so in Supercharge the "after" frames re-project to that theme's surfaces and the "before" frames stay on the mono values. That is a property of this review harness, not a defect in the old files — they simply did not carry these var names.

The indicator rule: the 3px current-location bar sits on the edge the nav touches the content. Sidebar inline-start, top nav block-end, tab bar block-start. That is what makes the three look related without any of them being a copy.

Two things that need your word

1 · Tab bar means the bottom bar, not section tabs

The lane brief described Tab bar as "in-page section tabs, scrollable overflow with fade, underline indicator, counts". That is what the gated Tabs component already is. Tab bar's own meta has said "app-level bottom navigation, NOT the in-page Tabs component" since it was surfaced. Redefining it would have made two components mean the same thing and left the mobile bottom bar homeless, so the canon meaning was kept — and the section-tab reading is drawn above, on these same nav tokens, inside the app frame.

Recommendation: KEEP. Tab bar stays the mobile bottom bar; the section-tab strip above becomes a proposal against Tabs in a later pass. The alternative is RENAME, which costs a component and a set of edges.

2 · Supercharge dark has no legal current-location colour

Driven measurement, 24 probes across four themes and two modes: the brand red indicator on the current-row surface reads 4.58 light / 3.01 dark in mono, legacy and console, and 3.89 / 2.73 in Supercharge. The pattern survives because colour is the third carrier and the 3px bar is a shape — but a red that fails in one theme is still a red that fails.

Recommendation: SHAPE. Leave the bar where it is and stop treating its colour as a carrier at all — the shape and aria-current already do the work in every theme. The alternative is TOKEN: give Supercharge its own current-location value, which is a new token and a new four-theme measurement.

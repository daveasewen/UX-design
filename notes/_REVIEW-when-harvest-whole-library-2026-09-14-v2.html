Three lanes covered 132 components in three slices — A (input · action · feedback · overlay, 45 proposals over 50 components), B (record-list · chart-panel · headline-metric · status-surface, 45), C (page-frame · page-title · wayfinding · arrangement, 42 over 42 metas — the brief’s thirteenth template does not exist on disk). Is a slider legal in a form at all?

slider · range-slider

slice A

 slider-vs-input-fields — GOV.UK HAS NO SLIDER COMPONENT AT ALL

GOV.UK rejects the interaction outright on accessibility grounds (WCAG 2.5.1 pointer gestures) and ships no slider. Six systems ship one with conditions. M3 adds a condition none of the others do: the change must take effect immediately — which would make a slider inside a submitted form illegal.
GOV.UKEXT“Avoid using range slider questions, where the user needs to click and drag”
Fluent 2EXT“Use a slider for imprecise values.”
AtlassianEXT“Don't use a range if choosing a specific value is important.”
IBM CarbonEXT“Do not use for extremely large ranges, for example, 1-1000.”
Material 3EXT“Changes made with sliders must take effect immediately”
Adobe SpectrumEXT“used when the upper and lower bounds to the range are invariable.”
NN/gEXT“Sliders or knobs are continuous controls.”
Apple HIGEXT“Consider supplementing a slider with a corresponding text field and stepper.”
USWDSEXT“no slider component”
OURSslider.meta antiPatterns[2] (REVIEW, inferred): 'using a slider where precise numeric entry is required (pair with an input)'
  1. Slider is legal in filters and settings only, never inside a submitted form (M3's immediacy test, and it sidesteps most of GOV.UK's objection since filters re-apply live).
  2. Slider is legal anywhere, always paired with a linked numeric field (NN/g and Apple HIG both require the pairing).
  3. Slider is removed from the input role and kept only as a display/filter control.
  4. Slider stands as built; record the GOV.UK divergence.

lane’s, not ruledLANE'S RECOMMENDATION: option 1 plus the pairing from option 2 wherever a value is committed. The immediacy test is the same test that separates Switch from Checkbox in s270 — one predicate, two families, which is worth more than a slider-specific rule.

Four systems fence links to navigation only. Spectrum crosses the line the other way (buttons may navigate). OUR OWN META crosses it in the direction four systems forbid, and 'download pdf' is a real, frequent HSBC case — every document row in the library depends on the answer.
IBM CarbonEXT“Do not use links for actions that will change data”
AtlassianEXT“Links navigate to a new page or anchor location, changing the URL.”
Fluent 2EXT“Don't use links for triggering actions other than navigation.”
Shopify PolarisEXT“Links are used primarily for navigation”
Adobe SpectrumEXT“Buttons allow users to perform an action or to navigate to another page.”

## ASK 1 — the on-chart bar key RISES pinned to the bar top (not a fade)
Dave: *"the alpha [letter key] should follow the animation of the bars i.e. rising vertically, pinned to the
top of the bar — this is how it was originally."* Today the key uses `dvKeyIn` (opacity fade). Replace with a
`translateY` RISE synced to the bar's `scaleY` grow so the letter travels up on top of its bar.

Recipe (this was built then REVERTED at the Red wrap to avoid a half-done state — reapply it):
```css
.dv-animate text.dv-barkey{opacity:0; transform-box:fill-box; animation:dvKeyRise var(--grow) both;}
@keyframes dvKeyRise{from{transform:translateY(var(--kh,0px)); opacity:0;} 25%{opacity:1;} to{transform:translateY(0); opacity:1;}}
```
Each key gets inline `style="animation-delay:<D>ms; --kh:<barHeight>px"`. `--kh` = that bar's height, so the key
starts at the baseline (bar collapsed) and rises to its final y as the bar grows. **Same duration (`--grow`=760ms),
same easing (`cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)`), same per-key delay as the bar ⇒ the letter stays pinned ~4px above the
rising bar top the whole way up.** The 12 keys (document order — delays already present, add `--kh`):

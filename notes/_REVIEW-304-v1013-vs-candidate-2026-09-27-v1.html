The answer

Composing stopped the tracing. It did not make the pages better. The candidate's pages look far less like the template: the template-likeness index halved, from 0.379 to 0.178. They also use more real canon parts: 14.7 against 6.3 non-chart components per run. Scored like for like across all ten destinations, the two groups tie at 10.0 out of 12, and both score 1 of 3 on geometry at 1440.

The candidate has three runs where v1.0.13 has three, so the tie means no measured gain. By eye, the candidate pages are more varied, and two of the three are organised under your three questions. They are not cleaner. Composing from real parts brought canon's own defects to the surface, and every candidate carries them. The KPI labels lose their descenders, the side navigation sits in a 640px box with a blank band below it, Common gets Mono's 40px gutter, and the receipt gate fails every chart.

The harness as it ran scored the candidate lower: 8.0 against v1.0.13's 10.0. That gap comes from the harness, not the pages. It rendered only the candidates' first view, it read text before the page's scripts had written it, and it clicked "Light" to test the theme switch on every run. The like-for-like columns below correct all three, and each correction is shown.

Your decision

  1. Is the candidate better enough to cut v1.0.14 on Tuesday?

    My recommendation: not as it stands. Cut on Tuesday only if these four canon fixes land first:

    • the Kpi-tile label crop;
    • the Common gutter key;
    • a full-height side-nav shell;
    • one ruled address for a chart region's receipt.

    Then one confirming cold run needs to beat v1.0.13's 10.0. Composing is the right direction. On this evidence, it puts every composed page onto four defects that the traced pages happened to step around.

Defects the runs surfaced, verified here

Four in canon, one in the tooling, two new

  • (a) Common gets Mono's 40px bento gutter. Confirmed. Fix in canon.

    The generated block AUTO-BENTO-ROLE-VARS in canon.css sets --bento-dashboard-main for mono, legacy, console and supercharge, but not for common. The same holds at repo lines 22018–22033 and in both packs. A Common page therefore falls back to :root's 40px.

    Measured wall gutters: v1013-r1 24px, v1013-r2 40px, v1013-r3 40px, cand-r1 40px, cand-r2 40px, cand-r3 40px. v1013-r1 set 24px itself.

    Fix: canon/gen_bento_role_vars.py should emit common beside legacy.

  • (b) The receipt mint and the receipt gate name different chart scripts. Confirmed. Latent in v1.0.13 too.

    The mint takes the address from the snippet's AUTO-BEHAVIOUR marker, which is dv-behaviour.js, looked up through component-types.json. Every chart meta says dv-render.js, and the gate trusts the meta.

    I composed one Chart-line with each pack's own --compose. Both packs give FAIL:BEHAVIOUR-ADDRESS-DISAGREES, and the repo's mint is byte-identical to the candidate's. The candidate is not the cause. It exposes the defect because it splices and receipts the chart figures, which v1.0.13 runs mostly did not.

    Fix: tooling (mint and gate), after a one-line ruling from you on which address a chart region carries.

  • (c) App-shell-side-nav is a fixed 640px frame. Confirmed. Fix in the component.

    Source: :where(.cn-app-shell-side-nav) .sh{…height:640px} at canon.css:5678. Doormat and multi-column carry the same rule, at 4697 and 5148.

    The frame measures v1013-r1 1000px, v1013-r2 640px, v1013-r3 none, cand-r1 640px, cand-r2 640px, cand-r3 640px. All three candidates kept it because their skill forbids resizing a part.

    Fix: a full-viewport form of the shell, and the skill wording to match.

  • (d) Inherited contrast in Common. Confirmed on all six. Fix in canon.

    KPI label and period: 3.71:1. That is --alpha-60 over Common's #333 ink, from .kpi-tile .lbl16 at 17932 and .kpi-lbl / .kpi-per at 13070 and 13098.

    Side-nav group label: 3.75:1. That is opacity:.72 on --muted, at 5787.

    Both are 14px/400 text, which needs 4.5:1. In dark the KPI label reaches 6.72:1, but the group label stays at 3.75:1.

  • (f) New: Kpi-tile clips its own label's descenders. Fix in the component.

    .kpi-lbl combines overflow:hidden with text-box-edge:text text and a 14px line box: the ds-005 trap, the label-crop class from #261.

    4b's geometry gate on the pack's own Kpi-tile.reference.html finds 12 labels cut at 1440. All three candidates inherit it: 2px cut, and visible on "Available liquidity" above. The v1.0.13 runs avoid it only because they traced the template's own KPI markup.

  • (g) New: the donut's centre total prints the raw floating-point sum. Fix in the chart engine.

    Examples: "791.9000000000001" on v1013-r1 and "2990.8999999999996" on cand-r3. The source is dv-render-donut.js:135 (String(total)), and the legend rewrites it the same way.

  • Seen by eye; cause not proven.

    Y-axis tick labels are cut at the left ("00 £m") on five of six runs; v1013-r2 fitted them. The thirty daily x-labels collide on four of six. Donut and pie charts stretch to the height of their tile, leaving large empty bands (cand-r2 twice, v1013-r2 once).

    Chart-engine defaults are the likely home for all of these. None of it separates the two groups.

Method, in brief

What was scored. The harness scored each run's entry page, and 4b's geometry and own-size gates ran on it at 1440 and 390. For v1.0.13 the entry page is the overview file plus the nine linked files, probed light. For the candidates, the harness rendered only the first view.

To make the surfaces comparable, R4s drove every destination of every run in the same way at 1440 × 1000: the candidates' ten views by URL, and v1.0.13's ten files. The like-for-like columns re-apply the harness's own rules to that data. Geometry and own-size are overview-only for all six, which is comparable.

The corrections.

  • Visually rich: counted over all ten destinations. "Clipped" still counts a chart cut by the as-shipped shell frame, as the harness does.
  • Full: the KPI and question words are read from rendered text, and the record rows from every destination.
  • Theme: each run's own Dark button was clicked, not "Light".

Persistent and interactive are the harness's own drive, unchanged.

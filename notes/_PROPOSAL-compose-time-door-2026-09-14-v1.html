01Why a third door

The spine has two doors and they answer different questions. _consult.py answers "what governs X?" over rules, rulings, assertions and gates. _memento_search.py answers "what did we decide about X?" over the session archive. Neither answers "what do I compose with?".

So the generate skill's step 1 — "Query canon for the components each screen needs (narrow context — just those nodes + their tokens)" — has no query surface behind it. In practice step 1 is read the metas: 138 files, 395,000 cl100k tokens if read whole, and the choice of which to open made before anything has been retrieved. The knowledge is superbly stored and the narrowing is left to judgement — the same shape that made the consult door necessary.

The third door closes that. Same posture as its siblings: refs first and detail behind them, a hand-authored lexicon that grows on miss, advisory at birth.

04What it cannot resolve today — and what lane 1 unlocks

A closed slice has to say what it could not close. Every one of these arrives as ref:null plus a note; none is filled in with a guess.

  • Roles are only half in the graph. provides is authored on 24 of 138 metas, so role → component resolution leans on roles.json's provider lists, which that file's own description calls a membership cross-check, not a source. Lane 1 of #270 is exactly this — when role nodes land, one function in the tool re-points and the soft half goes hard.
  • Which co-provider wins is not mechanical. when predicates are authored on 26 metas. The slice carries every candidate's when and names the runners-up it held back; the choice is still a judgement, and it says so.
  • The filter bar belongs to no role. filter-toolbar-bar — the exact component the demo prompt needs — is in no roles.json provider list and declares no provides. It reaches the slice by name, not by role. A real gap, found by driving.
  • Rules have no component tag. _rules-index.json carries 470 rules with stable ids and destinies and no link to a component. Attachment is therefore exact where a meta cites a rule id (38 metas do) and ROUTED by vocabulary otherwise — routed rules are labelled confidence:"routed" and never passed off as authored.
  • Most anti-neighbours are prose. 51 of 70 mustNotNeighbour edges carry ref:null: the prohibition is real, the other end is not a node, so no gate can check it. Surfaced, never dropped.
  • A chart with no intent word is refused, not guessed. "build me a financial dashboard" names no analytical intent, so the door returns no chart and says why — the five-word vocabulary in chart-intents.json is the gate on that role.
  • Two icon paths in the input-fields snippet (error.svg, success.svg) are not in icons.manifest.json. Reported as unresolved; not invented.

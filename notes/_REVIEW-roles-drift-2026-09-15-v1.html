Why (a): the membership is already ruled (s252-D1, “with the membership”), so this ENACTS, it does not rule. A resolver that can name substitutes without ordering them beats one that cannot see 78% of them.

when + priority + shape + answers — the DESK address, AUTHORED not copied: roles.json's when strings are prose like “default, read-mostly” and do not parse under the closed field grammar. This decision sets the SCOPE of authoring pass two.

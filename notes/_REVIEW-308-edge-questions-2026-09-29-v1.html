02 · Carousel and cards

Does a carousel hold cards, or does a card hold a carousel?

Each meta lists where its component lives, and the two lists point at each other. carousel.meta.json says it lives in "Cards (a media card sequence)". cards.meta.json says it lives in "Carousel". Nothing else in the record settles it. The Carousel snippet draws captioned media slides, not cards. The Cards snippet never mentions a carousel. The Carousel meta is still marked "PROPOSED … not registered, not ruled".

A Recommended

A carousel holds cards

What it keepsCards keep "Carousel" in the places they live.
What goesCarousel's "Cards (a media card sequence)" line. Read plainly, it describes what a carousel holds, not where it lives.
B The other reading

A card holds a carousel

What it keepsCarousel keeps "Cards (a media card sequence)".
What goesCards lose "Carousel" from the places they live.

Choose A. Both metas then say the same thing, and the loop check goes quiet.

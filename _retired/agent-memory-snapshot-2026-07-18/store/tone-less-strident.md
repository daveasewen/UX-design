Dave finds the current draft tone a little **strident / accusatory toward stakeholders**. Example he flagged (2026-06-29): *"Stop selling the drawing of screens. Start owning the experience."* — great editorially, but it almost accuses stakeholders of doing something wrong.

**Why:** the audience is leadership/stakeholders; they shouldn't feel blamed or corrected. A blaming tone undercuts a persuasive case.

**How to apply:** prefer **opportunity- and evidence-framed, inclusive "we"** language; describe the shift in the world (what AI/industry is doing) rather than instructing the audience to stop/start; reserve punchy imperatives. Keep the editorial sharpness but aim it at the situation, not the people.

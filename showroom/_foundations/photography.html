Photography

A Foundations tier entry, not a component. The 15 photographs below are the committed web derivatives in knowledge/assets/photography-web/ — the whole set is 251 originals, which stay NON-REPO behind the #211 .gitignore fence; a derivative is minted on demand for the photograph a page actually uses (ruling s217-D1). Derivative spec: max edge 1600 px, target 300 KB, sRGB, progressive JPEG. Every caption is the photograph's own EXIF description and its derived licence source, copied verbatim from knowledge/_PHOTOGRAPHY-MANIFEST.json — nothing is inferred and nothing is typed here. UNKNOWN means the field is absent on the file.

Tile spans are derived from each image's own aspect ratio, not arranged by eye: wider than 6:1 spans three columns, wider than 2.6:1 spans two, taller than 1:1.15 spans two rows, everything else is a single cell. The thresholds are canon (ruling s217-D2), read from knowledge/tokens/layout.json. Open any tile for the full description, both bases, and the measured sizes.

All twelve derivatives share one aspect ratio, so the aspect rule alone would draw a plain grid. Emphasis is taken from position instead — every 6th tile in filename order, from tile 1, is promoted to a 2×2 block (s217-D2). Position, not preference.

The bento — 15 derivatives

What this page is, and is not

  • Nothing is re-drawn. Every photograph is an <img> at its committed derivative. The layout vocabulary and the zero-JavaScript lightbox are copied from knowledge/_fitness-test/bento-gallery-showcase-v2.html (#216) — which is itself awaiting Dave's sign-off. Copying a layout he pointed at is not a certification of it.
  • No preference ordering. The 15 photographs carrying derivatives are specimen picks for the Image-block and Carousel pages; picking a specimen is not a ruling and Dave swaps by eye. Tile order is the derivative filename, ascending.
  • No tags, keywords or categories. The tagging approach is unruled (#211 owed item 3) and no meaning is inferred from the description text.
  • Page-local styles only. Dave parked structural bento components at #216, so this page mints no canon component and edits no canon rule. Every rule it ships is in its own head, scoped to .fx.
  • The lightbox ships no JavaScript. It is the platform's popover driven by command / commandfor on the button. The only script on the page is the theme/mode chrome.

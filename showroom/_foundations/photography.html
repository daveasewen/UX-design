What this page is, and is not

  • Nothing is re-drawn. Every photograph is an <img> at its committed derivative. The layout vocabulary and the zero-JavaScript lightbox are copied from knowledge/_fitness-test/bento-gallery-showcase-v2.html (#216) — which is itself awaiting Dave's sign-off. Copying a layout he pointed at is not a certification of it.
  • Every picture is lazy. All 251 wall images and all 251 lightbox images carry loading="lazy" decoding="async". Eagerly, the page would ask for roughly 84.2 MB before a single tile was on screen.
  • 6 photograph(s) carry no EXIF description, and their captions render the licence line only. The field is absent on the file; a plausible sentence is never invented to fill it, and the lightbox says UNKNOWN with the basis that proves it.
  • No preference ordering. Tile order is the derivative filename, ascending — mechanical, not curated. Emphasis is position, not preference.
  • No tags, keywords or categories. The tagging approach is unruled (#211 owed item 3) and no meaning is inferred from the description text.
  • Page-local styles only. This page mints no canon component and edits no canon rule. Every rule it ships is in its own head, scoped to .fx.
  • The lightbox ships no JavaScript. It is the platform's popover driven by command / commandfor on the button. The only script on the page is the theme/mode chrome.

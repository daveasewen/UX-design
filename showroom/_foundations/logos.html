Logos

A Foundations tier entry, not a component. Three lockups, each in Colour and Monotone, each in an On light and an On dark treatment — the on-dark artwork is the reverse cut, drawn for dark surfaces. 12 files in all, in knowledge/assets/logos/. Every fill in every file is hardcoded, so no logo follows a theme: each tile is pinned to the ground its artwork was drawn for, in all four themes and in both modes. The page chrome follows the theme; the tiles deliberately do not.

Tile spans are derived from each image's own aspect ratio, not arranged by eye: wider than 6:1 spans three columns, wider than 2.6:1 spans two, taller than 1:1.15 spans two rows, everything else is a single cell. The thresholds are canon (ruling s217-D2), read from knowledge/tokens/layout.json. Every viewBox, byte count and fill inventory below was read off the SVG files at build time. Nothing is typed.

The bento — 12 variants
⚠ Measured, not fixed — the two ON-DARK identifier exports carry no identifier label.

Both dark files are the plain masterbrand artwork on a 795-unit viewBox: the label paths are absent, so 480 units (60% of the box) render empty. The two tiles above show the files as they are; the gap is the export's, not the page's.
